# Finance Information Handling

## 1. Purpose
The primary objective of this internal guideline is to establish clear protocols for managing sensitive data and maintaining professional integrity within the financial sector of the Company. Employees must uphold the highest standards of transparency when dealing with external entities and secure credentials. By setting strict boundaries on handling gifts and system access, the organization protects its financial reputation and ensures that all commercial decisions remain entirely objective, free from any potential conflicts of interest or security breaches.

## 2. Applies to
This policy is restricted in its scope and applies only to the Finance department of the Company. Every staff member, analyst, accountant, and manager operating within this specific division must adhere strictly to these directives during their daily duties. Personnel outside this unit are governed by separate guidelines, meaning this document targets exclusively the financial operations, accounting workflows, and related internal processes managed by the designated team.

## 3. Department rule
Team members within the division must adhere to two mandatory behavioral and technical rules. First, any presents offered by business partners, vendors, or customers must be formally reported to the immediate supervisor. If such items possess the potential to sway commercial judgment or create a sense of obligation, staff are strictly prohibited from keeping them. Second, maintaining digital security is paramount; all staff must update their system passwords every 90 days. Furthermore, credentials must remain confidential and must never be disclosed to colleagues, external parties, or anyone else under any circumstances.

## 4. Rationale
The reasoning behind these security and ethical measures is to safeguard the financial assets and operational integrity of the Company. Unreported tokens of appreciation from suppliers can subtly bias financial forecasting, procurement choices, or contract awards, thereby compromising organizational fairness. Similarly, regular credential rotation and strict secrecy regarding login details prevent unauthorized system entry, data leaks, and potential fraud. Enforcing these safeguards ensures that internal financial records remain accurate, trustworthy, and protected against internal and external threats.

## 5. Escalation
Whenever uncertainty arises regarding the appropriateness of a vendor present, or if a security credential is inadvertently compromised, staff must immediately notify their direct manager for guidance. Attempting to conceal a doubtful offering or ignoring scheduled credential updates constitutes a serious breach of internal regulations. The management team will review each reported incident, determine the correct course of action, and take necessary disciplinary or corrective measures to ensure full compliance across the entire financial division of the Company.
