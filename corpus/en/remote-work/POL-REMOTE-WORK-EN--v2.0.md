# Remote Work Policy

## 1. Purpose

The purpose of this document is to establish clear guidelines for flexible working arrangements within the Company. As modern work environments evolve, the Company recognizes the value of offering flexibility to its workforce while maintaining high standards of productivity, collaboration, and operational continuity. This policy outlines how employees can balance their professional responsibilities between traditional office spaces and alternative locations, ensuring that all teams remain aligned with organizational objectives. By setting transparent standards, the Company aims to support staff well-being, enhance job satisfaction, and retain top talent across various disciplines, all while ensuring that team communication and project delivery are never compromised. Managers and team members must understand these guidelines thoroughly to foster a trust-based culture that respects both individual flexibility and collective goals.

## 2. Scope

This policy applies specifically to staff members operating within designated departments of the Company. Remote work arrangements are exclusively available to employees working in Engineering, IT, Marketing, Data and Analytics, Product, User Experience, Mobile, Infrastructure, and Information Security. Personnel belonging to any other departments are required to perform their duties on-site at the Company premises during all standard working hours. Department heads and supervisors in the eligible areas must evaluate operational readiness and ensure that client needs and project milestones are fully met before approving any telecommuting schedules. Staff members outside the approved departments cannot participate in this arrangement, maintaining standard on-site attendance as part of their daily routines.

## 3. Policy

Eligible employees may work remotely up to 2 days per week with their direct manager's approval. To ensure seamless coordination and fairness across teams, remote days must be requested in the portal at least 2 working days in advance. Requests submitted after this notification window may be subject to review or denial depending on operational demands. Once an alternative work location day is approved and underway, employees must adhere strictly to communication and availability standards. Specifically, on remote days employees must be online and reachable during core hours from 10:00 to 15:00. During this designated time frame, staff are expected to respond promptly to messages, attend scheduled virtual meetings, and maintain active status on corporate communication channels. Regular working hours outside this core window should be coordinated with the respective supervisor to fulfill daily contractual obligations.

## 4. Responsibilities

Both employees and management share the responsibility of upholding the standards set forth by the Company. Direct managers are tasked with reviewing portal requests fairly, ensuring adequate daily coverage within their teams, and supporting their staff while upholding productivity standards. Employees are responsible for submitting their requests within the required notification period, maintaining a secure and productive home office environment, and fulfilling all professional duties diligently. Furthermore, proper time tracking is essential for compliance and payroll accuracy. Attendance is recorded by the office badge reader or, on approved remote days, by checking in through the HR mobile app. Employees working off-site must remember to use the digital application to log their attendance accurately, whereas those working on-site will rely on the physical badge scanning system at the building entrance.

## 5. Exceptions

While this policy provides a structured framework for flexible arrangements, operational needs at the Company may occasionally require temporary adjustments. Edge cases such as critical project deadlines, urgent system maintenance, or mandatory team workshops may necessitate the suspension of telecommuting privileges for specific individuals or entire departments. In such scenarios, supervisors hold the authority to require staff presence on-site, overriding previously approved remote schedules. Any requests exceeding the standard weekly limit of remote days, or requests originating from staff in non-eligible departments, cannot be accommodated through the standard portal workflow and require specialized executive authorization. Employees facing exceptional personal circumstances should consult with human resources to explore available accommodations that comply with overall company guidelines.

## 6. Related documents

To ensure a comprehensive understanding of employment standards, this policy should be read in conjunction with other official guidelines issued by the Company. Relevant materials include internal communication protocols, employee handbooks, information security manuals, and timekeeping procedures available on the corporate intranet. Compliance with these related documents is mandatory for all personnel utilizing flexible work arrangements. Failure to adhere to the guidelines outlined in this policy or its complementary documentation may result in the revocation of remote work privileges and standard performance reviews. Management and human resources regularly review these resources to reflect current operational practices and organizational goals.
