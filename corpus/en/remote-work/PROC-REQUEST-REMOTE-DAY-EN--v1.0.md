# How to Request a Remote Work Day

## 1. Purpose
This internal procedure outlines the operational framework established by the Company to facilitate working away from the physical office location. The Company recognizes the importance of workplace flexibility and strives to balance operational efficiency with personal convenience for its staff. This document provides clear guidelines so that personnel understand how to properly submit, manage, and execute their flexible work arrangements while maintaining continuous communication and accountability. By following these established guidelines, team members ensure that their operational impact remains seamless, team collaboration continues uninterrupted, and productivity standards are fully met regardless of their physical workstation on any given day. Management relies on these standardized procedures to maintain proper oversight and ensure that business continuity and team coordination are never compromised by distributed work schedules.

## 2. Who this applies to
The provisions outlined in this procedural guide apply uniformly to all eligible employees of the Company who wish to utilize off-site work arrangements. Eligibility is determined based on role suitability, departmental requirements, and performance standing within the organization. While many personnel can take advantage of these flexible provisions, it remains the responsibility of each staff member to review their specific job duties and consult with their supervisors to confirm their eligibility status. The Company ensures that these policies are administered fairly across all qualifying departments, supporting a modern and adaptable corporate culture. Individuals who meet the necessary criteria are permitted to work remotely up to 2 days per week, provided they have secured the prior authorization and approval of their direct manager. This limitation ensures that teams still maintain adequate in-person presence for collaborative projects, urgent meetings, and essential physical operations.

## 3. Steps
Executing a flexible work request successfully requires adherence to specific operational actions within the organization's digital infrastructure. Staff members must initiate their request through the designated internal digital portal well ahead of their intended off-site date to allow for proper administrative review. Once submitted, the request routes automatically to the appropriate supervisor for evaluation. Following managerial approval, personnel must prepare for their off-site schedule by ensuring all necessary digital tools and secure connections are fully functional. On the designated day of off-site work, attendance tracking shifts from traditional physical methods to a digital process. Instead of utilizing the standard office badge reader, personnel must complete their attendance recording by checking in directly through the official HR mobile app. This digital check-in serves as the official record of presence, ensuring accurate administrative tracking while working away from the primary office premises.

## 4. Timelines
Timing is a critical component of the approval process to ensure that team leads and department heads can manage departmental staffing levels effectively. Consequently, remote days must be requested in the portal at least 2 working days in advance of the planned absence from the office. Submissions made past this deadline risk automatic rejection by the system or denial by management due to insufficient notice for operational planning. Furthermore, once the off-site arrangement is active, timing and availability remain strictly regulated. On approved remote days, employees must be online and reachable during core hours from 10:00 to 15:00. This mandatory availability window guarantees that colleagues, clients, and management can connect with the staff member for urgent discussions, collaborative meetings, and time-sensitive project developments during the standard working day.

## 5. Contacts
Navigating technical issues or administrative hurdles regarding off-site arrangements requires knowing the correct internal channels within the organization. If staff members encounter technical difficulties with the digital portal during their submission process, or if they experience challenges with the digital check-in functionality within the HR mobile app, they should immediately reach out to the appropriate internal support teams. For portal access and software malfunctions, the internal IT help desk serves as the primary point of contact for resolution. For questions regarding policy interpretation, approval status, or eligibility clarification regarding working remotely up to 2 days per week, personnel must direct their inquiries to their local human resources representative or their direct manager. Maintaining open communication channels ensures that any discrepancies regarding schedules, core hours from 10:00 to 15:00, or submission deadlines are resolved swiftly without disrupting daily workflow or compliance standards across the Company.
