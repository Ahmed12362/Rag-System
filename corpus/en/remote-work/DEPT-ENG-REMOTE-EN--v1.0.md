# Engineering Remote Work Practices

## 1. Purpose
The purpose of this internal guideline is to establish clear procedures for out-of-office arrangements within the Company, specifically designed to support flexibility while maintaining high productivity and seamless collaboration across technical projects.

## 2. Applies to
This policy applies exclusively to the Engineering department (الهندسة). No other departments within the Company are covered by these specific engineering guidelines, ensuring that technical teams can manage their off-site schedules effectively according to their unique operational needs.

## 3. Department rule
Staff members within the technical teams may work from home or other off-site locations for a maximum of 2 days per week, provided they obtain prior authorization from their direct manager. When utilizing these approved flexible days, personnel are strictly required to remain online and fully accessible throughout the core hours spanning from 10:00 to 15:00. This ensures that team syncs, urgent troubleshooting, and cross-functional meetings can proceed without interruption during the standard operational window of the Company.

## 4. Rationale
Modern technology teams thrive when given a balance of focused solo development time and collaborative office presence. By allowing technical professionals to operate off-site for a limited portion of the work cycle, the Company aims to boost job satisfaction and reduce daily commute friction while preserving the spontaneous communication essential for complex software delivery. Setting mandatory availability windows guarantees that project momentum never slows down, allowing teammates to rely on immediate responses during critical collaboration periods.

## 5. Escalation
Should any disagreements arise regarding schedule approvals, coverage during core hours, or compliance with availability expectations, staff should first consult their direct supervisor within the technical organization. If a resolution cannot be reached at the immediate management level, the matter may be forwarded to the departmental leadership of the Company for further review and final determination, ensuring all remote arrangements remain fair, transparent, and aligned with organizational objectives.
