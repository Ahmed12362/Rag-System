# Agency Staff and Remote Work

## 1. Purpose
This formal policy document serves to clarify the operational guidelines and daily working arrangements for external personnel performing duties on behalf of the Company. The primary objective is to establish transparent expectations regarding attendance, working hours, and leave entitlements for contracted individuals who are placed within the organization through third-party agencies. By setting clear operational standards, the organization aims to ensure seamless integration between external personnel and internal teams while maintaining distinct administrative boundaries. This document outlines how attendance tracking and time-off provisions are managed for this specific workforce segment, ensuring complete alignment with contractual agreements held between the workers and their respective staffing agencies.

## 2. Scope
The provisions detailed in this policy apply exclusively to agency staff working at Company premises who are supplied by external manpower vendors. It is important to note that this document does not change the rules for regular Company employees, who remain governed by the standard internal human resources guidelines and internal organizational policies. Regular staff members operate under distinct attendance frameworks, compensation models, and corporate leave structures that are entirely separate from the terms governing outsourced personnel. Therefore, managers and supervisors must carefully distinguish between internal employees and external agency contractors when addressing operational inquiries, timekeeping concerns, and administrative requests.

## 3. Policy
Regarding daily presence and timekeeping, agency staff working at Company premises follow the vendor's attendance rule with a 5-minute grace period. This means that external workers must adhere to the specific time and attendance protocols mandated by their employing manpower agency, while being granted a brief operational buffer upon arrival to account for minor logistical delays entering the workspace. Regarding time off and periodic absence, agency staff supplied by external manpower vendors receive 14 days of leave per year under their agency contract, not under Company policy. Consequently, any requests for planned time off, vacation days, or personal absence must be coordinated directly through the supplying vendor and calculated against the annual allocation provided in the individual worker's external contract.

## 4. Responsibilities
Management personnel, department heads, and supervising staff who oversee external contractors carry specific operational duties to ensure compliance with this policy. Supervisors must monitor daily attendance in accordance with the vendor's guidelines while accommodating the allowed 5-minute grace period without initiating disciplinary measures for minor tardiness within that window. Furthermore, supervisors must direct any leave inquiries or time-off requests submitted by agency workers back to the respective external manpower agency, as the organization itself does not manage or approve time off for these individuals. External manpower vendors are responsible for administering the 14 days of leave per year and ensuring that their assigned workers are fully informed of their contractual obligations and attendance expectations while stationed at the corporate location.

## 5. Exceptions
Given the strict adherence required to vendor-managed contracts, there are no discretionary deviations permitted regarding the core entitlements outlined in this policy. Department supervisors lack the authority to alter the annual time-off allocation or modify the attendance framework for individual contractors. Any perceived discrepancies concerning attendance rules or leave balances must be escalated directly to the external manpower vendor for resolution. In rare operational scenarios where unusual circumstances arise on-site, leadership must consult with the vendor management team rather than attempting to apply internal organizational exceptions to external staff.

## 6. Related documents
To gain a comprehensive understanding of workforce management within the organization, staff members and supervisors are encouraged to review complementary organizational references. These include vendor service level agreements, master service contracts established with external manpower suppliers, and internal operational guidelines that govern facility access and on-site conduct. Reviewing these supplementary materials ensures a holistic approach to managing mixed teams of internal personnel and external contractors effectively and harmoniously across all departments.
