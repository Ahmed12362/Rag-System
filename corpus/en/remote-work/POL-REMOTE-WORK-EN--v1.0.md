# Remote Work Policy

## 1. Purpose
The purpose of this document is to establish clear guidelines for remote work arrangements at the Company. As modern work environments evolve, the Company recognizes the value of flexibility in balancing professional duties and personal schedules. This policy outlines how eligible personnel can request and utilize work-from-home options while ensuring business continuity, seamless collaboration, and high performance across all operations. The framework is designed to provide predictability for teams, maintain strong productivity levels, and support employee well-being without disrupting daily office operations or team cohesion. By setting these standards, the Company aims to ensure fairness and transparency in how remote schedules are managed.

## 2. Scope
This policy applies to specific departments within the organization. Remote work opportunities are available exclusively for personnel operating within Engineering, IT, Marketing, Data and Analytics, Product, User Experience, Mobile, Infrastructure, and Information Security. Staff members belonging to any other departments not listed here are required to work on-site at Company offices for all their scheduled shifts. Department heads and supervisors in the eligible areas must ensure that operational coverage remains robust. Any ambiguity regarding department eligibility should be clarified with the human resources department before submitting any arrangement requests.

## 3. Policy
Eligible individuals may participate in remote work arrangements subject to operational needs and leadership approval. Specifically, eligible employees could work remotely 1 day per week with their manager's approval. To secure this arrangement, staff members must follow the proper scheduling protocol. Remote days must be requested in the portal at least 2 working days in advance. Submissions that fail to meet this advance notice requirement will not be processed automatically, and managers hold the discretion to decline late submissions. Employees are expected to maintain the same level of communication, responsiveness, and productivity during their remote day as they would while working from the Company office. Edge cases, such as overlapping team requests or critical project deadlines, may require managers to adjust or temporarily suspend remote work days to ensure adequate on-site presence.

## 4. Responsibilities
Managing remote work effectively requires active cooperation between staff members and leadership. Employees are responsible for submitting their schedule requests through the designated portal within the required timeframe and ensuring their home workspace is secure, quiet, and equipped with a stable internet connection. They must remain accessible via official communication channels during standard working hours. Managers are responsible for reviewing and responding to these portal requests in a timely manner, ensuring fair distribution of remote days among team members, and maintaining adequate daily on-site coverage for business needs. Both parties share the responsibility of addressing any technical or communication hurdles promptly to prevent disruptions to ongoing projects.

## 5. Exceptions
While this policy provides a structured approach to flexible work, unique operational circumstances may require adjustments. Exceptions to the standard remote work provisions can occur when urgent business needs, critical system deployments, or mandatory in-person team workshops arise. In such scenarios, managers may require staff members to report to the physical office even if a remote day was previously approved. Furthermore, employees in ineligible departments or those who have not fulfilled the advance notice period cannot claim remote work privileges unless a special exception has been formally authorized by senior leadership. Any unauthorized absence from the physical office will be addressed in accordance with general Company conduct guidelines.

## 6. Related documents
To ensure a comprehensive understanding of workplace expectations, this policy should be read alongside other internal guidelines. Relevant documentation includes the Company employee handbook, information security protocols, attendance guidelines, and standard operating procedures for internal portal usage. Employees must adhere to all data privacy and equipment security rules outlined in these related documents when accessing Company systems from a remote location. Compliance with these supplementary guidelines ensures that remote operations remain secure, compliant, and aligned with organizational standards.
