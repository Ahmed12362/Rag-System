# IT Remote Support Days

## 1. Purpose
The purpose of this document is to establish clear guidelines regarding remote work flexibility for technical support personnel. The Company aims to balance operational continuity with employee flexibility by outlining the framework for working away from the physical office. This policy ensures that service standards remain consistently high while allowing qualified team members to perform their duties from alternate locations without disrupting daily operations or technical service delivery to internal stakeholders.

## 2. Applies to
This policy applies exclusively to the IT department (تقنية المعلومات) within the Company. No other divisions, business units, or corporate groups are covered under these guidelines. Personnel belonging to other departments must consult their respective leadership or corporate handbooks for remote work eligibility and procedures applicable to their specific job functions.

## 3. Department rule
Eligible employees within the technical support division may work remotely up to 2 days per week with their direct manager's approval. To utilize this arrangement, remote days must be requested in the portal at least 2 working days in advance. Team members must secure formal managerial sign-off before commencing any off-site work. Spontaneous or unapproved off-site work is not permitted, and failure to submit requests through the designated portal within the required timeframe will result in the forfeiture of the remote work option for that period.

## 4. Rationale
The operational rationale behind this policy is to maintain robust technical infrastructure support while accommodating the personal and professional needs of the workforce. By capping the frequency at up to 2 days per week, the Company ensures that critical on-site presence is preserved for hardware maintenance, hands-on troubleshooting, and collaborative projects. Requiring requests to be submitted through the portal at least 2 working days in advance allows leadership to manage team schedules effectively, prevent coverage gaps, and maintain adequate staffing levels during peak operational periods.

## 5. Escalation
If a dispute arises regarding scheduling conflicts, portal submission issues, or denied remote work requests, employees must escalate the matter through the proper corporate channels. The direct manager should first be consulted to resolve any misunderstandings or operational constraints. Should the issue remain unresolved, the matter can be forwarded to senior departmental leadership for final review. All decisions made by management regarding off-site work approvals remain final and binding to ensure operational stability within the Company.
