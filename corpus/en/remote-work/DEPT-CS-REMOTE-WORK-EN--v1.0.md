# Customer Support Remote Work Exception

## 1. Purpose
This policy document establishes clear operational guidelines for the organization regarding workplace arrangements within specific operational units. The Company recognizes that different business units require tailored approaches to daily operations, collaboration, and client assistance. By outlining these standards, management ensures transparency across all internal teams and maintains alignment with overall corporate goals. Employees within the designated department must understand how these operational parameters govern their daily schedules, attendance expectations, and general workplace presence. The guidelines serve to prevent ambiguity concerning physical attendance versus remote flexibility, ensuring that every team member clearly understands their scheduling obligations and the reasoning behind them.

## 2. Applies to
The provisions outlined in this policy apply exclusively to the Customer Support department, known internally as دعم العملاء. No other department, business unit, or operational team within the Company falls under the scope of these specific regulations. Personnel assigned to this division are solely governed by these terms, distinguishing their workplace expectations from those of general corporate staff. While other organizational segments might follow standard flexibility frameworks—where eligible employees may work remotely up to 2 days per week with their direct manager's approval—the Customer Support department operates under a distinct operational mandate. Therefore, support agents and associated departmental personnel must strictly adhere to the unit-specific directives detailed in the subsequent sections without exception.

## 3. Department rule
Within the designated support division, staff members are strictly required to perform their duties on-site only. Remote work arrangements are entirely unavailable for this group, meaning personnel must report to the designated corporate facility for every scheduled shift. Operational leadership enforces this physical presence requirement consistently to maintain seamless daily workflows and ensure that all communication channels remain actively monitored during operational hours. Even in scenarios where other organizational employees might request location flexibility, support personnel cannot participate in alternate work arrangements. Management evaluates scheduling conflicts, commuting challenges, and personal requests under the strict premise that physical office attendance is a mandatory condition of employment for this specific role.

## 4. Rationale
The necessity for physical attendance within this specific unit stems directly from shift coverage requirements. Because the team handles high volumes of client inquiries around the clock, leadership must ensure that adequate staffing levels are maintained inside the primary workspace at all times. Managing continuous coverage becomes significantly more complex when dealing with distributed staff, making on-site coordination essential for real-time problem solving, shift handovers, and immediate peer support. Furthermore, maintaining a centralized environment allows supervisors to monitor service quality closely, facilitate rapid coaching, and handle unexpected coverage gaps efficiently. Allowing staff to operate outside the primary office would introduce risks to response times and service reliability, thereby undermining the core objectives of the department.

## 5. Escalation
Should a staff member encounter exceptional personal circumstances or believe that an operational misunderstanding has occurred regarding their workplace requirements, formal resolution pathways are available. Employees must first discuss any scheduling concerns or perceived discrepancies directly with their immediate supervisor to seek clarification. If a resolution cannot be reached at the supervisory level, the matter may be formally elevated to senior departmental management for a comprehensive review. Throughout this escalation process, the foundational rule remains active, ensuring that standard operations and on-site attendance expectations are not disrupted while individual inquiries are being assessed by the appropriate leadership channels within the Company.
