# Appealing a Disciplinary Decision

## 1. Purpose
This internal procedure outlines the official mechanism available to personnel who wish to contest formal disciplinary measures issued by the Company. Ensuring fairness, transparency, and accountability is a fundamental priority for the organization whenever corrective actions are administered. Through this framework, staff members are granted a formal opportunity to present counter-arguments, challenge factual inaccuracies, or highlight procedural oversights regarding penalties they believe were unjustly imposed. Management uses this structured approach to review previous determinations impartially, thereby maintaining trust and consistency across all internal operations. Furthermore, this policy guides supervisors and administrative staff on how to manage dispute submissions properly without compromising the integrity of workplace governance or organizational standards.

## 2. Who this applies to
The guidelines described in this document pertain to all active personnel across every department and operational level within the Company, regardless of employment status, tenure, or classification. Whether an individual holds a permanent contract, temporary assignment, or probationary role, they are fully entitled to utilize this review process if they receive an official disciplinary sanction. Department heads, operational supervisors, and managerial staff must also familiarize themselves with these protocols to ensure they handle grievances correctly and direct personnel to the appropriate administrative channels. Human Resources personnel carry the responsibility of overseeing the entire appeal lifecycle, ensuring that all submissions are processed objectively and in strict adherence to internal bylaws.

## 3. Steps
Initiating a challenge against a disciplinary measure requires strict adherence to a multi-stage workflow designed to protect the rights of all involved parties. First, the affected staff member must prepare a formal, written statement outlining the specific reasons for contesting the penalty, attaching any relevant evidence, correspondence, or witness testimonies that support their case. Second, the individual submits this written challenge to Human Resources for formal intake and review. Third, upon receiving the documentation, Human Resources completes a disciplinary investigation within 5 working days of the complaint to ascertain the facts, interview relevant witnesses, and evaluate whether the initial penalty was warranted. Fourth, investigators compile their findings into a comprehensive report and submit their final recommendation to executive leadership for sign-off. Finally, the department communicates the final determination to the employee in writing, detailing whether the original sanction is upheld, modified, or entirely rescinded. In complex edge cases where external factors or missing documentation surface, the investigative timeline may require careful coordination, though every effort is made to maintain efficiency and fairness.

## 4. Timelines
Strict scheduling parameters govern every phase of the dispute resolution process to prevent unnecessary delays and ensure prompt closure for all stakeholders. Specifically, an employee may appeal a disciplinary decision in writing within 7 calendar days from the date they receive the formal notification of the sanction. Missing this designated application window generally results in the closure of the review file, rendering the original penalty permanent. Following the timely submission of the paperwork, the internal review team must act swiftly, ensuring that Human Resources completes a disciplinary investigation within 5 working days of the complaint. Once a penalty is finalized and applied, it has a lasting administrative footprint; specifically, a warning remains active on the employee's file for 12 months, during which it may influence subsequent performance evaluations or future disciplinary proceedings if further infractions occur.

## 5. Contacts
Navigating the dispute process successfully requires open communication with the designated administrative units within the organization. Personnel seeking guidance on drafting their written submissions, understanding procedural requirements, or checking the status of their active files should reach out directly to the Human Resources department. Designated personnel within Human Resources are available during regular operational hours to provide clarification, receive formal paperwork, and coordinate interviews during the investigative phase. Additionally, departmental supervisors can offer preliminary advice on internal channels, though official submissions must always route through the central administrative team to ensure compliance with Company policies.
