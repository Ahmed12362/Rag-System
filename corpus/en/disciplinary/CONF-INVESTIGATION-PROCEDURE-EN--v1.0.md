# Workplace Investigation Procedure

## 1. Purpose
This internal guide establishes the standard operating procedure that the Company follows whenever workplace misconduct, policy violations, or behavioral complaints are formally reported. Maintaining a fair, structured, and consistent investigative mechanism is essential for protecting staff rights, ensuring a safe professional environment, and upholding institutional integrity. Every inquiry handled by the Company must balance thoroughness with strict confidentiality. This document outlines the responsibilities of administrative personnel, the exact sequence of investigative actions, and the protocols required to document findings properly. By adhering to these guidelines, the Company ensures that all allegations are reviewed impartially, potential liabilities are mitigated, and appropriate corrective measures can be determined based on objective evidence rather than assumptions. The procedures detailed herein serve as the definitive framework for resolving internal grievances across all departments.

## 2. Who this applies to
The directives outlined in this document pertain strictly to authorized management personnel and Human Resources staff operating within the Company. Regular employees and external parties are not granted access to these internal protocols, as this material is designated as strictly confidential. Management teams at all levels must understand their obligation to cooperate fully with inquiries and to maintain absolute discretion when workplace issues arise. Human Resources professionals bear the primary responsibility for executing the investigative steps, gathering testimonies, and compiling official records. By limiting visibility to leadership and personnel specialists, the Company safeguards the privacy of all involved parties, prevents premature disclosures, and preserves the integrity of the fact-finding process from external interference or workplace gossip.

## 3. Steps
When a grievance or allegation requires a formal review, the Company initiates a multi-stage process designed to uncover the facts accurately. First, the relevant department receives the initial notification of the issue and assesses its credibility to determine if a formal inquiry is warranted. Next, fact-finding sessions and disciplinary investigation interviews are scheduled and conducted. To ensure complete transparency, neutrality, and accurate minute-taking, disciplinary investigation interviews are attended by two HR representatives and documented in a signed record. These sessions allow investigators to question the complainant, the respondent, and any relevant witnesses regarding the disputed events. Following the conclusion of all interviews, the gathered evidence, digital records, and signed statements are meticulously analyzed against internal policies. Finally, the compiling personnel summarize their conclusions and forward the documented findings to appropriate executives to decide on the necessary course of action.

## 4. Timelines
Efficiency and promptness are critical components of any internal inquiry conducted by the Company. Delays can compromise the quality of evidence, weaken witness recollections, and create prolonged uncertainty for the staff involved. Therefore, strict scheduling parameters govern every phase of the fact-finding process. Specifically, Human Resources completes a disciplinary investigation within 5 working days of the complaint. This rapid turnaround ensures that workplace disputes do not linger indefinitely and that corrective measures or exonerations can be delivered swiftly. Investigators must manage their schedules proactively, prioritizing these reviews over routine administrative duties to meet the designated deadline. In rare scenarios involving exceptional complexity or unavailability of key individuals, leadership oversees the schedule to maintain momentum while upholding procedural fairness.

## 5. Contacts
Navigating internal reviews requires clear communication channels between operational leaders and the personnel department. Any management official or human resources specialist requiring clarification on these directives should reach out directly to the central internal relations office of the Company. General inquiries regarding scheduling, documentation standards, or procedural ambiguities can be directed to the primary administrative helpdesk maintained by the personnel division. For sensitive matters involving executive oversight or severe policy breaches, designated senior managers must be notified immediately through secure communication channels. Maintaining designated contact points ensures that all information remains restricted to authorized personnel and prevents unauthorized leaks that could compromise an ongoing inquiry or damage the reputation of the Company.
