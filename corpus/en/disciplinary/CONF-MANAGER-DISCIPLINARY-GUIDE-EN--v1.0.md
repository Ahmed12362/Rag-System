# Manager Guide to Disciplinary Cases

## 1. Purpose
This confidential internal document serves as an essential manual for management and Human Resources personnel within the Company. Addressing behavioral or performance issues requires a structured approach to maintain fairness, transparency, and operational consistency. This guide outlines the proper procedures that managers must follow when handling workplace infractions. By establishing clear standards, the Company ensures that all disciplinary matters are managed objectively and in accordance with established internal policies. Leaders play a crucial role in identifying issues early, documenting events accurately, and consulting with support departments to resolve conflicts appropriately before they escalate into formal procedures.

## 2. Who this applies to
The policies detailed in this manual apply strictly to all management personnel and Human Resources staff across the Company. Team leads, supervisors, and department heads must familiarize themselves with these guidelines to ensure they respond correctly to policy violations or performance shortcomings within their teams. Because this material contains sensitive personnel information and internal operational standards, it is designated for internal management and Human Resources use only. Unauthorized distribution or sharing of these guidelines outside of authorized leadership circles is strictly prohibited.

## 3. Steps
When addressing workplace infractions, leaders must follow a progressive framework to correct employee behavior. The disciplinary steps are, in order: verbal warning, written warning, final warning, salary deduction and suspension; serious misconduct may skip steps. 

To initiate a formal review, leaders must report any major infraction immediately. Following a formal complaint, Human Resources completes a disciplinary investigation within 5 working days of the complaint. During this fact-finding phase, disciplinary investigation interviews are attended by two HR representatives and documented in a signed record. These recorded interviews protect both the employee and the Company by ensuring that all statements are accurately captured and acknowledged by all participants. Once the investigation concludes, management reviews the findings and determines the appropriate level of corrective action based on the established sequence, unless the severity of the misconduct justifies bypassing earlier stages.

## 4. Timelines
Managing workplace issues efficiently depends on adhering to strict operational schedules. Delays can compromise the integrity of the process and impact team morale. As mandated by internal protocols, Human Resources completes a disciplinary investigation within 5 working days of the complaint. Managers must ensure that all preliminary notes, witness statements, and relevant evidence are submitted promptly at the start of this period to allow the review team sufficient time to conduct thorough interviews and finalize the documentation. Adherence to these schedules ensures timely resolution and maintains fairness for all parties involved.

## 5. Contacts
For guidance, questions, or clarification regarding disciplinary procedures and investigations, management personnel should reach out directly to the designated Human Resources department within the Company. HR advisors are available to assist leaders in determining the correct stage of intervention, reviewing documentation, and scheduling required interviews. Consulting with HR before taking any formal action ensures that all internal policies are properly applied and that the rights and responsibilities of both the employee and the organization are fully protected throughout the entire disciplinary process.
