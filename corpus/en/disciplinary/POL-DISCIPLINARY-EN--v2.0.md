# Disciplinary Policy

## 1. Purpose
The purpose of this internal document is to establish a clear, fair, and structured framework for maintaining professional standards and addressing conduct issues across the organization. The Company is dedicated to fostering a productive, respectful, and safe working environment for all staff members. This framework ensures that any performance or behavioral concerns are managed consistently, transparently, and equitably. By outlining expected behaviors and the consequences of failing to meet them, this policy protects the rights of workers while safeguarding operational integrity. It provides a reliable mechanism to investigate incidents, apply corrective measures, and offer opportunities for improvement, while ensuring that severe infractions are met with appropriate and decisive action.

## 2. Scope
This policy applies universally to all personnel employed by the Company, regardless of their department, tenure, or employment status. It covers all company-related activities, including remote work arrangements, official business travel, organization-sponsored events, and communications conducted via corporate tools. Every employee is expected to understand and adhere to the standards of conduct described herein. Direct managers, supervisors, and administrative personnel must also familiarize themselves with these guidelines to ensure they apply corrective measures uniformly and fairly. The principles outlined in this document apply to all routine workplace interactions as well as formal proceedings, ensuring that every team member receives fair treatment under identical operational standards.

## 3. Policy
Maintaining order and accountability requires a structured approach to corrective action. When performance or conduct falls below expected standards, the Company follows a progressive sequence of disciplinary steps, in order: verbal warning, written warning, final warning, salary deduction, and suspension. However, in cases of severe misconduct, the management reserves the right to skip steps and proceed immediately to more severe measures. 

To address specific attendance issues, punctuality is strictly monitored. Specifically, more than 3 late arrivals in the same calendar month result in a deduction of a quarter of a day's pay for each additional late arrival. 

Once issued, a warning remains active on the employee's file for 12 months. Should an employee face a disciplinary suspension, this measure may not exceed 5 working days. Privacy is paramount during these procedures; all disciplinary records are confidential and visible only to Human Resources and the employee's direct manager. Furthermore, if an employee wishes to contest an outcome, they may appeal a disciplinary decision in writing within 7 calendar days of receiving the notification.

## 4. Responsibilities
Management and staff share distinct duties in upholding workplace standards. Direct managers are responsible for monitoring team performance, identifying behavioral issues early, addressing minor concerns informally, and reporting serious infractions immediately. Managers must also maintain strict confidentiality regarding any ongoing reviews and collaborate with internal departments to ensure fair treatment. 

Human Resources plays a central role in managing the administrative and investigative aspects of these procedures. Human Resources completes a disciplinary investigation within 5 working days of the complaint, ensuring thorough evidence collection and impartial interviews with all involved parties. 

Employees are responsible for familiarizing themselves with workplace expectations, attending scheduled discussions punctually, and participating honestly in any fact-finding processes. Staff members also hold the responsibility of submitting formal appeals within the designated timeframe if they believe a decision was unjust.

## 5. Exceptions
While this framework is designed to cover the vast majority of workplace scenarios, rare operational constraints or extraordinary legal circumstances may require localized adjustments. Any deviation from the standard investigative timeline, progressive steps, or appeal procedures must be formally evaluated and approved by executive management in consultation with legal advisors. Furthermore, specific external regulatory requirements or contractual obligations that supersede internal guidelines will be addressed on an individual basis. In all exceptional cases, the core principles of fairness, transparency, and confidentiality must be maintained to the greatest extent possible, ensuring that employees' fundamental rights are not compromised by temporary operational hurdles.

## 6. Related documents
To ensure comprehensive understanding and alignment across the organization, this policy should be read in conjunction with other internal governance documents. These include the employee handbook, the code of conduct, the attendance and punctuality guidelines, the grievance procedure, and the performance management framework. Together, these documents form the complete regulatory foundation of the Company, guiding everyday professional behavior and ensuring that internal processes remain harmonized, legally compliant, and easily accessible to all personnel seeking further guidance on workplace rights and obligations.
