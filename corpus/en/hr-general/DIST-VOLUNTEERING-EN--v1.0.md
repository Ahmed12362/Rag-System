# Community Volunteering Programme

## 1. Purpose
The purpose of this document is to establish a clear framework for civic engagement and social contribution within the organization. The enterprise encourages its workforce to actively participate in charitable activities and support local causes that align with our corporate values. Giving back to society fosters team collaboration, enhances morale, and builds stronger connections with the communities where we operate. Through this initiative, staff members are empowered to dedicate their time and skills to impactful projects without financial detriment. This policy outlines how the initiative functions, ensuring that participation is organized transparently and efficiently, while maintaining business continuity across all departments.

## 2. Scope
This policy applies to all personnel who are eligible to participate in the corporate citizenship initiatives. It is important to note that this framework does not alter, modify, or replace any existing rules, regulations, or entitlements for regular Company employees. The guidelines detailed herein are designed to operate alongside standard operational procedures without introducing new leave categories or altering contractual obligations. Contractors, temporary workers, and third-party vendors should consult their respective management regarding their inclusion in corporate responsibility events. The provisions outlined in this document apply strictly to approved civic service activities endorsed by the organization.

## 3. Policy
The community programme managed by the enterprise offers 1 paid volunteering day per year. This allocated time allows individuals to contribute meaningfully to approved charitable causes, educational workshops, environmental cleanups, or other socially beneficial endeavors during regular working hours. Participants must schedule and arrange this benefit directly through the CSR team, as it is not recognized or processed as a leave type in the HR portal. Submitting a request through alternative channels or standard absence modules will not be accepted. Staff members must coordinate their absence well in advance with their direct supervisors to ensure that operational requirements and project deliverables remain unaffected by their participation in social initiatives.

## 4. Responsibilities
Managing corporate citizenship activities requires collaboration between multiple stakeholders within the organization. Participants wishing to engage in charitable work are responsible for identifying approved activities, planning their schedule, and coordinating directly with the CSR team to secure authorization for the designated day. Individuals must also inform their immediate managers promptly to guarantee that team workloads are managed effectively during their absence. The CSR team is tasked with overseeing the programme, verifying the legitimacy of external charitable organizations, coordinating administrative logistics, and maintaining records of participation. Supervisors and managers are responsible for reviewing requests objectively, ensuring fair distribution of workloads, and supporting team members who wish to contribute positively to society while upholding organizational commitments.

## 5. Exceptions
Any scenario that falls outside the standard parameters of this policy requires formal review and written approval from designated management. Because the framework does not alter standard rules for regular Company employees, any requests for additional time beyond the established allowance cannot be granted through this specific initiative. Circumstances involving international travel, high-risk physical activities, or affiliations with political or controversial organizations are strictly excluded from the scope of approved civic service. Any individual attempting to utilize this corporate benefit for unapproved purposes or failing to follow the correct booking channel through the CSR team will have their request denied. Exceptions are evaluated on a case-by-case basis by leadership to protect the reputation and operational integrity of the enterprise.

## 6. Related documents
To ensure comprehensive understanding and alignment with internal governance, participants should review several supplementary materials alongside this policy. Relevant documentation includes the corporate code of conduct, internal CSR guidelines, general workplace behavior frameworks, and departmental operational procedures. While the CSR team provides specific instructions on booking the allocated day, additional guidance regarding workplace standards and ethical participation can be found on the internal intranet portal. Familiarity with these related resources ensures that all organizational activities are conducted professionally, safely, and in full compliance with overarching corporate policies.
