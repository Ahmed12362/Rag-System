# HR Records Access Rules

## 1. Purpose
The management of employee information within the workplace requires strict governance to protect privacy while ensuring daily operations run smoothly. Within the organization, maintaining trust depends on how personnel data is handled across all internal teams. This policy outlines the standards for managing, viewing, and monitoring files associated with the workforce. By establishing clear guidelines, the business ensures that personal information is never exposed unnecessarily and that internal accountability is maintained at all times. Staff members managing these documents must understand their responsibilities to prevent data mishandling, unauthorized sharing, or improper internal browsing. Proper handling safeguards both the organization and its personnel from privacy breaches, ensuring that administrative duties are performed with the highest standard of discretion and professionalism.

## 2. Applies to
This directive is restricted in scope and applies exclusively to the Human Resources department. No other business units, divisions, or external entities are covered by these specific operational standards. Personnel working within this specific division must adhere strictly to these guidelines when interacting with workforce documentation, physical files, and digital databases. Leadership within the division is responsible for ensuring that all team members are fully trained on these boundaries and understand that the rules govern their daily administrative tasks without exception.

## 3. Department rule
Team members within the division view personnel files strictly on a need-to-know basis, ensuring that no file is opened without a direct, work-related justification. Every instance of accessing restricted records must be logged meticulously to create a transparent audit trail of who viewed what file and when. Furthermore, disciplinary records are strictly confidential and visible only to the personnel team and the employee's direct manager. Other colleagues, regardless of their standing in the organization, have no right to view these sensitive warnings or formal reprimands. Staff must verify that access requests meet these exact criteria before opening any sensitive documents in the system.

## 4. Rationale
Limiting visibility of sensitive documentation prevents gossip, bias, and unauthorized data sharing within the workplace. When personnel files and formal warnings are kept secure, employees feel safer knowing their personal struggles or performance corrections remain private. The requirement to log every interaction with restricted files ensures that the organization can investigate any potential data leaks or policy violations effectively. Restricting disciplinary notes strictly to the personnel team and the direct manager ensures that leadership actions remain professional and targeted, preventing unfair judgments from unrelated staff members who lack the full context of a situation.

## 5. Escalation
If a staff member within the division notices an unauthorized attempt to view restricted files, or if there is a system failure in the logging mechanism, they must report the issue immediately to senior management. Attempting to bypass the logging system or viewing confidential disciplinary data without proper authorization will trigger a formal internal review. Management will investigate any reported breaches promptly to determine the cause and apply corrective measures. Employees who are unsure whether a specific request meets the justification threshold must consult their supervisor before proceeding with opening any sensitive workforce files.
