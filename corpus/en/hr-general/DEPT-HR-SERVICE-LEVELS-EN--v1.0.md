# HR Service Levels

## 1. Purpose
This internal guideline establishes clear performance standards and delivery timelines for internal inquiries and formal procedures managed by the organization. The primary objective is to maintain operational transparency, fairness, and consistency across all internal operations. By setting specific performance benchmarks, the Company ensures that staff concerns are addressed methodically and professionally, mitigating potential risks and fostering a supportive work environment.

## 2. Applies to
The provisions outlined in this policy apply exclusively to the Human Resources department. No other organizational unit is bound by these specific operational timeframes, as this division holds sole responsibility for managing internal relations, investigations, and employee grievances. Every team member within the Human Resources department must adhere strictly to these defined service levels during their daily administrative duties.

## 3. Department rule
The department must operate in strict accordance with mandatory timeframes for processing formal concerns and inquiries. Specifically, Human Resources responds to a formal grievance within 10 working days of receipt. Additionally, Human Resources completes a disciplinary investigation within 5 working days of the complaint. These timeframes represent the maximum allowable duration for addressing such matters and require diligent tracking by the assigned personnel to prevent delays.

## 4. Rationale
Establishing rigid service benchmarks ensures that employee relations are handled with the urgency and seriousness they warrant. Delays in addressing formal complaints can erode trust and exacerbate workplace friction, making timely intervention essential. By committing to specific operational deadlines, the Company protects both its workforce and its institutional integrity, ensuring that investigations are thorough yet swift, and that formal grievances receive timely official acknowledgment.

## 5. Escalation
When internal timelines face potential delays due to unforeseen operational hurdles, staff must immediately notify management to prevent breaches of the mandated service standards. Cases requiring complex fact-finding or involving multiple parties may present edge cases where standard procedures take longer than usual. In such instances, the department lead must review the circumstances, document the bottleneck, and take corrective action to resolve the matter as close to the original deadline as practically possible.
