# Termination Process

## 1. Purpose
The purpose of this internal document is to establish a rigorous, fair, and transparent procedure for handling employment separations within the Company. Ensuring consistency across all departments is vital for maintaining organizational integrity, protecting employee rights, and mitigating legal and operational risks. Whenever an employment relationship is brought to an end, strict adherence to established protocols prevents misunderstandings and guarantees that every case is managed with the utmost professionalism. This guide serves as the definitive framework for supervisors, administrators, and leadership personnel to navigate complex employment transitions smoothly and ethically.

## 2. Who this applies to
This policy is strictly confidential and is intended exclusively for Human Resources professionals, department managers, and executive leadership within the Company. It applies to all personnel involved in initiating, reviewing, approving, or executing employment terminations across any division, branch, or subsidiary. Because personnel matters demand high discretion, this document must never be shared outside authorized management circles. Understanding these guidelines is mandatory for any leader who oversees staff, as improper handling of separations can expose the organization to significant liabilities.

## 3. Steps
Executing an employment separation requires a careful, multi-stage approach to ensure fairness and full compliance with internal governance. First, any termination requires a documented investigation and approval from the Head of Human Resources and the department head. Supervisors cannot unilaterally dismiss personnel without completing this factual inquiry and securing dual sign-off from both designated authorities. Once the investigation concludes and the required approvals are formally granted, leadership must prepare the official notification and transition plan. Furthermore, if a disciplinary penalty is issued, an employee may appeal a disciplinary decision in writing within 7 calendar days. This appeal right ensures that the affected individual has a fair opportunity to present counter-arguments or new context before final operational measures take effect. Human Resources must log all submitted appeals, review the contested facts impartially, and communicate the final review outcome to the worker without unnecessary delay.

## 4. Timelines
Managing schedules efficiently during an employment separation is critical for administrative closure and payroll accuracy. While specific durations depend on the complexity of the case and the nature of the infraction, strict deadlines govern the appeals process. Specifically, an employee may appeal a disciplinary decision in writing within 7 calendar days following the formal issuance of the notice. Adhering to this window is mandatory; submissions received after the expiration of this period will not be considered by management, and the original ruling will become permanent. Department heads and human resources staff must factor these scheduling windows into their transition planning to avoid premature offboarding or delayed responses to appeals.

## 5. Contacts
For guidance, clarifications, or procedural support regarding employment separations, authorized leaders must reach out to the appropriate internal channels. The primary point of contact for all formal approvals, investigation protocols, and disciplinary appeals is the Head of Human Resources, alongside the respective department head who holds operational oversight. Because these matters are sensitive and strictly confidential, all inquiries, documentation submissions, and escalation requests must be handled through secure corporate communication channels designated exclusively for management and human resources personnel.
