# HR Services FAQ - Part 2

## 1. How do I initiate my resignation process with the Company?
When an employee decides to leave their position, proper notice must be provided to management. Specifically, employees are required to give 30 calendar days of written notice when resigning. This advance notification period allows the Company to plan for operational continuity, initiate recruitment for the vacancy, and prepare the necessary offboarding paperwork. Submitting formal written notice starts the official countdown. During this period, the employee is expected to continue fulfilling their regular duties, collaborate with supervisors on transition planning, and ensure that their daily responsibilities are documented so that colleagues can easily step in.

## 2. When must Company equipment be returned during offboarding?
As part of the departure procedure, every departing staff member is required to return all property belonging to the Company. This includes items such as laptops, security badges, access tokens, mobile phones, and any physical documents or assets assigned during employment. Furthermore, exit clearance must be completed by the last working day. Failing to return these items or complete the clearance workflow on time can create administrative delays and potential compliance issues. Supervisors and the HR team assist departing personnel in completing their checklist to ensure all assets are accounted for before the final departure hour.

## 3. When can I expect to receive my final settlement payment?
The final financial reconciliation is processed after the employee fulfills all offboarding obligations. The final settlement is paid within 3 weeks of clearance. This timeframe allows the payroll department to calculate accrued leaves, final salary adjustments, and any other applicable dues once the official exit clearance form is signed and approved. Delays in returning Company assets or delayed completion of the exit clearance will consequently postpone the start of this processing window. Employees should verify that their banking details remain active and unchanged during this period to facilitate a smooth transfer of funds.

## 4. Am I eligible to apply for an internal transfer within the Company?
Career development and internal mobility are actively supported for staff members wishing to explore new career paths. Employees may apply for an internal transfer after 12 months in their current role. This tenure requirement ensures that staff members have established a solid performance record, mastered their initial responsibilities, and contributed meaningfully to their current team before transitioning elsewhere. Applying before completing this required duration is generally not permitted unless an explicit exception is authorized by senior management due to exceptional business restructuring or urgent departmental needs.

## 5. What steps should I follow to submit an internal transfer application?
To pursue a new position internally, an employee who meets the necessary tenure requirement should first discuss their career aspirations with their current direct manager. Open communication ensures that expectations are managed transparently. Following this discussion, the employee can submit their application through the internal careers portal. It is important to review the job description of the target role to confirm that your skills and background align with the department's expectations. The receiving manager will coordinate with HR to review the application, schedule interviews, and determine a suitable handover timeline if the transfer is approved.

## 6. How does an internal transfer impact my current responsibilities and timeline?
When an internal move is successfully arranged, coordinating the transition date between the current department and the hiring department is crucial. The employee must wrap up ongoing projects, hand over current tasks smoothly, and ensure no critical gaps are left behind. While the 12-month tenure rule qualifies the staff member to apply, the actual physical or functional move date is subject to operational readiness. Both managers collaborate to agree on a realistic handover period, balancing the needs of the outgoing team with the urgency of the incoming role, ensuring that the employee's professional growth aligns with the ongoing success of the organization.
