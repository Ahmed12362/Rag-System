# Resignation and Exit Clearance

## 1. Purpose
This internal procedure outlines the official framework established by the Company for handling staff resignations and the subsequent departure process. Managing employee departures effectively ensures a smooth transition of responsibilities, protects organizational assets, and maintains operational continuity. The Company is committed to treating departing personnel with professionalism while safeguarding its operational interests. This guide details the obligations of resigning individuals, the responsibilities of managers and administrative departments, and the sequence of events required to finalize an employment contract. By standardizing these actions, the organization ensures consistency, fairness, and compliance across all departments when an individual decides to leave their position.

## 2. Who this applies to
The guidelines described in this document apply universally to all personnel currently employed by the Company across all departments, job levels, and employment types. Whether an individual holds a permanent, temporary, full-time, or part-time role, the protocols for ending the employment relationship remain consistent. Department heads, direct supervisors, human resources representatives, and finance personnel must also familiarize themselves with these directives to properly execute their respective duties during a staff member's departure. Adherence to this procedure is mandatory for all internal stakeholders involved in preparing, reviewing, processing, or approving exit documentation and final settlements.

## 3. Steps
The departure process begins when a staff member formally decides to leave their role. As part of this transition, employees must give 30 calendar days of written notice when resigning, formally informing management of their departure date in writing. Upon receiving this notification, the direct supervisor schedules a handover meeting to reassign ongoing tasks and projects to other team members. Throughout this notice period, the departing individual is expected to complete pending assignments and document their daily workflows. A critical part of the departure procedure involves company property. Company equipment must be returned and exit clearance completed by the last working day, ensuring that all physical assets, keys, access cards, and digital devices are handed over to the appropriate department. Human resources and IT departments then verify the returned items and sign off on the clearance form. Following the successful completion of all handover and asset return requirements, the finance department calculates the final financial dues. The final settlement is paid within 3 weeks of clearance, ensuring all earned wages and approved expense reimbursements are fully disbursed to the departing individual.

## 4. Timelines
Managing the schedule of a departure requires careful adherence to established deadlines to prevent administrative delays. The initial notice period requires resigning personnel to submit their formal written intent well in advance, ensuring the organization has adequate time to adjust workforce capacity. Specifically, staff members must provide notice covering the designated timeframe of 30 calendar days of written notice when resigning. Throughout this duration, operational handover must take place progressively. The final milestone for returning company property and obtaining administrative approvals occurs on the employee's final day of service, as company equipment must be returned and exit clearance completed by the last working day. Once the clearance process is finalized and all administrative hurdles are cleared, the financial team initiates the disbursement of final dues. The subsequent payment timeline dictates that the final settlement is paid within 3 weeks of clearance, providing a structured window for accounting teams to process calculations and transfer funds securely.

## 5. Contacts
For any questions, guidance, or clarifications regarding the resignation process, staff members should reach out to the appropriate internal support channels within the organization. The human resources department serves as the primary point of contact for submitting formal resignation letters, discussing notice period requirements, and coordinating the logistics of the exit clearance form. Additionally, the IT department is available to assist with the proper return of digital devices, software license reassignments, and email archiving. The finance team handles inquiries related to the final financial dues and the subsequent payout schedule. Supervisors and department managers are also available locally within each team to guide personnel through daily handover tasks and ensure a collaborative, transparent transition period for everyone involved.
