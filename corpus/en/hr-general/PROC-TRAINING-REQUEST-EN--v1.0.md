# Applying for Training

## 1. Purpose
The purpose of this procedure is to outline the process for team members at the Company to request, schedule, and complete their professional development activities. Continuous learning is a vital part of our culture, ensuring that everyone remains updated with industry standards and enhances their daily performance. By establishing a clear framework, the Company helps staff align their personal career aspirations with broader organizational goals. This document provides clear guidelines to ensure all educational requests are reviewed fairly, documented properly, and supported adequately by management. Team members are encouraged to proactively identify skill gaps and pursue educational opportunities that benefit both their individual career trajectories and the overall mission of the organization.

## 2. Who this applies to
This procedure applies to all active personnel currently employed by the Company across all departments and locations. Whether you are a newly onboarded team member or a long-serving professional, these guidelines govern how you request and undertake educational programs. It is important to note that each employee is expected to complete at least 40 hours of training per year to maintain their professional edge and meet internal performance standards. Managers are also included in the scope, as they play a crucial role in reviewing, guiding, and approving submissions from their direct reports. Contractors, temporary staff, and external consultants are generally excluded from this internal development framework unless specifically authorized by their department head or through a specialized agreement with the Company.

## 3. Steps
To successfully apply for any educational program or professional development session within the Company, individuals must follow a structured series of sequential actions. First, review your annual educational goals and ensure you are working toward the mandatory target of spending at least 40 hours of training per year on approved activities. Second, discuss your chosen course, seminar, or certification path with your direct supervisor to confirm its relevance to your current role or future career progression. Third, complete the internal requisition form with all relevant details, including dates, provider information, and associated costs. Fourth, submit the completed application through the internal portal for managerial review. In cases where an individual is preparing for a certification, employees sitting an approved professional exam may take up to 5 days of study leave per year, deducted from annual leave, provided they submit their formal exam schedule alongside their initial request. Fifth, await final confirmation from the human resources department before registering or making travel arrangements. If your application is deferred, review the feedback with your manager, adjust your submission, and resubmit during the next open window.

## 4. Timelines
Managing schedules effectively requires adherence to specific deadlines throughout the application and learning lifecycle. Team members must submit their educational requests well in advance of the proposed start date to allow adequate time for managerial review and logistical planning. When preparing for professional certifications, planning ahead is essential, particularly if you intend to utilize the provision where employees sitting an approved professional exam may take up to 5 days of study leave per year, deducted from annual leave. This preparation ensures that team coverage is maintained within the department while individuals are away studying. Throughout the calendar cycle, participants should monitor their progress diligently to ensure they successfully meet the baseline expectation where each employee is expected to complete at least 40 hours of training per year. Any requests submitted past the seasonal cutoff dates will automatically be deferred to the following evaluation period, unless an exception is granted by senior leadership due to urgent operational necessity.

## 5. Contacts
If you encounter any questions or require further clarification regarding the educational application process, several internal resources are available to assist you. Your primary point of contact should always be your direct supervisor or department manager, who can provide guidance on role-specific skill requirements and help you plan how to fulfill the expectation that each employee is expected to complete at least 40 hours of training per year. For technical issues related to the submission portal or questions concerning policy interpretations, you can reach out directly to the internal human resources helpdesk. Additionally, if you need assistance coordinating your schedule around special accommodations, such as utilizing the policy where employees sitting an approved professional exam may take up to 5 days of study leave per year, deducted from annual leave, the personnel administration team is available to help process your paperwork and ensure a smooth transition before your examination date.
