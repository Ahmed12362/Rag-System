# HR Case Handling Standards

## 1. Purpose
This document establishes the official framework and standard operating procedures for managing employee relations matters within the organization. The Company requires a consistent, fair, and structured approach to address workplace concerns, disputes, and conduct issues. By defining clear operational workflows, the Company ensures that all internal matters are handled with utmost professionalism and adherence to internal policies. These standards protect organizational integrity while safeguarding the rights of all personnel. Every team member involved in case management must understand their duties to maintain a harmonious work environment. The guidelines detailed here help prevent escalation, promote transparent communication, and ensure equitable treatment across all departments.

## 2. Who this applies to
The directives outlined in this manual apply strictly to designated personnel within the Company. Specifically, Human Resources professionals, department heads, and executive management must follow these protocols when addressing workplace conflicts or disciplinary events. Because employee records and conduct files require strict privacy, disciplinary records are confidential and visible only to Human Resources and the employee's direct manager. Unauthorized access or sharing of such sensitive information is strictly prohibited. Managers at all levels must coordinate closely with internal personnel specialists whenever a behavioral or performance dispute arises, ensuring that all confidential data remains restricted to authorized viewers only.

## 3. Steps
Managing employee cases requires a methodical approach to ensure fairness and accuracy. When formal issues are raised, the Company follows a structured sequence of actions. First, the relevant department logs the inquiry and routes it to the appropriate internal team. Next, fact-finding sessions and formal inquiries are scheduled. Specifically, disciplinary investigation interviews are attended by two HR representatives and documented in a signed record. This dual-attendance requirement guarantees complete objectivity and prevents any perception of bias during fact-finding. Following the inquiry, the gathered evidence is reviewed thoroughly by the administrative committee. In complex scenarios, such as when an employee disputes the documented facts or refuses to sign the record, the attending specialists must add an explanatory addendum to the file while strictly limiting access to authorized management levels.

## 4. Timelines
Adhering to strict schedules is vital for maintaining trust and operational efficiency in case resolution. The Company mandates prompt action for every reported incident or formal complaint. For instance, Human Resources responds to a formal grievance within 10 working days. This timeframe allows the evaluation team to gather necessary context, interview relevant parties, and formulate a considered response without causing undue delays. Edge cases, such as the sudden absence of a key witness or complex multi-departmental grievances, may stretch administrative investigations; however, the initial acknowledgement and response clock still runs toward the mandatory threshold to ensure accountability.

## 5. Contacts
For guidance, reporting, or clarification regarding these standards, personnel must reach out to the appropriate internal channels. The Company maintains dedicated points of contact to assist with grievance submissions, interview scheduling, and file access requests. Employees should direct their inquiries to the primary human resources helpdesk or schedule an appointment with their designated personnel advisor. Management personnel seeking clarification on viewing permissions for confidential disciplinary files should contact the centralized administrative oversight team. Ensuring proper communication channels helps maintain confidentiality and expedites the resolution of any workplace matter.
