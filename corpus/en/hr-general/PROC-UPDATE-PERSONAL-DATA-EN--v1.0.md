# Updating Your Personal Data

## 1. Purpose
Maintaining accurate employee records is essential for effective administration and communication within the Company. When life events occur, such as relocating to a new residence, changing your marital status, or altering who should be reached in an urgent situation, keeping this information current ensures that operational workflows, benefit administrations, and emergency responses proceed without interruption. The Company relies on accurate data to provide appropriate support to its workforce. This internal procedure outlines the precise mechanism by which staff members must maintain the integrity of their personnel files. Compliance with this procedure is a standard expectation for all personnel, safeguarding both the individual employee and the organization by ensuring that official records reflect reality.

## 2. Who this applies to
This internal procedure applies universally to all active personnel currently engaged by the Company, regardless of their department, role, or contractual status. Every individual working within the organization is personally responsible for reviewing their own records and submitting required updates whenever their personal circumstances change. Managers, team leads, and administrative staff are also expected to encourage their teams to maintain up-to-date profiles. While the human resources department oversees the overarching data management systems, the direct obligation to initiate modifications rests firmly with each employee. Understanding this responsibility helps maintain a seamless administrative environment across all company locations.

## 3. Steps
When a modification occurs in your personal life, such as a change of residential address, a shift in marital status, or a replacement for your designated emergency contact, you must execute the update through the designated digital portal. First, log into your personal profile using your official credentials provided by the Company. Navigate to the profile management or personal details section of the interface. Locate the specific field that requires adjustment, whether it is your street address, your family status, or the phone number of your primary emergency contact. Input the correct and verified information into the designated form fields carefully, ensuring there are no typographical errors. Finally, save and submit the changes within the system to ensure your personnel file is refreshed immediately.

## 4. Timelines
Time management is critical when managing official records. Employees must update changes to personal data (address, marital status, emergency contact) in the portal within 7 days of the event occurring. Adhering to this timeframe prevents administrative discrepancies and ensures that the Company holds legally compliant and valid information for every worker. Failing to meet this requirement may result in delayed communications, missed benefits notifications, or complications during urgent situations where emergency contacts are required. It is best practice to submit your updates immediately after any life event takes place, well before the final deadline expires, to guarantee continuous compliance with internal policies.

## 5. Contacts
Navigating internal procedures sometimes generates inquiries regarding specific requirements or technical system functions. Questions about HR policies are raised through the HR helpdesk in the portal. The dedicated support team monitors these inquiries to provide timely guidance on how to properly maintain your personal records or how to interpret compliance expectations. If you encounter technical difficulties while attempting to access your profile or if you are unsure whether a specific life event requires a formal record modification, submitting a ticket through the helpdesk ensures your inquiry is routed to the appropriate specialist within the Company.
