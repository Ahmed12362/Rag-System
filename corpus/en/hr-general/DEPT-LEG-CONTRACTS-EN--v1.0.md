# Legal Contract Review Turnaround

## 1. Purpose
This policy establishes a clear timeline and operational standard for processing contractual agreements submitted to internal teams. The goal of this document is to ensure that operational workflows remain predictable and efficient across all business units interacting with the legal department. By setting explicit processing expectations, the organization minimizes bottlenecks during negotiations and safeguards its commercial interests. Clear turnaround guidelines also empower staff to plan their project timelines accurately, factoring in the time required for thorough legal scrutiny before finalizing any agreements with external partners, vendors, or clients.

## 2. Applies to
This policy is exclusively applicable to the Legal department (الشؤون القانونية). No other operational units, business divisions, or administrative branches are governed by the procedures outlined herein. All staff members, legal counsels, and department heads operating within the legal division must adhere strictly to the directives and timelines specified in this document when processing agreements submitted by other parts of the organization.

## 3. Department rule
The internal rule mandates that the legal team reviews standard contracts within 5 working days of receiving a complete request. To initiate this timeframe, the submission must include all necessary background documentation, approved commercial terms, and required stakeholder signatures or inputs. If an incoming submission lacks vital information, the turnaround clock does not start until the submitting party provides the missing details. Legal professionals must prioritize incoming standard agreements efficiently to meet this service level agreement, ensuring that routine reviews do not experience unnecessary delays while still maintaining rigorous scrutiny of all clauses and potential risks.

## 4. Rationale
Establishing a defined processing window balances the need for thorough risk mitigation with the fast-paced demands of commercial operations. Standard agreements often contain routine clauses that do not require extended negotiation, making a structured timeline feasible for the legal team. Adhering to this timeframe prevents operational gridlock, allowing the organization to close deals promptly without compromising on legal compliance or protective contractual language. Furthermore, transparent expectations help build trust and accountability between the legal division and the various business units relying on their expertise.

## 5. Escalation
When a review request faces potential delays or exceptional complications, specific escalation procedures must be followed to resolve the bottleneck. If a standard contract cannot be evaluated within the established timeframe due to high workloads or unexpected complexities, the matter should be brought to the attention of the legal department head. Submitting teams experiencing undue delays may also flag the issue through designated internal communication channels to request status updates and prioritization adjustments. This escalation pathway ensures that senior management can reallocate resources or address systemic blockages swiftly, keeping the company's contractual operations running smoothly and maintaining alignment across all departments involved in the review process.
