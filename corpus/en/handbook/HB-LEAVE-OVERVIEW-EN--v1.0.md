# Employee Handbook: Leave Types Overview

## 1. Overview
Welcome to the internal guide on time off at the Company! We believe that taking breaks, resting, and handling unexpected life events are essential for maintaining a healthy work-life balance. To support this, the Company provides a variety of leave types designed to give you peace of mind and adequate downtime throughout the calendar year. Whether you are planning a well-deserved vacation, recovering from an illness, managing a sudden personal emergency, or needing an extended break with management approval, we have structures in place to help. Understanding your time-off benefits ensures you can rest effectively while keeping your team informed and operations running smoothly.

## 2. Details
Our time-off program encompasses several distinct categories, each tailored to different situations you might encounter during your employment. For your regular rest and recreation, employees are entitled to 21 working days of paid annual leave per calendar year. When health issues arise and you need time to recuperate, employees are entitled to 15 working days of paid sick leave per calendar year. Life is unpredictable, and urgent matters often pop up without warning; to handle these situations, employees have 6 days of casual leave per calendar year for urgent personal matters. Sometimes, you may need an extended period away from the office for unique circumstances. For such situations, unpaid leave of up to 30 working days per year may be granted with approval from the direct manager and Human Resources; it has no balance. Each of these allocations resets or applies according to specific guidelines to ensure fair and consistent support for everyone across the organization.

## 3. What employees should do
Navigating your time off requires clear communication and adherence to our internal procedures. Whenever you need to take a break or handle an emergency, it is vital to follow the correct administrative path. Please note that all leave requests are submitted and tracked through the HR self-service portal; email or verbal requests are not valid. By using the portal, you create an official paper trail that protects both you and the Company, ensuring your time off is accurately recorded and approved by the right individuals. Make sure to submit your requests well in advance for planned vacations, or as soon as possible for unexpected situations like sick leave or casual days, so your department can prepare accordingly.

## 4. Related policies
To ensure a harmonious workplace and consistent application of our guidelines, this leave overview connects closely with several other internal company directives. For instance, understanding how your time off interacts with attendance expectations, performance management, and corporate behavioral standards will help you make the most of your benefits. While the details above cover the core types of time off available at the Company, always consult supplementary documentation regarding rollover rules, documentation requirements for medical absences, and specific workflows within the HR self-service portal to remain fully compliant with internal standards.
