# Employee Handbook: Working Time at a Glance

## 1. Overview
Welcome to the internal guidelines designed to help you navigate your daily schedule successfully at the Company. Understanding how our time is structured allows everyone to collaborate effectively, meet our shared goals, and maintain a healthy balance between professional duties and personal life. Time management is a cornerstone of our vibrant workplace culture. Whether you are collaborating in person or participating remotely, being aligned on our core operational hours ensures that teams across the organization can connect, brainstorm, and deliver exceptional results together. This chapter provides a clear snapshot of our standard working schedule, giving you all the essential details you need to plan your routine efficiently.

## 2. Details
To ensure seamless coordination across all departments, the Company has established a consistent structure for the working week and daily hours. The standard working week runs from Sunday to Thursday, making Friday and Saturday the designated weekend for rest and rejuvenation. Each standard working day starts at 09:00 and ends at 17:00, creating an eight-hour framework for daily productivity and teamwork. We recognize that minor disruptions can happen during morning commutes or due to unexpected daily hurdles, which is why arrivals up to 15 minutes after the 09:00 start time fall within an established grace period and are not recorded as late. Beyond the physical office, flexibility is also part of our operational model: eligible employees may work remotely up to 2 days per week, provided they have secured prior approval from their direct manager.

## 3. What employees should do
As a valued member of the team, you play an active role in upholding these scheduling standards. Plan your daily commute or morning routine so that you arrive at your workstation or log in promptly. If minor transit delays occur, remember that arrivals up to 15 minutes after the 09:00 start time are covered by the grace period, meaning they will not be logged as tardy. However, it remains your responsibility to communicate with your supervisor if significant delays arise outside this window. If you wish to take advantage of flexible arrangements, discuss your preferences with your direct manager well in advance. Since eligible employees may work remotely up to 2 days per week, ensure you coordinate your off-site days collaboratively with your team to guarantee continuous coverage and smooth project workflows.

## 4. Related policies
To ensure a comprehensive understanding of your employment conditions, this chapter should be read alongside other internal guidelines established by the Company. Our flexible arrangements and daily attendance guidelines interact directly with broader corporate governance, performance expectations, and code of conduct standards. Familiarizing yourself with these complementary documents ensures that you remain fully informed of your rights and responsibilities. If you have any questions regarding how your schedule intersects with other internal guidelines, please reach out to the human resources department for clarification and guidance.
