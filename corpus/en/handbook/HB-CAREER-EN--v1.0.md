# Employee Handbook: Career Moves and Internal Transfers

## 1. Overview
Welcome to the guidelines on career growth within the Company. We strongly believe in supporting your professional journey and helping you discover new horizons right here. If you are feeling ready to take on a fresh challenge, explore a different department, or learn new skills, moving internally is a fantastic way to achieve your career aspirations. We want you to thrive and grow, and our internal mobility framework is designed to empower you to steer your own career path while continuing to contribute to our collective success.

## 2. Details
To ensure a smooth transition and maintain team stability, there are a few important timing guidelines to keep in mind. Specifically, employees may apply for an internal transfer after 12 months in their current role. This timeframe ensures that you have had enough time to master your current responsibilities, build strong relationships with your team, and deliver meaningful impact before embarking on a new adventure. Additionally, we evaluate overall progress and professional development regularly; performance reviews are held twice a year, in June and December. These evaluations provide valuable feedback and help align your career goals with the evolving needs of the organization.

## 3. What employees should do
If you are planning to make a move within the organization, proactive communication and preparation are key. First, take a close look at your employment timeline to confirm you meet the 12-month tenure requirement in your current position. Next, reflect on the feedback received during your recent performance reviews held in June and December to identify your strengths and areas for growth. Have an open and honest conversation with your current manager about your career ambitions before submitting any formal applications. When a suitable internal opportunity arises, prepare your application materials thoughtfully, highlighting how your skills and dedication align with the new role. Finally, coordinate with your leadership team to ensure a seamless handover of your duties should your application be successful.

## 4. Related policies
Navigating your career path successfully involves keeping familiar with several complementary guidelines. Be sure to review our general talent acquisition guidelines, performance management framework, and professional development guidelines to get a holistic view of how internal mobility integrates into our broader workplace culture. These complementary resources provide further insights into compensation adjustments, onboarding expectations for new internal roles, and continuous learning opportunities available to everyone across the organization.
