# Employee Handbook: Employee Referrals

## 1. Overview
Welcome to the employee referral guide at the Company! We truly value the insights and networks of our team members. Because your daily experiences shape our culture, you are uniquely positioned to identify talented professionals who would thrive alongside us. This chapter outlines how we reward you for helping us grow through your personal and professional connections. When you bring great talent to our attention, you directly contribute to our collective success and help us maintain our high standards of excellence. 

## 2. Details
To ensure fairness and transparency, our referral program has clear milestones. A referral reward is granted when the referred candidate completes probation. Since new employees serve a probation period of 3 months, the reward is processed once this initial evaluation phase is successfully concluded. This ensures that the individual has fully integrated into their role, demonstrated their capabilities, and proven to be a great cultural and operational fit for the Company. Please note that referrals submitted after a candidate has already applied through our public career portal do not qualify for the reward, as our systems track original application sources closely.

## 3. What employees should do
Participating in this program is simple and rewarding. First, speak with your professional contact to confirm their interest in joining the Company before submitting their details. Next, use our internal submission channels to log the referral with all required candidate information. Once your contact is hired, they will begin their standard 3 months of evaluation. During this time, you can offer informal support to help them settle in, though their performance and evaluation remain strictly between them and their manager. After they successfully finish this period, the designated referral reward will be processed and awarded to you automatically through our standard payroll or recognition channels. 

## 4. Related policies
Our referral program works hand in hand with several other workplace guidelines to ensure a smooth hiring process. This chapter aligns closely with our equal opportunity recruitment standards, ensuring that all candidate evaluations are based solely on merit and qualifications. Additionally, internal hiring guidelines and code of conduct policies apply to ensure there are no conflicts of interest when employees recommend former colleagues, friends, or family members. We encourage you to review these supplementary guidelines to ensure a transparent, fair, and successful referral experience for everyone involved.
