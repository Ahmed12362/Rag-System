# Employee Handbook: Leaving the Company

## 1. Overview
Parting ways is a natural part of any professional journey, and the Company aims to ensure every transition is as smooth and respectful as possible for all parties involved. Whether you are moving on to new adventures, pursuing personal goals, or embarking on a different career path, understanding how departures work helps maintain a positive relationship with the Company. This chapter of the employee handbook is designed to guide you through the entire exit process, outlining what you can expect and what is expected of you when you decide to leave the organization. By clarifying these steps early on, the Company ensures that your departure remains transparent, structured, and entirely stress-free.

## 2. Details
When you decide to transition out of the organization, certain administrative and operational steps must take place to finalize your departure. Specifically, when resigning, employees must give 30 calendar days of written notice. This timeframe allows the Company enough breathing room to manage knowledge transfer, adjust team workloads, and begin searching for a replacement. Furthermore, Company equipment must be returned and exit clearance completed by the last working day. This ensures that all corporate assets, digital access, and physical belongings are properly accounted for. Following the successful completion of your clearance, your final settlement is paid within 3 weeks of clearance, giving you timely access to your dues without unnecessary delays.

## 3. What employees should do
To ensure a seamless exit, you need to take proactive steps as soon as you make the decision to resign. First, prepare a formal resignation letter and submit it to your manager and human resources, ensuring you properly calculate and respect the 30 calendar days of written notice. Next, work closely with your team to hand over ongoing projects, document your daily tasks, and organize your files. As your final day approaches, gather all laptops, access cards, and other company-owned items. Company equipment must be returned and exit clearance completed by the last working day. Completing these forms and handovers promptly prevents any hold-ups in your final payout, ensuring that your final settlement is paid within 3 weeks of clearance.

## 4. Related policies
Leaving the organization intersects with several other internal guidelines that govern your employment. These include policies on corporate property usage, data confidentiality, final payroll processing, and IT security protocols regarding the surrender of digital assets. Adhering to the timeline where Company equipment must be returned and exit clearance completed by the last working day aligns closely with our asset management rules. Similarly, the timeline ensuring your final settlement is paid within 3 weeks of clearance complies with standard financial processing standards at the Company. Reviewing these complementary chapters in the handbook will help you fully understand your rights and obligations during your final days with us.
