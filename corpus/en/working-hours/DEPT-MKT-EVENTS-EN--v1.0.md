# Marketing Weekend Events Compensation

## 1. Purpose
The primary objective of this internal guideline is to establish a fair and consistent process for recognizing the hard work and dedication of staff who give up their rest days to support promotional activities. When organizational operations require team members to sacrifice their personal time, the Company ensures they receive appropriate time off to maintain a healthy work-life balance and avoid burnout. This policy outlines how time off is earned and regulated following weekend assignments, ensuring transparency and accountability across all departmental scheduling.

## 2. Applies to
This document specifically governs personnel working within the Marketing department. It does not apply to any other business unit, division, or corporate function within the Company. Marketing staff at all seniority levels who are assigned to support promotional functions, exhibitions, or public relations gatherings on their scheduled days of rest are covered under these guidelines.

## 3. Department rule
Under this department-specific framework, eligible team members who perform duties at a promotional gathering during a weekend are granted 1 compensatory day off per event day worked. To ensure proper operational planning and personal recovery, the earned rest must be utilized within 30 days from the date the weekend duty was completed. Employees are responsible for coordinating their requested absence with their direct supervisor in advance to guarantee adequate coverage. In edge cases where multiple assignments occur consecutively, the time off accrues per day worked, but the accumulation does not extend the original usage window from the earliest earned date. Unused time off remaining after the expiration period will automatically lapse, as the Company encourages timely rest rather than indefinite accumulation.

## 4. Rationale
Executing promotional functions often requires intense physical presence and long hours outside of the standard Monday through Friday schedule. The Company deeply values the flexibility and commitment demonstrated by the Marketing team during these high-pressure assignments. Offering restorative time off is a vital mechanism to show appreciation, sustain morale, and safeguard the physical and mental well-being of the personnel. Providing a clear structure prevents ambiguity, ensures equitable treatment for all contributors, and aligns departmental practices with broader human resources standards.

## 5. Escalation
Should any disputes, scheduling conflicts, or questions arise regarding the accrual or usage of the compensatory time, staff members must first raise the matter with their immediate supervisor. If a resolution cannot be reached at the direct supervisory level, the issue may be formally brought to the human resources department for review. The human resources team will assess the situation against departmental records and corporate guidelines to render a final decision, ensuring that all parties are treated fairly and that the operations of the Company continue to run smoothly.
