# Requesting Overtime Approval

## 1. Purpose
This internal procedure outlines the official guidelines for requesting and securing authorization to work extra hours at the Company. Ensuring proper oversight helps manage departmental workflows effectively while maintaining compliance with internal standards. Employees must understand that working beyond standard shift hours is not permitted without explicit prior authorization. The Company enforces this policy to ensure operational transparency, maintain accurate records of extra hours worked, and manage compensation fairly. Any extra time put in by personnel must be strictly necessary to complete urgent tasks that cannot wait until the next working day. Without the proper authorization process, the Company cannot track or validate extra hours, which directly impacts compensation eligibility. Therefore, adhering to this established procedure protects both the interests of the workforce and the operational integrity of the Company.

## 2. Who this applies to
This directive applies to all active personnel across every department within the Company who may occasionally need to extend their workday to handle pressing responsibilities. Because standard operations conclude at 17:00, any tasks requiring attention past this designated hour fall under this procedure. Both full-time employees and relevant staff members must follow these guidelines whenever their daily duties require them to stay past the normal departure time. Direct managers are also bound by this procedure, as they are responsible for reviewing, evaluating, and either approving or denying submissions from their team members. It is the responsibility of every staff member to familiarize themselves with these operational guidelines to ensure their extra efforts are officially recognized and properly authorized by management within the Company.

## 3. Steps
To secure authorization for extra working hours, personnel must follow a specific sequence of actions within the internal digital system. First, the employee must identify the need to stay past the standard departure time of 17:00 to complete urgent tasks. Next, the employee is required to submit a formal request directly through the portal before commencing any additional work. This submission must detail the specific tasks that need attention and the estimated duration required to finish them. Following this submission, the direct manager receives the notification and must review the details provided by the employee. The manager evaluates the operational necessity of the request to ensure it aligns with departmental goals and does not exceed the limit of 10 hours per week. Once the manager evaluates the submission, they must grant prior written approval through the portal before any extra work begins. Employees must remember that unapproved overtime is not compensated under any circumstances by the Company. In edge cases where operational emergencies arise unexpectedly, staff must still make every effort to secure this digital authorization as quickly as possible, or at the absolute minimum, inform their direct manager immediately to regularize the request through the proper channels.

## 4. Timelines
Managing schedules efficiently requires strict adherence to timeframes established by the Company. The standard working day concludes precisely at 17:00, marking the transition from regular operational hours to potential extra work periods. Employees must submit their requests through the portal well in advance to give direct managers sufficient time to review and grant prior written approval before the extra work is scheduled to begin. Furthermore, when management reviews the weekly volume of extra hours requested by any individual, they must ensure that approved overtime may not exceed 10 hours per week under any circumstances. If an employee approaches this weekly ceiling, any subsequent requests for that weekly period must be declined by the direct manager to remain compliant with internal limits. Adhering to these temporal boundaries ensures that the Company maintains a healthy balance between productivity and employee well-being, preventing burnout while accurately accounting for all authorized extra efforts.

## 5. Contacts
For any questions, technical difficulties with the portal, or further clarification regarding this procedure, personnel should reach out to the appropriate internal departments within the Company. Employees experiencing issues while submitting their requests should contact the human resources department or the internal IT support team for assistance. Direct managers are also available to provide guidance on how to properly estimate tasks and manage weekly workloads to stay within the permitted limits. Maintaining open communication ensures that every member of the workforce can navigate the authorization process smoothly and efficiently without facing unnecessary administrative delays.
