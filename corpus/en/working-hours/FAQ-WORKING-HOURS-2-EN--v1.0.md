# Working Hours FAQ - Part 2

## 1. How are weekly hours structured at the Company?
To maintain operational consistency and support business needs, the Company establishes a standard workload for all personnel. Understanding this baseline helps teams plan their schedules effectively. The standard working week is 40 hours. This total serves as the core foundation for measuring regular attendance, managing daily tasks, and calculating any additional time worked beyond the standard schedule. Employees are expected to manage their responsibilities efficiently within this defined period, ensuring that all departmental goals are fully met without unnecessary schedule extensions.

## 2. What schedule applies during the holy month of Ramadan?
Special timing adjustments take effect during the holy month of Ramadan to accommodate the personal and spiritual needs of the workforce while maintaining continuous business operations. During Ramadan the working day runs from 10:00 to 16:00 (6 hours). This reduced schedule applies across all standard departments within the Company. Personnel must adjust their daily routines and meetings to fit within this compressed timeframe. Managers are responsible for ensuring that critical tasks are prioritized so that productivity remains high despite the shorter daily presence in the office or on the system.

## 3. What is the policy regarding extra work hours?
Working beyond the established schedule is sometimes necessary to address urgent projects or unexpected operational demands. However, such efforts require strict adherence to internal protocols. Overtime requires prior written approval from the direct manager through the portal; unapproved overtime is not compensated. Employees must submit their requests in advance, detailing the specific tasks that necessitate the extra time. Engaging in additional work without official clearance means the time will not be recognized for compensation, safeguarding the Company against unauthorized schedule extensions.

## 4. Are there any limits on extra work hours?
To protect employee well-being and maintain a healthy balance, the Company enforces strict boundaries on how much additional time any individual can log. Approved overtime may not exceed 10 hours per week. This ceiling applies universally, ensuring that no worker experiences exhaustion or burnout from excessive scheduling. Direct managers must monitor the extra hours logged by their team members carefully. If a project requires more time than this maximum cap allows, management must reallocate resources or postpone non-urgent deliverables rather than asking an individual to exceed the weekly limit.

## 5. How does the portal submission process work for extra hours?
The digital portal acts as the central mechanism for requesting and tracking any time worked outside the standard schedule. Personnel must submit their requests through this system before starting the extra tasks. The direct manager reviews the submission within the portal and provides written approval or rejection based on operational necessity and the weekly limits set by the Company. Relying on verbal agreements or informal messaging is strictly prohibited. This digital paper trail ensures complete transparency, accurate record-keeping, and fair compensation for all validated extra hours.

## 6. What happens if extra work is performed without manager authorization?
Adhering to the formal approval chain is mandatory for all personnel seeking recognition for additional working hours. Unapproved overtime is not compensated under any circumstances, even if the completed tasks benefited the team or met a critical deadline. If an employee chooses to work late without submitting a request through the portal and receiving a positive response from their direct manager, the Company bears no financial or compensatory obligation for that time. Workers are therefore strongly advised to verify their authorization status before committing to any extended shifts.
