# Operations Shift Handover

## 1. Purpose
The primary objective of this document is to establish a standardized and reliable procedure for transferring operational responsibilities between incoming and outgoing staff at the Company. Effective communication during shift transitions ensures operational continuity, minimizes the risk of miscommunication, and prevents disruptions in daily workflows. By defining clear expectations for how information is shared, the Company helps team members prepare adequately for their duties, maintain situational awareness, and uphold high service standards across all operational areas.

## 2. Applies to
This policy applies exclusively to the Operations department within the Company. All personnel working within this specific unit must follow the outlined procedures during every shift transition, without exception. Other departments within the Company are not subject to these exact operational guidelines, ensuring that this framework remains tailored to the unique workflow demands and safety requirements of the Operations team.

## 3. Department rule
All staff members within the unit must ensure that operations shift handovers take 15 minutes and are recorded in the handover log. This designated time window allows outgoing employees to brief incoming colleagues on ongoing tasks, pending issues, and critical updates. Both parties must actively participate in this dialogue to clarify any ambiguities. Once the briefing concludes, the designated staff member must document the details of the exchange in the handover log, creating a transparent historical record that supervisors and team leads can review if necessary.

## 4. Rationale
The structured approach mandated by the Company ensures that critical information is never lost between shift changes. Allocating a specific timeframe for communication helps prevent rushed handovers that could lead to costly oversights or operational bottlenecks. Furthermore, maintaining written accountability through the handover log fosters a culture of responsibility, enabling management to trace the status of various tasks and resolve any operational discrepancies efficiently.

## 5. Escalation
If team members encounter unresolved issues, missing equipment, or critical system failures during the transition process, they must immediately escalate the matter to their direct supervisor. Should a dispute arise regarding the accuracy of the recorded details in the handover log, the involved parties must notify departmental management promptly. Management will review the documented entries and intervene to resolve the issue, ensuring that normal operations within the Company proceed safely and without unnecessary delay.
