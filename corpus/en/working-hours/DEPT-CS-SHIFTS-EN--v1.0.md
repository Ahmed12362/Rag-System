# Customer Support Shift Schedule

## 1. Purpose
This document establishes the operational hours and daily attendance framework for the Company. It ensures seamless service delivery by maintaining consistent coverage throughout the operational day while providing a structured approach to employee scheduling and workload management within the organization.

## 2. Applies to
This policy is designed exclusively for the Customer Support department (دعم العملاء). No other division or business unit within the Company is governed by these specific shift rules. Team members operating outside this specific unit must consult their respective departmental guidelines for their applicable working hours.

## 3. Department rule
Staff members within the designated unit follow two distinct shifts, operating either from 08:00 to 16:00 or from 12:00 to 20:00. These schedules are rotated on a weekly rota basis to ensure fair distribution among all agents. Additionally, the standard working day across the broader organizational framework starts at 09:00, which serves as the general anchor point for administrative alignment. Supervisors must ensure that all roster slots are adequately filled during every operational window, handling any potential overlaps or coverage gaps proactively according to team requirements.

## 4. Rationale
The structured timetable balances client needs with staff well-being, guaranteeing robust coverage during peak inquiry hours. By utilizing a rotating system, the Company prevents burnout associated with fixed schedules and maintains high morale among support representatives. The alignment with the baseline start time of 09:00 also facilitates cross-departmental communication, allowing support agents to collaborate effectively with other teams that begin their day at that hour. Edge cases, such as individual availability conflicts or sudden staffing shortages, are managed by leadership through careful adherence to the established rota structure.

## 5. Escalation
Should operational disputes, scheduling conflicts, or persistent coverage issues arise, employees must follow the formal reporting chain within the Company. Agents should first discuss any roster challenges with their direct supervisor. If a resolution cannot be reached at the immediate supervisory level, the matter is escalated to department management for further review and final determination. All adjustments to the weekly rota must be formally approved through this internal management hierarchy to maintain operational continuity and fairness.
