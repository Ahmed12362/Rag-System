# IT Weekend Support Rota

## 1. Purpose
The primary aim of this internal policy is to establish clear operational guidelines for handling critical technical emergencies outside of standard working hours. The Company relies heavily on its digital infrastructure to maintain business continuity, meaning that unexpected disruptions require immediate intervention. Through this framework, the organization ensures that system failures are addressed promptly, minimizing downtime and maintaining high availability for essential services. This document outlines the structured approach that team members must follow when managing after-hours responsibilities, ensuring transparency, accountability, and fairness across all participating personnel during high-stress situations.

## 2. Applies to
This policy is strictly limited in scope and applies exclusively to the IT department (تقنية المعلومات). No other business units, divisions, or operational groups within the Company are covered by these specific guidelines. Personnel outside of this department operate under separate protocols regarding weekend availability, on-call duties, and extra working hours compensation. Within the IT department (تقنية المعلومات), every staff member participating in the coverage schedule must adhere to these directives without exception.

## 3. Department rule
To guarantee continuous oversight, the IT support keeps a weekend rota for critical system outages, ensuring that qualified professionals are always accessible when major technical failures occur. Managing these severe disruptions often demands extended hours beyond the standard weekly schedule. Consequently, overtime requires prior written approval from the direct manager through the portal; unapproved overtime is not compensated. Staff members must secure this formal authorization before initiating any extra work to ensure their time is officially logged and eligible for remuneration. Any after-hours troubleshooting or emergency response undertaken without this preliminary digital sign-off will remain uncompensated by the Company.

## 4. Rationale
Establishing a formal weekend schedule and strict approval mechanisms protects both the organization and its workforce. Without a structured rotation, critical system outages might go unnoticed or unaddressed, causing severe operational bottlenecks and financial loss for the Company. Furthermore, requiring formal managerial sign-off ensures proper resource allocation and prevents unauthorized scheduling discrepancies. It maintains financial control while respecting employee work-life balance, ensuring that extra efforts are duly recognized and authorized through the appropriate digital channels rather than through informal agreements.

## 5. Escalation
When severe technical problems arise during the scheduled coverage period, staff must follow a defined chain of command to resolve the issue efficiently. If an initial responder encounters a complex failure that exceeds their immediate troubleshooting capacity, they must escalate the matter to senior technical leads or department supervisors. Every escalation must be documented through official communication channels to maintain an accurate audit trail of the incident response. Adhering to this hierarchical progression guarantees that critical system outages receive the appropriate level of technical expertise and managerial oversight as quickly as possible.
