# Working Hours and Overtime Policy

## 1. Purpose
The purpose of this internal document is to establish clear guidelines regarding operational hours, rest periods, and extra work management within the Company. Establishing a structured framework ensures that all personnel maintain a healthy balance between professional duties and personal life while supporting the operational requirements of the Company. This policy provides transparency on expected attendance, meal intervals, and the precise process required for managing additional working hours. Adherence to these operational guidelines helps the Company maintain productivity, fairness, and compliance across all departments.

## 2. Scope
This document applies to all staff members, contractors, and personnel engaged by the Company, regardless of their department or hierarchical level. It governs all regular attendance requirements, standard weekly expectations, scheduled meal intervals, and exceptional scheduling conditions such as holy month observances. Every team member must understand their individual obligations concerning time management and coordinate closely with their leadership team to ensure smooth daily operations and uninterrupted workflow across the entire organization.

## 3. Policy
To maintain consistent operational standards, the daily schedule at the Company is structured carefully. Specifically, the working day starts at 09:00 and the working day ends at 17:00. Under normal circumstances, the standard working day is 8 hours, including a 30-minute break. Across the standard weekly schedule, personnel fulfill commitments totaling the standard working week is 40 hours. Regarding rest periods, employees are entitled to a paid break of 30 minutes per day, usually taken between 12:00 and 14:00. This meal interval allows personnel to rest and recharge during the middle of the daily shift. During special religious periods, specifically during Ramadan the working day runs from 10:00 to 16:00 (6 hours), accommodating the needs of staff while sustaining business continuity. 

When operational demands require personnel to perform duties beyond their standard schedules, strict protocols apply. Overtime requires prior written approval from the direct manager through the portal; unapproved overtime is not compensated. To protect the well-being of staff and prevent exhaustion, approved overtime may not exceed 10 hours per week. Any additional hours worked outside this boundary without prior authorization will not be recognized or remunerated by the Company.

## 4. Responsibilities
Managing attendance and extra hours is a shared duty between personnel and leadership within the Company. Employees are responsible for logging their attendance accurately, respecting schedule boundaries, and submitting requests for extra hours well in advance through the designated portal. Managers are tasked with monitoring team workloads, evaluating requests for additional hours, and ensuring that no individual exceeds weekly thresholds. Furthermore, leadership must review portal submissions promptly and verify that business justification exists before granting formal authorization for extra duties.

## 5. Exceptions
While the standard schedule applies universally, certain operational emergencies or specialized roles may encounter edge cases. However, no exception supersedes the mandatory requirement for prior written managerial authorization via the portal for extra hours. Even under urgent operational pressures, the maximum threshold for additional hours remains strictly capped, meaning that personnel cannot exceed weekly limits regardless of project urgency. Any unique scheduling arrangements must be formally documented and approved by senior management to ensure alignment with Company governance.

## 6. Related documents
To ensure comprehensive understanding of workplace expectations, personnel should review complementary guidelines issued by the Company. These include the attendance handbook, the portal user manual for scheduling requests, and departmental standard operating procedures. Consulting these related resources ensures complete alignment with all internal governance frameworks established by the Company.
