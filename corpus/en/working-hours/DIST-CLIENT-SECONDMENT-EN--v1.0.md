# Working Calendar for Consultants Seconded to Clients

## 1. Purpose
The primary objective of this formal internal human resources document is to establish a clear, standardized guideline regarding schedule management for personnel who are temporarily assigned to work on external client premises. In the daily operations of the organization, maintaining alignment between deployed staff and the operational hours of host organizations is critical for seamless project delivery, client satisfaction, and professional accountability. When professionals are assigned away from internal offices to client sites, operational discrepancies can easily arise if attendance frameworks are not explicitly defined. This document ensures that all relevant personnel understand their scheduling obligations while deployed externally. It bridges the gap between internal operational standards and external client expectations, mitigating confusion regarding attendance, availability, and observance of non-working days. By establishing these guidelines, the organization ensures that deployed personnel integrate smoothly into the client's operational environment, fostering trust and operational efficiency without causing disruptions to the core organizational administrative structure. Furthermore, this policy sets expectations for operational alignment, ensuring that project milestones are met in accordance with the host's business rhythm and calendar constraints.

## 2. Scope
This operational policy applies specifically to a distinct group of professionals within the organization: consultants who are seconded full-time to a client site. It is vital to note that this document does not change the rules, scheduling frameworks, or holiday observances for regular employees who operate out of internal offices or remote arrangements not classified as full-time client secondments. The scope is strictly limited to full-time on-site secondments, meaning that part-time assignments, advisory roles with intermittent site visits, and standard internal staff members are entirely excluded from the provisions outlined herein. For the defined group of seconded individuals, these guidelines govern their daily attendance expectations for the entire duration of their assignment at the external location. Team leaders, project managers, and human resources personnel must review this scope carefully to ensure that it is applied exclusively to the intended cohort, preventing misinterpretation or improper application of these rules to internal staff members who maintain different scheduling requirements.

## 3. Policy
The core directive governing this operational framework is straightforward: consultants seconded full-time to a client site follow the client's working calendar and public holidays for the duration of the secondment. This means that if the host organization observes a specific working week structure, specific operational hours, or particular public holiday closures that differ from standard internal corporate observances, the deployed professional must adopt those exact schedules. For example, if the host organization recognizes a local public holiday that the organization does not observe internally, the seconded professional is not required to report to the client site on that day. Conversely, if the host organization operates on a day that is a corporate holiday internally, the professional must follow the host's calendar and fulfill their deployment obligations accordingly, provided that appropriate coordination occurs. This alignment ensures that the professional remains fully accessible to the client team, participates in all mandatory client meetings, and matches the operational tempo of the host environment. The policy remains in active effect for the entire duration of the secondment, meaning any changes to the host's schedule during the placement period are automatically mirrored in the professional's working schedule.

## 4. Responsibilities
Managing the practical application of this scheduling policy involves shared duties across multiple roles within the organization. The seconded professional is personally responsible for familiarizing themselves with the host organization's scheduling framework, noting all relevant public holidays, and adjusting their attendance accordingly for the duration of the placement. Project managers and supervisors overseeing these deployments must ensure that clear communication channels are established so that any discrepancies between internal corporate calendars and external host calendars are identified early. Human resources personnel bear the responsibility of archiving deployment records, verifying that the policy is applied exclusively to the designated cohort of full-time seconded staff, and providing guidance when edge cases or scheduling conflicts arise between the host organization and the professional. Additionally, administrative teams must ensure that time-tracking systems accommodate the specific working calendars of host sites, allowing seconded personnel to log their hours accurately without triggering automated compliance alerts for working on days that differ from the standard internal calendar.

## 5. Exceptions
Given the diverse nature of client engagements, occasional edge cases may arise where strict adherence to a host organization's calendar presents operational challenges. For instance, if a client site closes for an extended seasonal break that exceeds standard operational norms, the professional and their project manager must consult with human resources to determine appropriate alternative activities or remote tasks. However, such edge cases do not alter the foundational rule: consultants seconded full-time to a client site follow the client's working calendar and public holidays for the duration of the secondment. Any temporary deviation from the host's schedule—such as needing to attend internal corporate training sessions or mandatory internal company meetings—must be coordinated well in advance with the client supervisor to ensure minimal disruption to external deliverables. Under no circumstances may regular employees use these exceptions to modify their own schedules, as this policy applies exclusively to the defined group of seconded personnel. All exception requests must be formally documented and approved by the appropriate departmental leads to maintain transparency and compliance with contractual obligations.

## 6. Related documents
To ensure comprehensive understanding and governance, this document should be reviewed in conjunction with other relevant internal HR resources, operational guidelines, and deployment agreements. These related materials include general deployment contracts, remote work guidelines for internal staff, time-tracking procedures, and project management handbooks. While those documents provide broader context on employment standards, benefits, and general workplace conduct, this policy remains the definitive reference for scheduling and holiday observances specific to full-time seconded placements. Personnel are encouraged to consult internal portals for the most up-to-date supplementary materials, ensuring that all aspects of their professional deployment remain fully aligned with organizational standards and client expectations.
