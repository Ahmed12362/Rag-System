# Salary Bands and Compensation Guidelines

## 1. Purpose
This formal company policy, designated as Version 1.0 and effective 2026-01-01, is classified as strictly confidential and is intended exclusively for Human Resources and management personnel within the Company. The primary objective of this document is to establish a structured, transparent, and equitable framework for employee compensation across all departments. By setting clear financial boundaries and review mechanisms, the Company ensures internal pay equity, attracts top-tier talent, and maintains long-term financial sustainability. This framework guides decision-makers in hiring, promotions, and annual salary adjustments while eliminating arbitrary compensation decisions. Human Resources utilizes these guidelines to benchmark roles accurately, ensuring that every position aligns with the strategic financial goals of the organization without compromising on fairness or market competitiveness. Managers must familiarize themselves with these principles to conduct transparent compensation discussions with their teams while upholding strict confidentiality regarding all payroll matters.

## 2. Scope
The provisions outlined in this policy apply to all active employees across every division and operational unit within the Company. This encompasses full-time personnel at all organizational levels, as well as part-time workers whose compensation calculations are specifically governed by distinct proportional adjustments. Temporary staff, contractors, and external consultants operating under separate service agreements fall outside the direct parameters of these standard salary bands, unless explicitly stated otherwise in their individual contracts. Human Resources oversees the application of these guidelines across all regions and branches, ensuring that every designation adheres to the defined financial parameters. Management personnel at all levels must adhere strictly to these operational boundaries when structuring job offers, planning departmental budgets, or submitting salary modification requests.

## 3. Policy
To maintain structured financial progression, the Company implements defined monthly salary bands measured in EGP for all internal tiers. For entry-level developmental roles, the designated intern range spans from 5,000 to 8,000. For general professional positions, the staff bracket ranges from 12,000 to 35,000. For supervisory roles guiding operational units, the team lead tier is set between 35,000 and 55,000. For senior executives overseeing major business divisions, the department head bracket extends from 60,000 to 90,000. In scenarios involving flexible working arrangements, part-time salaries are calculated precisely at 60 percent of the corresponding full-time band across all respective tiers. Regarding career progression and financial adjustments, salary reviews take effect each January and are strictly based on the December performance review. Edge cases involving mid-year hires or structural job changes require careful evaluation by Human Resources to ensure alignment with the overarching annual review cycle, preventing off-cycle discrepancies unless authorized through formal governance channels.

## 4. Responsibilities
Execution and oversight of this compensation policy require active collaboration between Human Resources, departmental managers, and executive leadership within the Company. Human Resources is primarily responsible for maintaining the accuracy of the salary bands, auditing payroll allocations, and providing guidance on complex compensation queries. Management personnel are tasked with evaluating their team members fairly during the designated assessment period, ensuring that performance metrics accurately justify any proposed adjustments for the upcoming cycle. Department heads must operate strictly within their allocated financial limits when planning promotions or adjustments within the established bands. Furthermore, every stakeholder involved in the compensation process must safeguard the confidentiality of salary information, preventing unauthorized disclosures that could disrupt workplace morale or violate internal governance standards.

## 5. Exceptions
Any deviation from the established compensation framework or the standard review schedule requires formal authorization. Requests for exceptions typically arise from exceptional market conditions, critical skill shortages, or unique retention scenarios that threaten the operational continuity of the Company. Under such circumstances, departmental managers must submit a comprehensive written justification to Human Resources and senior executive leadership for rigorous evaluation. Approval is never guaranteed and is granted only on a case-by-case basis to protect organizational integrity. Under no circumstances may a manager independently alter a salary band or grant an off-cycle adjustment outside the regulated January timeline without prior written clearance from the authorized governing body.

## 6. Related documents
To ensure comprehensive understanding and operational alignment, this policy should be read in conjunction with other internal guidelines maintained by the Company. Relevant materials include the comprehensive employee handbook, the annual performance evaluation guidelines, the recruitment and onboarding protocol, and the internal promotion framework. Management and Human Resources personnel must reference these supplementary documents to maintain compliance with all internal governance standards and administrative procedures governing compensation and workforce management.
