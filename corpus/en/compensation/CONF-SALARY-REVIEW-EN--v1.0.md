# Annual Salary Review Cycle

Confidential. For Human Resources and management only.

## 1. Purpose

The primary objective of this formal company policy is to establish a structured, transparent, and equitable framework for evaluating employee compensation within the Company. Ensuring fair remuneration is vital for recognizing employee contributions, maintaining high levels of motivation, and supporting organizational growth. This document outlines the mechanisms governing how compensation adjustments are synchronized with formal evaluations. Through this process, the Company aims to reward performance consistently and maintain internal equity across all departments. Human Resources and management utilize these guidelines to administer compensation changes in a standardized manner, ensuring that every employee understands the connection between their professional achievements and financial progression.

## 2. Scope

This policy applies universally across the Company, covering all eligible personnel regardless of department, seniority, or geographic location. Management and Human Resources staff are directly bound by these rules when planning, reviewing, and approving compensation adjustments. While the framework is designed to encompass the entire workforce, certain employment categories or specific contractual arrangements may operate under distinct guidelines as determined by executive leadership. Nevertheless, the overarching principles of performance-based compensation remain applicable to all standard operational units within the enterprise, ensuring that every worker has an equal opportunity to be considered for remuneration updates based on their evaluated output.

## 3. Policy

Compensation adjustments within the Company are strictly tied to structured evaluations of employee performance. To ensure that remuneration accurately reflects professional contributions, performance reviews are held twice a year, specifically in June and December. These evaluations provide a comprehensive assessment of achievements, milestones, and areas for development over the review period. Following the conclusion of the second assessment period, the outcomes are compiled to determine adjustments. Specifically, salary reviews take effect each January and are directly based on the December performance review. This timing ensures that the financial rewards align seamlessly with the most recent and comprehensive evaluation of the employee's yearly output. Managers must prepare their assessments diligently so that Human Resources can process the resulting remuneration changes accurately and on schedule for the January implementation.

## 4. Responsibilities

Maintaining the integrity of the compensation review cycle requires clear division of duties between management and Human Resources. Management personnel are responsible for conducting thorough evaluations during the June and December review periods, providing constructive feedback, and submitting accurate documentation to Human Resources. Supervisors must ensure that their assessments are objective, transparent, and reflective of actual performance standards established by the Company. Human Resources is tasked with overseeing the entire cycle, ensuring compliance with this policy, and coordinating the implementation of salary adjustments that take effect each January based on the December performance review. Additionally, Human Resources provides guidance to managers on evaluation criteria, resolves discrepancies, and maintains confidential records of all review outcomes across the organization.

## 5. Exceptions

While the compensation review cycle is designed to apply universally, exceptional circumstances may occasionally arise that require deviation from standard procedures. Edge cases such as mid-year promotions, substantial changes in job responsibilities, or retention requirements for critical talent may necessitate out-of-cycle adjustments. Any departure from the standard schedule, which dictates that salary reviews take effect each January and are based on the December performance review, requires explicit written approval from senior management and Human Resources. Such exceptions are strictly monitored to prevent inequities and are granted only when business necessity clearly justifies bypassing the regular semi-annual evaluation framework. Under no circumstances may individual managers authorize compensation changes independently of Human Resources oversight.

## 6. Related documents

To ensure comprehensive governance, this policy operates in conjunction with other internal guidelines maintained by the Company. Relevant documents include the performance management handbook, employee evaluation forms utilized during the June and December assessment periods, and general compensation guidelines. Management and Human Resources personnel should consult these supplementary materials to fully understand the operational procedures supporting the rule that salary reviews take effect each January and are based on the December performance review. Keeping these resources aligned guarantees that the evaluation process remains transparent, consistent, and fully supported by all associated internal documentation.
