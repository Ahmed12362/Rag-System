# Employee Handbook: Acceptable Use of IT Systems

## 1. Overview
Welcome to our internal guide on navigating the digital workspace safely and effectively. At the Company, we rely heavily on technology to collaborate, communicate, and drive our daily operations forward. Because our digital infrastructure connects everyone, maintaining a secure environment is a shared responsibility that protects both our data and our professional community. This chapter is designed to help you understand how to use our technological tools appropriately. By following these guidelines, you ensure that our network remains stable, secure, and accessible for all colleagues. We want everyone to feel confident while using company devices and networks, knowing that clear standards are in place to support safe digital habits.

## 2. Details
Our digital ecosystem requires consistent maintenance and strict security measures to prevent unauthorized access and potential breaches. One of the fundamental pillars of our security framework involves credential management. Specifically, system passwords must be changed every 90 days. This routine update minimizes the window of opportunity for any potential security compromise. Furthermore, these credentials must remain strictly confidential; they must not be shared with anyone, including colleagues, managers, or external parties. Adhering to this rule safeguards sensitive company information and preserves the integrity of our entire IT infrastructure.

## 3. What employees should do
As a member of the Company, you play a vital role in upholding our security culture. You should proactively manage your digital credentials by updating your system passwords every 90 days without exception. When creating or updating your credentials, make sure to keep them private and secure at all times. Remember that your login details are for your personal use only; they must not be shared with coworkers, even if someone asks for temporary access to complete a task. If you ever suspect that your credentials have been compromised, or if you encounter any unusual system behavior, report it to the IT department immediately. Developing these positive daily habits ensures a secure workspace for everyone.

## 4. Related policies
To maintain a comprehensive understanding of your workplace responsibilities, this chapter should be read alongside other internal guidelines. The Company maintains various protocols governing data privacy, hardware maintenance, and secure communication channels. Familiarizing yourself with these complementary resources will give you a complete picture of your obligations regarding company resources. Each document works together to create a secure, transparent, and productive environment for all personnel across the organization.
