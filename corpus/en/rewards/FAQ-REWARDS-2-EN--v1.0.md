# Rewards FAQ - Part 2

## 1. What is the limit on spot awards?
The Company values timely recognition through spot awards, but certain restrictions apply to ensure fair distribution. An employee may receive at most 3 spot awards per calendar year. This limit applies regardless of the department or the nature of the exceptional contribution. Managers should plan their recognition strategies carefully across the calendar year to acknowledge outstanding efforts without exhausting this allowance prematurely. In edge cases where a team member delivers multiple exceptional projects within the same annual cycle, supervisors must prioritize which achievements to submit for spot recognition, keeping the yearly cap in mind.

## 2. Who is eligible for the annual performance bonus?
Rewarding dedication and consistent performance is a priority for the Company, and qualifying for the annual performance bonus requires meeting specific tenure criteria. Employees must have completed 6 months of service to be eligible for the annual performance bonus. This qualifying period ensures that personnel have spent sufficient time contributing to organizational goals before bonus evaluations occur. For example, individuals who join the organization late in the evaluation cycle and have not reached the required duration of employment will not be considered for this specific payout, but they will become eligible in subsequent annual cycles once their tenure milestone is met.

## 3. How is perfect attendance recognized?
Reliability and punctuality form a cornerstone of workplace productivity at the Company, and exemplary attendance habits are formally acknowledged. Employees with no late arrivals and no unexcused absences in a calendar quarter receive a perfect attendance certificate. This recognition celebrates individuals who maintain impeccable punctuality and presence throughout every working day of the three-month period. For instance, arriving late even once or taking an unauthorized day off during that timeframe will disqualify a person from receiving the certificate for that specific quarter, though they have the opportunity to reset and qualify in the following quarter.

## 4. How do managers handle spot award nominations?
When supervisors wish to acknowledge a staff member's extraordinary effort, they must navigate the internal nomination process while keeping the annual limits in mind. Leadership is responsible for verifying that the nominee has not already reached their yearly maximum for such recognitions. If an individual has already received their maximum number of spot recognitions within the current calendar year, any further nominations for that person will not be processed. Supervisors should explore alternative methods of appreciation, such as verbal praise or professional development opportunities, when financial or spot recognition caps have been fully utilized for the year.

## 5. What happens if a bonus-eligible employee transitions roles?
Internal mobility is encouraged within the organization, but staff members must ensure they understand how job changes impact their overall rewards profile. Provided that an employee has fulfilled the necessary tenure requirement within the organization, transferring between departments does not erase their standing for yearly performance incentives. The receiving department evaluates overall yearly contributions, incorporating feedback from previous supervisors. Edge cases may arise if a transition happens during the final review stages, but as long as the initial eligibility threshold was successfully crossed, the review process moves forward seamlessly.

## 6. Can attendance certificates be appealed?
Occasionally, discrepancies arise regarding attendance records, particularly concerning unexpected transit delays or administrative logging errors. If a staff member believes their record was marked incorrectly regarding punctuality or presence, they must raise the issue with human resources promptly for review. Should the investigation reveal an administrative mistake or an authorized excused absence that was misclassified, the record is corrected, and the person may still be awarded the quarterly recognition. However, unexcused tardiness or undocumented absences remain valid disqualifiers, and the initial decision stands if the logs are accurate.
