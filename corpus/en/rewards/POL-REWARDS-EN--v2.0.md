# Rewards and Recognition Policy

## 1. Purpose
The primary objective of this internal document is to establish a structured and transparent framework for acknowledging and celebrating exceptional contributions made by personnel across the organization. The Company values dedication and high standards, and this policy ensures that diligent efforts do not go unnoticed. By implementing a comprehensive system of appreciation, the enterprise aims to boost morale, encourage consistent productivity, and foster a positive workplace culture where dedication is regularly highlighted and rewarded.

## 2. Scope
This directive applies universally to all active personnel engaged with the enterprise, regardless of their operational level or tenure, ensuring equitable access to appreciation opportunities. Every division and department within the organization is included under these guidelines. While the overarching framework covers all workers, specific eligibility criteria—such as minimum service duration or behavioral metrics—apply to certain accolades to maintain fairness and operational integrity throughout the firm.

## 3. Policy
To appropriately celebrate different levels of achievement, the enterprise provides several distinct recognition types: spot award, Employee of the Month, certificate, performance bonus, and promotion recognition. 

For the monthly accolade, each department may select one Employee of the Month based on outstanding performance and dedication during the period. To ensure timely processing, Employee of the Month nominations must be submitted by managers by the 25th day of the month preceding the announcement.

Immediate acknowledgments for exceptional daily tasks are handled through spot awards. To maintain balance and broad access across the workforce, an employee may receive at most 3 spot awards per calendar year. 

Regarding annual financial incentives, employees must have completed 6 months of service to be eligible for the annual performance bonus. This ensures that newer personnel have sufficient time to demonstrate their impact on the organization's goals before being evaluated for financial rewards.

Furthermore, punctuality and reliability are formally acknowledged. Employees with no late arrivals and no unexcused absences in a calendar quarter receive a perfect attendance certificate, highlighting the enterprise's commitment to disciplined work habits.

## 4. Responsibilities
Management personnel and supervisory staff play a crucial role in the successful execution of this framework. Department heads are tasked with identifying deserving candidates, ensuring timely submissions, and applying the established criteria objectively. Specifically, managers must observe deadlines, such as submitting monthly nominations by the required date, and monitor the frequency of immediate accolades to ensure compliance with annual caps. Employees share the responsibility of maintaining high standards of attendance and performance, remaining mindful of the qualifying thresholds for annual financial incentives and quarterly certificates. Human resources oversees the entire process, ensuring equity, consistency, and adherence to the outlined parameters across all business units.

## 5. Exceptions
Navigating unique workplace scenarios requires clear boundaries to preserve the integrity of the framework. Personnel who fall short of the minimum tenure requirement are excluded from the annual financial incentive until the threshold is met, regardless of their output. Similarly, workers who record even a single unexcused absence or tardiness during a quarter will not qualify for the quarterly attendance recognition, maintaining the absolute standard required for that specific certificate. Any deviations from the nomination deadlines or award frequencies are strictly managed by human resources to prevent favoritism and ensure that every decision aligns with the core principles of the organization.

To ensure comprehensive understanding and alignment across operations, this document should be reviewed in conjunction with other internal guidelines maintained by the enterprise.
