# Granting a Spot Award

## 1. Purpose
The purpose of this procedure is to outline the internal process used within the Company for acknowledging and rewarding exceptional contributions made by personnel. Expressing appreciation in a timely manner helps maintain high motivation, reinforces company values, and encourages excellence across all departments. This document provides a clear framework to ensure that recognition is managed fairly and transparently. Managers play a vital role in identifying outstanding efforts and initiating the acknowledgment process. By following these guidelines, the Company ensures that all staff members have equal opportunities to be recognized for their hard work and dedication. The procedures detailed here apply to various forms of acknowledgement, ensuring consistency and equity in how appreciation is distributed throughout the organization.

## 2. Who this applies to
This procedure applies to all active personnel across every department within the Company, regardless of their position, tenure, or employment status. Both full-time and part-time staff members are eligible to be nominated and considered for various recognition types. Direct supervisors, managers, and department heads are responsible for identifying qualifying behaviors and submitting the necessary paperwork. Human Resources oversees the entire process to verify that guidelines are applied consistently and fairly across all business units. Every employee within the Company should familiarize themselves with these guidelines to understand how performance is celebrated and what forms of acknowledgement are available.

## 3. Steps
Initiating and granting recognition within the Company involves a series of structured actions designed to maintain fairness and accuracy. First, the manager identifies an outstanding achievement or contribution that surpasses regular job expectations. Second, the manager selects the appropriate form of acknowledgement from the approved list of recognition types, which includes a spot award, Employee of the Month, a certificate, a performance bonus, or promotion recognition. Third, the manager prepares and submits the nomination documentation to Human Resources, detailing the specific reasons for the recognition. Fourth, Human Resources reviews the submission to ensure it complies with internal policies and verifies that the recipient has not exceeded the limit, noting that an employee may receive at most 3 spot awards per calendar year. Fifth, upon approval, the recognition is formally processed, and the acknowledgement is presented to the recipient in an appropriate setting. Finally, Human Resources updates the personnel records to reflect the granted recognition.

## 4. Timelines
Managing the schedule of recognition is essential for ensuring that appreciation remains meaningful and impactful for personnel within the Company. Nominations should be submitted promptly by managers following the completion of the exceptional work or behavior being acknowledged. Human Resources generally processes the paperwork and verifies the eligibility criteria within a reasonable timeframe after receipt. Because an employee may receive at most 3 spot awards per calendar year, tracking the frequency of these accolades is crucial to avoid scheduling conflicts or policy violations. Managers must plan their nominations strategically across the annual cycle to ensure fair distribution and to prevent clustering multiple recognitions too closely together for the same individual. Adhering to these scheduling practices helps maintain the integrity of the acknowledgement program across the entire organization.

## 5. Contacts
For any questions, guidance, or assistance regarding the recognition process, personnel within the Company can reach out to the designated support channels. The Human Resources department serves as the primary point of contact for clarifying eligibility requirements, understanding the different recognition types, or resolving submission issues. Managers seeking advice on how to properly document an exceptional contribution or how to manage the annual limit of accolades can consult their assigned HR business partner. Additionally, departmental leaders can provide internal guidance on aligning nominations with specific team goals and company values. Maintaining open communication with these contacts ensures that the recognition procedure runs smoothly, transparently, and fairly for all staff members.
