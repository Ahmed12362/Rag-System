# Rewards FAQ - Part 1

## 1. What are the available recognition types within the organization?
The Company offers multiple ways to acknowledge and appreciate staff contributions. The available recognition types are: spot award, Employee of the Month, certificate, performance bonus and promotion recognition. Employees who demonstrate exceptional dedication, leadership, or project success may be nominated for these acknowledgments. Each type serves a distinct purpose, ranging from immediate appreciation for a specific task to formal career progression milestones. Managers should review the criteria for each category before submitting any nominations on behalf of their team members to ensure proper alignment with corporate standards.

## 2. Who is eligible to receive recognition from the Company?
All active personnel across all business units are eligible for appreciation under the established programs. Whether someone is working remotely, in an office, or on a hybrid schedule, their contributions are valued equally. The Company ensures that evaluation processes remain fair, objective, and transparent. New hires, seasoned staff, and leadership team members can all be considered for rewards, provided they meet the specific performance metrics associated with each category.

## 3. How many individuals can be selected as Employee of the Month?
To maintain exclusivity and high standards of appreciation, the selection is strictly regulated per organizational unit. Each department may select one Employee of the Month. This ensures that every team has a dedicated moment to highlight an outstanding contributor who has gone above and beyond their daily responsibilities. If multiple high performers exist within a single team during the same period, managers are encouraged to consider alternative appreciation methods, such as spot awards, for the other deserving candidates.

## 4. When must Employee of the Month nominations be submitted by managers?
Timely submission of candidates is vital for the smooth operation of the evaluation cycle. Employee of the Month nominations must be submitted by managers by the 25th day of the month. Missing this deadline means the candidate cannot be considered for that specific monthly cycle and will automatically be deferred to the next period. Managers should set personal reminders to review their team's performance well in advance to avoid any last-minute administrative delays.

## 5. What happens if a manager misses the submission deadline?
Adherence to internal administrative schedules is critical for maintaining fairness across all business units. If a manager fails to submit their candidate documentation within the designated timeframe, the nomination will not be processed for the current evaluation cycle. The Company does not grant exceptions for late submissions to ensure that the evaluation committee has adequate time to review all properly filed paperwork. Managers are advised to prepare their nomination details early to account for any unexpected technical issues or personal absences.

## 6. Can an employee receive multiple types of recognition simultaneously?
Staff members who consistently exceed expectations may find themselves eligible for various forms of appreciation. While it is entirely possible for an individual to earn different types of awards across the year, overlapping the exact same award category within a single cycle is typically restricted. The Company encourages a balanced distribution of appreciation to motivate the entire workforce. Managers should consult internal guidelines to understand how different accolades interact with one another and to ensure equitable distribution of recognition across their teams.
