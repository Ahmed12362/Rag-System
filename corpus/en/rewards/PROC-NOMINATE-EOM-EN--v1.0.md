# Nominating the Employee of the Month

## 1. Purpose
The purpose of this internal directive is to establish a clear and structured framework for recognizing exceptional performance within the organization. Acknowledging staff members who consistently exceed expectations fosters a positive work environment and drives overall productivity across all business units. The Company believes that regular appreciation is vital for maintaining high morale, encouraging continuous professional development, and reinforcing core organizational values among all personnel. This procedure ensures that recognition is distributed fairly and transparently, providing every team with an equal opportunity to highlight their top performers. By implementing a standardized nomination process, the Company aims to celebrate individual achievements while inspiring peers to strive for excellence in their daily responsibilities.

## 2. Who this applies to
This internal procedure applies universally to all operational divisions, business units, and functional teams operating within the Company. Every permanent and contract staff member is eligible to be considered for the recognition program, provided they meet the performance standards set by their respective leadership. Team leads, supervisors, and department heads hold the direct responsibility for identifying eligible candidates based on merit, dedication, and notable contributions to ongoing projects. While leadership manages the submission process, the underlying philosophy of the program relies on peer feedback and observed workplace achievements. Ultimately, each department may select one Employee of the Month, ensuring that every operational branch has dedicated representation in the monthly accolades distributed by the Company.

## 3. Steps
The nomination process requires careful observation and timely action from operational leadership to ensure a seamless selection experience. Department heads must evaluate the daily performance, reliability, and collaborative spirit of their team members throughout the evaluation period. Once a top-performing individual is identified, the supervising manager must compile a brief justification outlining the candidate's specific accomplishments and contributions during the cycle. Employee of the Month nominations must be submitted by managers by the 25th day of the month using the official internal portal managed by the human resources department. In edge cases where multiple candidates display equal merit, supervisors are encouraged to consult with senior leadership to determine the final selection before the submission deadline. Late submissions cannot be processed for the current cycle and must be deferred to subsequent evaluation periods.

## 4. Timelines
Adhering to strict scheduling is essential for the smooth administration of the recognition workflow within the Company. Operational planning requires that all evaluations and administrative reviews occur in a synchronized manner to prevent delays in announcement and reward distribution. Supervisors must continuously monitor performance metrics, allowing them to prepare their recommendations well in advance of any administrative cutoffs. Because Employee of the Month nominations must be submitted by managers by the 25th day of the month, leadership must finalize their internal reviews prior to this date. Any submissions attempted after the designated deadline will not be considered valid for the active monthly cycle, ensuring that the human resources team has adequate time to verify all entries and prepare the official announcements for the Company.

## 5. Contacts
For any inquiries, clarifications, or technical difficulties regarding the submission workflow, staff members and supervisors should reach out to the appropriate internal support channels. The human resources department oversees the administration of the recognition program and remains available to assist leadership with any procedural questions or edge cases that may arise during the nomination cycle. Internal communication teams can also provide guidance on how winning candidates are announced across the organization. Supervisors are encouraged to reach out to their designated human resources representative well in advance of the deadline if they require assistance with portal access or policy interpretation, ensuring that every eligible individual within the Company receives fair consideration.
