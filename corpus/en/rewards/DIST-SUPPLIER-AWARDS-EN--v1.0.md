# Supplier Excellence Awards

## 1. Purpose
The purpose of this formal company policy document is to establish the framework for the annual supplier excellence award managed by the Company. This initiative is designed to highlight, appreciate, and celebrate the exceptional performance, reliability, and dedication demonstrated by our external partners. By formally acknowledging outstanding contributions, the organization aims to foster long-term strategic relationships with key external collaborators who consistently deliver high standards of quality and service. Furthermore, this program serves as a benchmark for operational excellence across our entire supply chain ecosystem, encouraging all external partners to continually elevate their performance standards while aligning with our overarching business objectives and strategic goals.

## 2. Scope
It is vital to clarify the boundaries and applicability of this framework to avoid any operational confusion within the organization. This document applies strictly to external vendors who supply goods and services to the Company. To ensure absolute clarity regarding internal governance, it is explicitly noted that the annual supplier excellence award recognises vendors, not employees. Consequently, internal personnel, departmental teams, and individual staff members are entirely excluded from consideration under this specific policy. Regular employees of the Company continue to be governed by separate internal human resources policies, performance management structures, and internal reward mechanisms that are entirely distinct from this external vendor recognition program.

## 3. Policy
The core policy dictates that the Company shall conduct an annual evaluation process to identify and honour top-performing external partners through the designated excellence award. Participation in this recognition scheme is open to all active external suppliers who maintain a contractual relationship with the organization during the evaluation cycle. The selection methodology relies on objective performance metrics, including delivery punctuality, product or service quality, cost competitiveness, responsiveness, and adherence to safety and ethical standards. Any external partner that meets the rigorous qualifying criteria may be nominated for the award. Edge cases, such as vendors with newly established contracts or those undergoing restructuring, are evaluated on a case-by-case basis by the oversight committee to determine their eligibility for the annual cycle.

## 4. Responsibilities
Managing the annual supplier excellence award requires a coordinated effort across multiple functional areas within the Company. Procurement teams bear the primary responsibility for tracking vendor performance data, compiling operational metrics, and submitting preliminary nominations for the award. Senior management holds the responsibility of reviewing the shortlisted candidates, applying final governance checks, and approving the ultimate award recipient. Additionally, the corporate communications department is responsible for coordinating the public announcement and formal presentation of the award to the winning vendor. External partners are responsible for maintaining accurate operational records and cooperating fully with any audits or performance reviews conducted by our internal evaluation teams throughout the year.

## 5. Exceptions
While the framework is designed to be comprehensive, certain situations may require procedural exceptions. These exceptions typically arise in scenarios involving force majeure events, sudden supply chain disruptions, or extraordinary market conditions that temporarily impact normal vendor operations. In such rare instances, the governing committee has the authority to adjust evaluation timelines or waive specific criteria to ensure fairness in the assessment process. However, no exception under any circumstances shall alter the fundamental rule that the annual supplier excellence award recognises vendors, not employees. Any deviation from the standard evaluation workflow must be formally documented, justified, and authorized by the head of procurement before being applied to any specific vendor file.

## 6. Related documents
To ensure a holistic approach to external partner management and corporate governance, this policy operates in conjunction with several other internal instruments. Relevant documents include the overarching procurement guidelines, the vendor code of conduct, supply chain quality assurance procedures, and the annual audit protocols maintained by the Company. While those documents govern day-to-day commercial interactions and compliance standards, this specific policy remains the sole authority concerning the annual supplier excellence award. All stakeholders, including internal procurement specialists and external partner representatives, are encouraged to familiarize themselves with these interconnected guidelines to ensure seamless operational alignment and compliance with corporate governance standards.
