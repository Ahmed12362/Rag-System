# Attendance FAQ - Part 2

## 1. How does the Company track daily employee attendance?
To ensure accurate timekeeping and maintain operational standards within the workplace, recording presence is a mandatory daily routine for all personnel. Employees must register their arrival and departure using the office badge reader located at the main entry points of the premises. Alternatively, on days where remote work has been officially authorized and approved by management, staff members must register their presence by checking in through the dedicated HR mobile app. This dual system guarantees that whether an individual is working on-site or off-site, their working hours are properly documented. Supervisors and team leads rely on these digital logs to monitor daily capacity, while human resources uses the records to ensure labor compliance and seamless payroll processing without discrepancies.

## 2. What is the procedure if I miss a check-in or record an incorrect time?
Mistakes and oversights can occasionally happen when logging daily hours, but the Company provides a clear mechanism to resolve such errors. If an employee forgets to register their arrival or departure, or if the system captures an inaccurate timestamp, the discrepancy must be addressed promptly. The correction process requires the staff member to submit a formal adjustment request through the designated internal portal. To maintain data integrity and prevent fraudulent entries, this submission must be completed within 2 working days of the missed or faulty punch. Furthermore, the request cannot be finalized on its own; it requires explicit manager confirmation to validate that the reported hours reflect actual time worked. Employees should communicate with their supervisors immediately if they notice any logging errors to avoid processing delays during the payroll cycle.

## 3. What are the financial and disciplinary consequences of an unexcused absence?
Failing to report to work without securing prior approval or providing a valid justification is treated as a serious breach of Company policy. When an employee takes an unexcused absence, the financial penalty is direct and uncompromising. Each day of unauthorized absence results in a monetary deduction equal to one day's pay from the individual's monthly salary. Beyond the immediate financial impact, such behavior disrupts team productivity and project timelines. Therefore, taking unauthorized time away from duties may also trigger formal disciplinary action, which could range from an official warning to more severe administrative measures depending on the frequency and context of the infractions. Managers and human resources closely track attendance patterns to address chronic absenteeism and enforce workplace accountability.

## 4. How are approved leave days handled in the attendance records?
When staff members apply for time off and receive official approval through the appropriate administrative channels, their status changes within the system. Days that are fully covered by an approved leave request are officially logged and categorized as on leave in the corporate database. Because the absence is sanctioned and accounted for in advance, employees do not need to perform any daily check-ins while they are away from their duties. This exemption prevents false error flags from appearing in the tracking software and ensures that the payroll department does not mistakenly penalize individuals who are legitimately utilizing their vacation, sick leave, or other authorized time off. Supervisors are responsible for reviewing team schedules to ensure adequate coverage while colleagues are away on approved breaks.

## 5. Why is timely correction of attendance records critical for operations?
Maintaining precise time logs is essential for the smooth functioning of the Company and ensures fairness across all departments. When employees neglect to fix missed or inaccurate check-ins within the allowed timeframe, it creates a cascade of administrative bottlenecks that affect payroll calculations and performance reviews. Timely submissions through the portal allow managers to verify operational hours while memories are fresh, reducing the likelihood of disputes during salary disbursements. Furthermore, accurate data helps the organization analyze attendance trends, optimize staffing levels, and maintain a transparent work environment where policies are applied consistently to everyone. Both employees and supervisors share the responsibility of keeping these records accurate and up to date.

## 6. What should I do if my manager is unavailable to confirm my portal correction?
Navigating administrative procedures can occasionally present challenges, particularly when key personnel are absent or unavailable to approve portal requests. If an employee submits a correction for a missed check-in within the required timeframe, but their direct supervisor is unable to provide confirmation due to leave or travel, the employee should notify the human resources department immediately. Taking this proactive step ensures that the request does not languish in the system past the designated operational window. Human resources can then intervene, verify the circumstances, and provide the necessary oversight to ensure the time log is corrected properly. Communication is key in resolving these edge cases, safeguarding the employee from unfair payroll deductions while upholding internal governance standards.
