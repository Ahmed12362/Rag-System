# Correcting a Missed Check-in

## 1. Purpose
This internal procedure outlines the standard operating protocol when an employee forgets or is unable to properly register their arrival time at the workplace. Ensuring accurate attendance records is essential for operational transparency and smooth administrative processing within the organization. Every working day officially starts at 09:00, serving as the benchmark for measuring punctuality and identifying discrepancies in the daily logs. When an oversight happens, personnel must promptly utilize the designated internal system to rectify their status. This mechanism ensures that timekeeping data remains precise and up to date across all departments.

## 2. Who this applies to
The directives contained in this manual apply to all active personnel across the organization, regardless of their department or hierarchical level. Personnel generally register their presence upon arrival using the physical office badge reader located at the building entrances. Alternatively, staff members working on approved remote days record their attendance by checking in directly through the official HR mobile app. If either of these digital or physical mechanisms fails to capture the arrival accurately, or if the individual forgets to log their presence, the remedial workflow detailed in this document becomes mandatory. Supervisors and department heads are also governed by these rules insofar as they are required to review and validate the adjustments submitted by their direct reports.

## 3. Steps
When an individual realizes their arrival was not properly captured, they must initiate a correction request through the internal portal. The employee navigates to the attendance module, selects the relevant date, enters the correct arrival time, and provides a brief justification for the omission. Once submitted, the system automatically routes the request to the designated manager for review. The manager evaluates the explanation against team schedules and remote work authorizations before approving or rejecting the adjustment. If approved, the system updates the official attendance ledger automatically. Edge cases, such as simultaneous app errors and badge reader failures, should be documented within the portal comments to assist supervisors in verifying the exact timeline of the workday.

## 4. Timelines
Time sensitivity is crucial when addressing discrepancies in attendance logs to prevent payroll errors and compliance issues. Specifically, any missed or incorrect check-in must be corrected through the portal within 2 working days, with manager confirmation. Failing to complete the submission and subsequent approval within this window may result in unexcused absence markings or delayed compensation adjustments. Managers are likewise expected to process pending requests promptly to ensure that the administrative cycle remains synchronized with the internal calendar. Employees who anticipate being away should make every effort to resolve any prior logging errors before their departure to avoid missing the mandatory completion window.

## 5. Contacts
For assistance with technical issues related to the portal, the HR mobile app, or the physical badge reader, personnel should reach out to the internal technical support helpdesk. Questions concerning policy interpretations, manager approvals, or unresolved attendance disputes should be directed to the human resources department. Providing clear details, such as the date of the incident and screenshots of any error messages, will help the support staff resolve inquiries more efficiently.
