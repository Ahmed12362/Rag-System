# Sales Field Visit Attendance

## 1. Purpose
The purpose of this document is to establish clear attendance and activity tracking procedures for field operations conducted by personnel in the Sales department. Operating outside the main office requires alternative methods to record presence and document daily professional activities effectively. This policy ensures that the Company maintains accurate operational records while supporting the flexibility required by sales professionals during their interactions with external clients. Proper attendance and activity tracking safeguard the interests of both the staff and the Company, ensuring transparency and accountability across all external engagements.

## 2. Applies to
This policy applies exclusively to the Sales department. All personnel working within this specific division must adhere to the guidelines outlined herein when conducting business outside of the corporate premises. No other departments are covered by these specialized field tracking rules, as they maintain different operational requirements tied directly to fixed office locations and standard badge-reading equipment.

## 3. Department rule
Sales staff members who are actively engaged in client visits check in through the HR mobile app at the client location instead of the badge reader. This digital check-in serves as the official attendance record for the day's field activities, replacing the traditional physical clock-in process. Furthermore, to ensure complete visibility into ongoing business pipelines and client interactions, sales personnel are strictly required to log every client visit in the CRM within 24 hours of the meeting concluding. Failing to record a visit or missing the designated reporting window constitutes a deviation from standard operational compliance. Supervisors and team leads monitor these digital submissions regularly to verify field presence and ensure CRM records remain current and reliable for future planning.

## 4. Rationale
The operational rationale behind these distinct procedures lies in the mobile nature of modern sales roles. Traditional time and attendance hardware, such as wall-mounted badge readers, cannot accommodate employees who travel directly to external client sites without first reporting to the main office. Utilizing a location-based smartphone application for attendance captures accurate geographic verification without imposing unnecessary commuting burdens on traveling staff. Concurrently, prompt documentation within the CRM protects business intelligence, allowing the Company to track customer relationship lifecycles, measure sales productivity, and evaluate field performance fairly based on objective data rather than guesswork.

## 5. Escalation
When compliance issues arise regarding attendance logs or reporting timelines, a structured escalation process is initiated by management. If an employee fails to check in properly through the HR mobile app or neglects to enter required visit data into the CRM system, their direct supervisor addresses the discrepancy during routine performance check-ins. Persistent failures to meet the designated reporting deadlines or chronic attendance verification errors are escalated to senior management and human resources for formal review. Repeated non-compliance may result in disciplinary measures, underscoring the vital importance of accurate and timely administrative record-keeping for all traveling sales representatives.
