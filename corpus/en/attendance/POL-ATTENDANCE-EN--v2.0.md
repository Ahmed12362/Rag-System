# Attendance and Lateness Policy

## 1. Purpose
The Company establishes this framework to outline expectations regarding working hours, punctuality, and attendance tracking. Maintaining reliable attendance ensures smooth daily operations, supports fair collaboration across all teams, and helps the Company deliver consistent results for clients and stakeholders. This policy provides clear guidelines so that every team member understands their daily obligations, the methods used to record working time, and the consequences associated with unexcused absences or chronic tardiness. By setting clear standards, the Company fosters a professional environment built on accountability, mutual respect, and operational excellence.

## 2. Scope
This policy applies to all personnel engaged by the Company, regardless of department, role, or employment contract type. The guidelines cover standard office-based staff as well as individuals authorized to work remotely on designated days. Every member of the organization is required to adhere to the established schedule and utilize the designated attendance tracking mechanisms outlined in this document. Adherence to these standards is essential for maintaining team alignment and ensuring business continuity across all operational units within the Company.

## 3. Policy
Working time within the Company is structured around a specific weekly schedule and daily timetable. The working week runs from Sunday to Thursday, while Friday and Saturday are designated as the weekend. Each working day starts at 09:00. 

Punctuality is expected from all personnel. To accommodate minor unforeseen delays such as traffic or transit disruptions, arrivals up to 15 minutes after the 09:00 start time fall within the grace period and are not recorded as late. However, chronic tardiness disrupts team operations. Specifically, more than 3 late arrivals in the same calendar month result in a deduction of a quarter of a day's pay for each additional late arrival exceeding that threshold. 

Regular attendance is mandatory. Each day of unexcused absence is deducted at one day's pay and may lead to disciplinary action. 

Attendance tracking varies depending on the work location. Attendance is recorded by the office badge reader or, on approved remote days, by checking in through the HR mobile app. If an individual forgets to badge in or encounters a technical glitch, a missed or incorrect check-in must be corrected through the portal within 2 working days, with manager confirmation. 

Certain situations naturally exempt personnel from daily check-in requirements. Days covered by an approved leave request are recorded as on leave and do not require a check-in. Edge cases such as system outages or approved business travel must be coordinated directly with supervisors to ensure records remain accurate.

## 4. Responsibilities
Maintaining accurate attendance records requires active cooperation between personnel, managers, and the human resources department. Personnel are personally responsible for swiping their badges, using the HR mobile app correctly on remote days, and monitoring their own punctuality to avoid exceeding the monthly tardiness limits. Furthermore, personnel must proactively submit portal requests to fix any missed or incorrect check-in entries within 2 working days, ensuring manager confirmation is secured promptly. 

Managers play a vital oversight role within the Company. They are tasked with reviewing and confirming check-in corrections submitted through the portal, monitoring team attendance patterns, and addressing unauthorized absences or frequent lateness in coordination with human resources. Human resources oversees the overall enforcement of this policy, processes payroll deductions when financial penalties apply, and provides guidance on disciplinary procedures stemming from unexcused absences.

## 5. Exceptions
While this policy applies universally across the Company, operational realities sometimes require flexibility. Situations involving approved leave requests are fully exempt from daily attendance recording, as days covered by an approved leave request are recorded as on leave and do not require a check-in. Emergency situations, severe weather events, or sudden technical failures preventing access to the office badge reader or the HR mobile app are handled on a case-by-case basis. In such exceptional edge cases, personnel must notify their supervisor immediately. Any adjustments to standard working hours or attendance expectations must be formally authorized by management to ensure payroll accuracy and compliance with Company standards.

## 6. Related documents
To ensure a comprehensive understanding of workplace expectations, personnel should review complementary internal guidelines maintained by the Company. Relevant materials include the internal leave guidelines, the remote work agreement documents, the employee handbook, and the internal disciplinary procedures framework. These documents work in tandem with this policy to provide detailed instructions on requesting time off, managing remote work arrangements, and understanding the full scope of workplace conduct standards expected by the Company.
