# Attendance Rules for Agency Staff on Company Premises

## 1. Purpose
The purpose of this internal document is to establish clear guidelines regarding the time and presence management for external personnel operating within our corporate facilities. When external contractors or temporary workers perform daily duties on our grounds, aligning their schedule tracking with operational expectations is vital for maintaining a productive environment. This policy ensures that all third-party contributors understand how their working hours are monitored while they are physically present at the offices of the Company. It also clarifies that these guidelines exist solely to govern external vendor personnel without modifying any existing internal protocols or standard operating procedures that currently apply to our permanent workforce.

## 2. Scope
This policy applies exclusively to agency staff working at Company premises. It does not change the rules for regular Company employees. Permanent staff members must continue to follow their designated internal schedules, timekeeping methods, and corporate presence guidelines as outlined in their respective employment contracts and standard organizational handbooks. External contractors, consultants, and personnel provided by third-party staffing agencies who utilize our physical offices, workstations, or facilities fall entirely within the boundaries of this document. Management must ensure that external partners are fully briefed on these operational expectations before their personnel begin tasks on-site.

## 3. Policy
Regarding daily arrival and operational presence, agency staff working at Company premises follow the vendor's attendance rule with a 5-minute grace period. This means that external personnel must primarily adhere to the punctuality and scheduling guidelines established by their direct employment agency or staffing provider. However, to accommodate minor transit delays, elevator queues, or security check-in procedures upon arrival at our physical location, a punctual window of 5 minutes is granted beyond the official start time set by the vendor. Workers who arrive within this designated window will not be flagged for tardiness. Conversely, individuals arriving after the expiration of this specific timeframe will be recorded as late according to the guidelines set by their supplier.

## 4. Responsibilities
Managing adherence to these time and presence guidelines requires active coordination among multiple stakeholders. The third-party staffing agency is primarily responsible for establishing the baseline schedule and communicating attendance expectations clearly to their placed workers before they arrive at our facilities. Agency staff working at Company premises are personally responsible for monitoring their own arrival times, respecting the established schedules, and utilizing the allotted 5-minute grace period appropriately rather than treating it as a daily target. Facility supervisors and designated internal coordinators are responsible for overseeing daily operations, noting any persistent attendance irregularities, and communicating promptly with the external vendor if punctuality standards agreed upon by the supplier are consistently unmet.

## 5. Exceptions
Given that agency staff working at Company premises follow the vendor's attendance rule with a 5-minute grace period, deviations from this standard framework are strictly limited. Exceptional circumstances, such as severe facility closures, localized emergencies, or extraordinary operational disruptions affecting our buildings, may warrant temporary adjustments to daily arrival protocols. In such rare scenarios, internal leadership will coordinate directly with the staffing agency to communicate alternative arrangements. Individual requests for personal schedule modifications or customized grace periods outside the established vendor parameters cannot be accommodated, as the primary scheduling authority remains with the external provider.

## 6. Related documents
To ensure comprehensive understanding and alignment across all operational areas, this policy should be read in conjunction with other pertinent organizational materials. Relevant documentation includes the general workplace conduct guidelines, facility access and security protocols for visitors and contractors, and any master service agreements established between the Company and external staffing vendors. Personnel should also consult their specific agency handbooks for further details regarding how their respective employers track overall hours, manage leave requests, and address disciplinary matters related to punctuality.
