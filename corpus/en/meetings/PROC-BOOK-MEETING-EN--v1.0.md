# Booking a Meeting Room

## 1. Purpose
This procedure ensures that all workspace reservations within the organization are managed fairly and efficiently. To maintain a productive workplace environment, the Company provides designated spaces for team collaborations, discussions, and presentations. Proper scheduling prevents scheduling conflicts, optimizes the utilization of available office facilities, and ensures that everyone has access to appropriate spaces when needed. Clear guidelines help maintain professional standards, foster transparency across departments, and ensure that shared resources are respected by all personnel.

## 2. Who this applies to
This guideline applies to all personnel, team leaders, and department members who wish to reserve and utilize collaboration spaces for official business purposes. Every employee is responsible for following these instructions to maintain operational efficiency. Supervisors and event organizers must ensure their teams adhere strictly to these operational parameters. Compliance with these rules guarantees fair access for all staff members who rely on these rooms for daily duties and project coordination.

## 3. Steps
Reserving a workspace requires careful planning and adherence to specific operational workflows. The process involves utilizing digital tools to secure the space and preparing necessary materials for attendees. Organizers must follow the established protocol to guarantee a successful gathering without inconveniencing colleagues.
1. Access the shared room calendar provided by the Company to check space availability for your desired time slot.
2. Submit your reservation request through the system, ensuring all details regarding the duration and attendee count are accurate.
3. Prepare the meeting agenda and distribute it to all participants well in advance of the gathering.
4. Arrive promptly on the day of the event to utilize the reserved space, keeping in mind that bookings not used within 10 minutes of the start are released for others to use.
5. Conclude the session on time, clear any personal belongings, and leave the environment ready for the next team.

## 4. Timelines
Time management is crucial for maintaining a smooth workflow across the organization. Specific deadlines govern how and when arrangements must be made. Adhering to these schedules prevents last-minute disruptions and ensures maximum efficiency. 
- Organizers must share the meeting agenda at least 24 hours before the meeting takes place. This ensures that all participants have adequate time to review topics and prepare necessary contributions.
- Bookings not used within 10 minutes of the start are released automatically, allowing other teams to claim the space if the original organizer fails to show up on time.
- To respect work-life balance and operational wind-down periods, no internal meetings may be scheduled after 15:00 on Thursdays.

## 5. Contacts
For assistance with workspace reservations, technical difficulties with the scheduling platform, or questions regarding scheduling policies, employees should reach out to the designated support team. The workplace administration department is available during standard working hours to resolve any scheduling conflicts or address inquiries related to room usage. Reaching out to the appropriate administrative staff ensures that any issues are handled promptly, maintaining a seamless experience for everyone in the organization.
