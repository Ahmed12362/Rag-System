# Sales Client Meeting Rules

## 1. Purpose
This document establishes the operational standards for organizing and documenting client interactions within the Company. Effective management of client engagements ensures professional representation, accurate record-keeping, and streamlined internal communications across the organization. By outlining clear protocols for scheduling and logging meetings, the Company aims to maintain high standards of accountability and customer relationship management. Employees must understand these expectations to support seamless daily operations and protect the professional reputation of the enterprise during all external engagements.

## 2. Applies to
This policy applies exclusively to the Sales department. All personnel operating within the sales division must adhere strictly to these guidelines when interacting with clients and managing schedule records. Other departments within the Company are governed by separate protocols and are not subject to the specific rules detailed in this document. Sales managers and representatives bear the direct responsibility of ensuring that every team member complies with these departmental standards without exception.

## 3. Department rule
Sales staff must log every client visit in the CRM within 24 hours. This timeline ensures that all customer interaction data remains current and accessible to authorized personnel across the business. Additionally, any meetings involving external participants or more than 10 attendees require approval from the department head before invitations are confirmed. Representatives must secure this managerial authorization well in advance of sending out calendar invites to avoid scheduling conflicts or unauthorized external commitments.

## 4. Rationale
Prompt documentation of client visits prevents the loss of critical information and supports effective pipeline tracking for the Company. Timely CRM entries allow management to review customer feedback, monitor deal progress, and provide appropriate coaching or support. Meanwhile, requiring managerial review for large or external gatherings safeguards corporate interests, maintains protocol consistency, and ensures that appropriate resources and personnel are allocated for complex presentations or discussions.

## 5. Escalation
Failure to log client visits within the designated timeframe or scheduling restricted gatherings without prior leadership authorization will result in internal review by the department head. Employees who encounter difficulties securing timely approvals or experience technical issues with the CRM must notify their supervisor immediately to prevent compliance breaches. Supervisors will address reported delays on a case-by-case basis, determining the appropriate corrective action and ensuring that all records are updated and brought into alignment with established corporate standards.
