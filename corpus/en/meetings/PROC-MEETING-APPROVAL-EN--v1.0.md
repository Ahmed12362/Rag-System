# Requesting Approval for a Meeting

## 1. Purpose
This internal procedure outlines the standard protocol that personnel must follow when organizing professional gatherings within the Company. Effective scheduling ensures that gatherings are productive, necessary, and properly authorized before any commitments are made to attendees. By establishing clear guidelines, the Company aims to optimize operational time and maintain high standards of communication across all departments. This document details the administrative responsibilities of organizers and the expectations placed upon invitees, ensuring that every session runs smoothly from conception to execution. Proper planning prevents scheduling conflicts and guarantees that all necessary preparations are finalized well in advance.

## 2. Who this applies to
The directives contained in this procedure apply universally to all personnel, staff members, and organizers across every department within the Company. Whether an employee is planning a routine team check-in, a cross-departmental strategy session, or a formal consultation, adherence to these instructions is mandatory. Department heads also play a crucial role, as they are responsible for reviewing and authorizing specific requests based on the scope and attendee list. It is the individual responsibility of every organizer to determine the nature of their session and follow the appropriate authorization and notification pathways established by the Company.

## 3. Steps
Organizing a gathering requires careful attention to procedural steps to ensure compliance with the internal policies of the Company. First, the organizer must review the attendee list and the composition of the participant group to determine if special authorization is necessary. Specifically, meetings with external participants or more than 10 attendees require approval from the department head before invitations are confirmed. Organizers must submit the request to their supervisor and wait for formal clearance before sending out final calendar notices. Once clearance is granted and invitations are dispatched, the organizer must share the meeting agenda at least 24 hours before the meeting. This advanced notice allows participants to review discussion topics and prepare adequately. Finally, invitees should review the calendar notice upon receipt. Invitees should accept, decline or mark an invitation as tentative within 24 hours. If an edge case arises, such as a last-minute addition of an external guest, the organizer must immediately pause confirmation and seek retroactive approval from the department head to remain compliant with internal governance.

## 4. Timelines
Adherence to strict scheduling timeframes is essential for maintaining operational efficiency across the Company. When dealing with pre-planning requirements, the organizer must ensure that the meeting agenda is shared at least 24 hours before the meeting begins, providing participants with sufficient preparation time. Additionally, recipients of calendar notices have a defined window to respond; invitees should accept, decline or mark an invitation as tentative within 24 hours of receiving the request. These timeframes help organizers finalize headcounts, book appropriate facilities, and adjust agendas if quorum requirements are not met. Failure to meet these timeline expectations may result in the postponement or cancellation of the gathering to prevent disruptions to daily workflows.

## 5. Contacts
For any questions, clarifications, or support regarding this procedure, personnel can reach out to the designated internal administration team within the Company. If an organizer is uncertain whether their proposed gathering requires authorization from the department head, they should contact their direct supervisor or the internal human resources helpdesk for guidance. Maintaining open communication channels ensures that all scheduling activities align with the governance standards of the Company, reducing administrative delays and fostering a collaborative workplace environment.
