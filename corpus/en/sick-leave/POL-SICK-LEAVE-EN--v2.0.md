# Sick Leave Policy

## 1. Purpose

The Company is committed to supporting the health and well-being of its workforce. When illness strikes, personnel require adequate time to recover and return to their duties fully restored. To ensure operational continuity while providing necessary support, the Company establishes clear guidelines regarding medical absences. This policy outlines the standards and procedures for managing health-related absences, ensuring fairness and transparency across all organizational levels. Understanding these procedures helps maintain a productive work environment and ensures that every individual receives the appropriate support during their recovery period, while also upholding the standards expected by the Company.

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to all personnel engaged by the Company, regardless of their department or hierarchical standing. Whether an individual is a staff member, a supervisor, or a senior leader, these regulations govern how health-related absences are reported, documented, and evaluated. The framework ensures consistency in how medical leave is handled throughout the entire organization, leaving no ambiguity regarding the responsibilities of staff members and management alike. Every member of the organization is expected to adhere strictly to these guidelines whenever health issues prevent them from fulfilling their regular duties.

## 3. Policy

Maintaining personal health is paramount, and the Company provides structured support for recovery. Personnel are granted 15 working days of paid sick leave per calendar year. This annual allotment is designed to cover routine illnesses and minor medical conditions that require rest and recuperation away from the office. 

When an absence occurs, timely communication is essential. An employee who is sick must inform their direct manager before 10:00 on the first day of absence. Furthermore, the formal sick leave request must be recorded in the HR portal no later than 2 calendar days after the first day of absence. 

Documentation requirements depend on the duration of the absence. A medical certificate is required for any sick leave longer than 1 working day. Once the individual is back on duty, this medical certificate must be uploaded to the HR portal within 3 working days of returning to work. 

Edge cases may arise when health issues exceed standard allocations. Sick leave beyond the 15-day annual entitlement is reviewed case by case by Human Resources with a medical report. This ensures that prolonged illnesses receive appropriate administrative and medical evaluation without causing undue distress to the affected individual.

## 4. Responsibilities

Clear division of duties ensures that medical absences are managed efficiently. Personnel are responsible for notifying management on time, submitting formal records through digital systems, and providing required medical documentation within the designated deadlines. 

Managers play a crucial role in operational oversight. Specifically, leave requests are approved by the employee's direct manager; requests from department heads are approved by the Head of Human Resources. This supervisory structure prevents conflicts of interest and ensures proper authorization at every level of the organization. Human Resources remains responsible for overseeing complex cases that exceed standard annual limits, reviewing medical reports, and guiding both staff and leadership through exceptional situations.

## 5. Exceptions

While the guidelines detailed in this document cover the vast majority of scenarios, exceptional circumstances occasionally occur. For instance, severe or chronic health conditions requiring extensive absence beyond standard annual allocations do not automatically result in unpaid status or disciplinary action. Instead, sick leave beyond the 15-day annual entitlement is reviewed case by case by Human Resources with a medical report. No other departures from the established reporting times, documentation rules, or approval hierarchies are permitted unless explicitly authorized by senior management in writing.

## 6. Related documents

To ensure comprehensive understanding and seamless execution of personnel procedures, this policy works in tandem with other internal guidelines maintained by the organization. Staff members and managers should consult the internal employee handbook, performance management standards, and digital system user guides for the HR portal. Reviewing these complementary resources ensures full compliance with all internal standards and provides additional context on general attendance management, workplace health and safety protocols, and digital record-keeping practices within the organization.
