# Sick Leave FAQ - Part 2

## 1. When do I need to provide a medical certificate for an absence?
Team members are required to submit an official medical certificate whenever they take a health-related absence that exceeds 1 working day. For shorter absences of 1 working day or less, this documentation is not mandatory, though the Company still expects proper notification to supervisors. When planning or dealing with unexpected health issues, understanding this threshold helps employees manage their administrative responsibilities correctly. In edge cases where an illness spans multiple days, counting begins from the very first missed shift. Supervisors and HR use this guideline to ensure fairness and consistency across all departments while accommodating legitimate health concerns.

## 2. What is the deadline for submitting my medical certificate after coming back?
Once a staff member resumes their duties, they have a strict timeframe of 3 working days to upload the required medical certificate to the HR portal. Missing this submission window can cause administrative delays and potential payroll discrepancies. Responsibilities lie entirely with the employee to secure the necessary paperwork from their healthcare provider and complete the upload process promptly upon return. If technical difficulties arise with the portal, staff must notify support immediately to document their attempt to meet the deadline. Compliance with this rule ensures that personnel records remain accurate and up to date.

## 3. Where should I upload my medical documentation?
All medical certificates must be submitted digitally through the designated HR portal provided by the Company. Employees should not send these sensitive documents via standard email or messaging apps to maintain data privacy and security standards. The portal system guides users through the submission steps and provides an instant confirmation receipt once the file is successfully uploaded. Responsibilities for maintaining this digital record belong jointly to the employee, who must provide the file, and HR, who must securely store it. Edge cases involving corrupted files or unreadable scans require the individual to re-upload a clear copy within the active submission window.

## 4. What happens if my medical leave exceeds my yearly allocation?
Staff members are entitled to a specific annual allowance of 15 days for health-related absences. When an individual requires sick leave that goes beyond this 15-day annual entitlement, the situation cannot be automatically approved through standard channels. Instead, the Company handles these extended absences through a rigorous evaluation process. Both supervisors and employees need to be aware that exceeding this threshold triggers a formal review of the individual's employment status and ongoing health requirements, necessitating open communication with management.

## 5. Who evaluates my situation if I need more time off for health reasons?
When health absences surpass the standard annual allocation, Human Resources takes direct charge of evaluating the case on an individual basis. This review is never handled by direct line managers alone, ensuring that company-wide policies are applied uniformly and fairly. The evaluation team looks closely at the specific circumstances surrounding the prolonged absence to determine the appropriate next steps for both the worker and the organization. Employees are responsible for cooperating fully with HR during this review period and providing any requested information regarding their ongoing recovery status.

## 6. What additional documentation is necessary for extended health absences?
For any health-related absence stretching beyond the standard yearly limit, a comprehensive medical report is strictly required to support the review conducted by Human Resources. A basic doctor's note is insufficient for these extended scenarios; the detailed medical report must outline the diagnosis, treatment plan, and expected recovery trajectory. The Company relies on this documentation to make informed decisions regarding extended leave approvals and workplace accommodations. Employees facing long-term health challenges should coordinate closely with their medical providers to ensure this detailed report is generated and ready for submission to HR as soon as requested.
