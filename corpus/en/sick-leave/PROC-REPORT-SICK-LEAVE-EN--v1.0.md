# How to Report Sick Leave

## 1. Purpose
This document establishes the official procedure for reporting and recording medical absences within the organization. The Company aims to maintain operational continuity while ensuring personnel receive necessary recovery time. Establishing a clear protocol helps managers plan coverage and ensures that absences are documented accurately for payroll and compliance purposes. Every staff member is required to follow these guidelines whenever illness prevents them from attending work, thereby maintaining transparency across all departments and supporting a healthy workplace environment.

## 2. Who this applies to
This procedure applies to all active personnel across all departments and locations. Whether working remotely or on-site, every worker is bound by these rules. Direct managers, team leads, and human resources personnel also have specific responsibilities in overseeing, receiving, and validating these submissions. It is essential that all staff members familiarize themselves with these requirements before any medical absence occurs, ensuring they understand their obligations when illness strikes unexpectedly.

## 3. Steps
Reporting an illness requires adherence to specific actions to ensure proper documentation. An employee who is sick must inform their direct manager before 10:00 on the first day of absence. This initial communication can be made via phone call or direct messaging to give the team immediate awareness of the situation. 

Following the initial notification, formal administrative steps must be completed. All leave requests are submitted and tracked through the HR self-service portal; email or verbal requests are not valid. Logging into the system allows the staff member to input the start date and expected duration of their absence. 

Furthermore, documentation rules depend on the length of the absence. A medical certificate is required for any sick leave longer than 1 working day. If the absence spans multiple days exceeding this threshold, the official doctor's note must be scanned and uploaded directly into the system. Failure to provide this documentation may result in the absence being marked as unauthorized.

## 4. Timelines
Adhering to strict timeframes is critical for the proper processing of medical absences. The initial notification to the direct manager must happen promptly, specifically before 10:00 on the first day of absence, allowing teams to adjust daily schedules and reassign urgent tasks. 

Regarding administrative entry, the sick leave request must be recorded in the HR portal no later than 2 calendar days after the first day of absence. Missing this deadline can cause delays in payroll processing and compliance tracking. For absences extending beyond 1 working day, securing and submitting the medical certificate must also be done within a reasonable timeframe to support the portal entry.

## 5. Contacts
For assistance with medical absence reporting, staff members should reach out to the appropriate internal resources. The primary point of contact for the initial notification is the direct manager. For technical difficulties related to the system, questions about documentation requirements, or policy clarifications, personnel should contact the human resources support team through the official internal directory channels.
