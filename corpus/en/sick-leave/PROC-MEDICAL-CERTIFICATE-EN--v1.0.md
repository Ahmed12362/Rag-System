# Submitting a Medical Certificate

## 1. Purpose
The primary aim of this internal policy is to establish a clear and consistent protocol for how staff members notify the Company about health-related absences and provide the necessary medical documentation. Ensuring transparency and proper record-keeping is vital for maintaining smooth daily operations and accurate attendance tracking across all departments. Whenever a team member falls ill, the Company requires formal validation for extended absences to protect both the individual and the business. This procedure ensures that sick leave is recorded correctly, compliance with internal standards is met, and payroll processing remains accurate without unnecessary disruptions or misunderstandings between employees and management.

## 2. Who this applies to
This directive applies universally to all active personnel employed by the Company, regardless of their department, seniority level, or employment type. Every staff member who needs to take time off due to personal illness or medical appointments must adhere to these guidelines. Team leaders and human resources personnel also play a vital role in overseeing and facilitating this process, ensuring that every employee understands their obligations regarding health documentation. Whether an individual works on-site or remotely, adherence to this procedure is mandatory whenever health issues impact their regular work schedule.

## 3. Steps
Navigating the documentation process requires adherence to established digital channels to ensure your absence is officially recognized and approved. All leave requests, including health-related absences, must be submitted and tracked through the HR self-service portal; email or verbal requests are not valid. Relying on informal messages sent directly to managers or colleagues does not fulfill administrative requirements and may result in unauthorized absence status. 

To remain fully compliant with internal regulations, employees must follow specific thresholds depending on the length of their absence. A medical certificate is required for any sick leave longer than 1 working day. If you are unwell for a brief duration, formal medical proof might not be mandatory, but once your absence extends past the initial threshold, obtaining documentation from a licensed healthcare provider becomes compulsory. 

Furthermore, timing is critical when it comes to administrative submission. The medical certificate must be uploaded to the HR portal within 3 working days of returning to work. Failing to upload the required paperwork within this window can lead to delayed leave approval or adjustments to your compensation. Employees should ensure their files are clear, legible, and successfully submitted through the designated portal interface before the deadline expires.

## 4. Timelines
Managing deadlines effectively prevents administrative delays and ensures that employee records remain up to date. The framework relies on specific operational windows that every staff member must respect. For short absences, the initial day off does not trigger the requirement for a doctor's note, but anything extending past that limit demands formal proof. Following your resumption of duties, you are granted a limited window to finalize your paperwork. The medical certificate must be uploaded to the HR portal within 3 working days of returning to work. Adhering strictly to these schedules guarantees that human resources can process your absence correctly and prevents any complications during the upcoming payroll cycle.

## 5. Contacts
If you encounter technical difficulties while navigating the self-service portal or have questions regarding your documentation obligations, support is readily available. You can reach out to the human resources department through the internal communication channels provided on the Company intranet. The support team is equipped to assist with portal login issues, submission errors, or clarifying whether your specific medical documentation meets internal standards. Proactive communication with HR ensures that any unforeseen complications are resolved swiftly, keeping your leave records accurate and compliant with all Company directives.
