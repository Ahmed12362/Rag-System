# Sick Leave FAQ - Part 1

## 1. How many days of paid sick leave are granted each calendar year?
The Company provides all staff members with a specific annual allocation to support their health and recovery when they fall ill. Every employee is entitled to 15 working days of paid sick leave per calendar year. This allowance resets annually and is designed to cover standard medical absences. To manage this benefit properly, staff must keep track of their utilized days. For instance, if an individual falls ill and takes time off, these days are deducted from the yearly total. Responsibilities include monitoring one's own leave balance to avoid exceeding the annual limit. Edge cases may involve new hires joining mid-year or staff members transitioning between departments, but the baseline allocation remains standard for eligible personnel as defined by the Company.

## 2. What is the deadline for notifying the direct manager about an absence due to illness?
Communication is vital when an unexpected medical issue arises, ensuring that the team can adjust workflows and manage daily operations smoothly. An employee who is sick must inform their direct manager before 10:00 on the first day of absence. This early notification requirement allows leadership to redistribute urgent tasks and make necessary staffing arrangements. Responsibilities fall directly on the absent staff member to reach out via phone, email, or messaging as soon as symptoms or medical situations prevent them from attending work. An edge case might occur if someone is incapacitated or hospitalized suddenly; in such scenarios, a family member or colleague should attempt to notify management as soon as possible, keeping the spirit of the Company policy in mind while adhering strictly to the operational timeline.

## 3. How and when must the sick leave request be officially logged in the system?
Beyond direct communication with leadership, administrative tracking is mandatory for all formal absences. The sick leave request must be recorded in the HR portal no later than 2 calendar days after the first day of absence. This administrative step ensures that the records department and payroll can accurately process the time off without discrepancies. Employees carry the responsibility of accessing the portal and submitting the required details promptly. An edge case could involve system maintenance or technical outages preventing portal access; if the platform is down, the staff member should notify HR via email to create a paper trail, ensuring compliance with the timeline despite technical hurdles.

## 4. What happens if notification to the direct manager is delayed past the morning deadline?
Timely reporting keeps the workplace running efficiently and prevents unnecessary delays in project execution. Failing to notify leadership prior to the established morning cutoff can create operational bottlenecks and confusion within the team. The Company emphasizes that communication must happen proactively, reinforcing the rule that an employee who is sick must inform their direct manager before 10:00 on the first day of absence. Responsibilities dictate that staff prioritize this communication even when feeling unwell. An edge case might involve sudden severe emergencies where waking up or reaching a device before the deadline is physically impossible, requiring subsequent clarification and discussion with management upon return.

## 5. Can the annual sick leave allocation be carried over to the next calendar year if unused?
Managing yearly benefits efficiently requires understanding the expiration terms associated with health-related time off. The Company structures its medical leave policy on a strict annual cycle. Because employees are entitled to 15 working days of paid sick leave per calendar year, any unused portion of this allowance expires at the end of the calendar period and does not roll over. Responsibilities include planning necessary medical appointments or wellness days within the active year. Edge cases do not alter this expiration rule, meaning staff cannot accumulate unused days to increase future balances.

## 6. What are the consequences of failing to log the absence in the HR portal on time?
Administrative compliance is just as important as medical reporting to maintain accurate employment records. Neglecting the digital submission timeline can lead to processing delays or unexcused absence classifications. Because the sick leave request must be recorded in the HR portal no later than 2 calendar days after the first day of absence, missing this window compromises official tracking. Employees are responsible for meeting this administrative milestone regardless of how they feel. An edge case could involve returning to work on the third day and immediately submitting the entry, though any delay beyond the designated window requires special HR review and justification.
