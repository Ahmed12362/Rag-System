# Annual Leave Policy

## 1. Purpose
The purpose of this document is to outline the annual leave framework established by the Company for version 1.0, which remained in force from 2023-01-01 to 2025-12-31. Rest and time away from the workplace are vital for maintaining employee well-being, productivity, and overall engagement. This policy ensures that all personnel understand their time-off entitlements, the procedural requirements for requesting leave, and how rest periods are calculated within the organization. By providing clear guidelines, the Company aims to balance operational continuity with the personal needs of the workforce, ensuring that every team member has adequate opportunity to recharge throughout the calendar year while maintaining clear expectations across all departments.

## 2. Scope
This policy applies universally to all eligible personnel operating under the Company structure during the designated active period. Whether an employee is newly hired or a long-standing member of the organization, this framework governs all standard time-off accruals and usage. It covers standard working schedules and outlines how various employment milestones interact with leave balances. Department leaders, individual contributors, and administrative personnel must all adhere to the provisions detailed herein. By defining the scope clearly, the organization ensures that time-off administration is handled fairly, consistently, and transparently for everyone on the payroll, regardless of their specific role or internal department.

## 3. Policy
Time away from work is a fundamental benefit provided to support staff health and work-life balance. Under this policy, personnel are entitled to 21 working days of paid annual leave per calendar year. To recognize loyalty and extended tenure, personnel with 10 or more full years of service are entitled to 30 working days of annual leave per year. This enhanced long-service entitlement applies from the calendar year after 10 full years of service are completed. When planning time off, all personnel must ensure that annual leave requests are submitted at least 7 calendar days in advance of the intended start date. This advance notice allows supervisors to manage team capacity and operational coverage effectively. For the purpose of calculating balances, leave is counted strictly in working days, which are defined as Sunday to Thursday. Consequently, Friday, Saturday, and official public holidays are not deducted from any leave balance, ensuring that weekends and recognized public holidays remain fully protected rest days for the workforce.

## 4. Responsibilities
Managing time off successfully requires active cooperation between employees, supervisors, and administrative personnel. Personnel are responsible for planning their time away responsibly, ensuring they submit their required notifications in a timely manner, and verifying that their schedules do not compromise critical business deliverables. Leave requests are approved by the employee's direct manager, who evaluates operational needs, team coverage, and upcoming project deadlines before granting authorization. To maintain proper governance and prevent conflicts of interest, requests originating from department heads are approved by the Head of Human Resources. Human Resources personnel are responsible for maintaining accurate leave records, auditing balance calculations, and ensuring that all departmental workflows comply with the standards set forth in this policy document.

## 5. Exceptions
While the standard guidelines govern the vast majority of time-off scenarios, exceptional circumstances may occasionally arise. Operational emergencies or unexpected business demands might require adjustments to standard notice periods or scheduling patterns, though these situations must be managed with appropriate oversight. Personnel who have not yet completed their initial qualifying periods or those navigating unique contractual arrangements must consult with human resources to clarify how time off is administered in their specific case. Any deviation from the standard approval hierarchy or standard balance deductions requires formal review and written authorization from the Head of Human Resources to ensure fairness and consistency across the organization.

## 6. Related documents
To ensure comprehensive understanding of workforce guidelines, this policy should be read alongside other internal governance materials maintained by the organization. Relevant supplementary documentation includes the employee handbook, operational workflow manuals, and internal communication guidelines issued by the human resources department. These related documents provide broader context regarding workplace conduct, attendance expectations, and administrative procedures, helping personnel navigate their employment experience seamlessly while aligning with the overarching standards established by the Company.
