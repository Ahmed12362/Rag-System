# Annual Leave Policy

## 1. Purpose
The purpose of this formal document issued by the Company is to establish clear and transparent guidelines regarding time away from work for personal rest, rejuvenation, and family matters. The Company values the well-being and work-life balance of all personnel. By outlining clear procedures, the Company ensures operational continuity while granting staff adequate opportunities to recharge away from their daily professional responsibilities. This policy provides a structured framework for managing time off fairly, consistently, and transparently across all departments within the enterprise, ensuring that every worker understands their rights, duties, and the administrative mechanisms involved in scheduling their rightful time off throughout the professional cycle.

## 2. Scope
This policy applies universally to all personnel actively employed by the Company across all organizational tiers and operational units. Whether staff members are newly onboarded or have accumulated extensive tenure with the organization, the provisions set forth herein govern how rest periods are requested, evaluated, tracked, and utilized. Understanding the scope helps individuals determine their specific tier of entitlement based on their duration of service, ensuring seamless application of the rules regardless of departmental assignment or hierarchical placement within the corporate structure.

## 3. Policy
Every worker at the Company is entitled to 21 working days of paid annual leave per calendar year. For long-serving personnel, staff members with 10 or more full years of service are entitled to 30 working days of annual leave per year. This long-service entitlement applies from the calendar year after 10 full years of service are completed. 

When planning time away, annual leave requests should be submitted at least 14 calendar days before the first day of leave. Shorter notice is accepted only at the manager's discretion. Regarding duration limits for continuous absence, a single annual leave request may cover at most 15 consecutive working days unless the Head of Human Resources approves a longer period. 

To ensure proper operational staffing, leave is counted in working days, defined as Sunday to Thursday. Consequently, Friday, Saturday, and official public holidays are not deducted from any leave balance. Furthermore, regarding carry-over rules, up to 5 unused annual leave days may be carried over to the next year and must be used by 31 March. Any other unused days are forfeited. 

Transparency is maintained through digital portals; the remaining balance shown in the portal equals the yearly entitlement minus approved days minus days in pending requests.

## 4. Responsibilities
Managing time away requires active cooperation between staff and leadership. Employees must submit their scheduling applications well in advance, monitoring their portal balances to ensure accuracy. Direct supervisors and leaders bear specific procedural duties. Specifically, managers must approve or reject a leave request within 3 working days of submission. 

Concerning authority levels, leave requests are approved by the employee's direct manager. However, requests from department heads are approved by the Head of Human Resources to maintain proper governance and oversight. Leaders must evaluate operational needs while respecting staff entitlements, ensuring fair distribution of rest periods without compromising organizational output or departmental deliverables.

## 5. Exceptions
While standard procedures apply universally, certain edge cases and special situations may arise. For instance, if an employee requires shorter notice for urgent personal matters, the request is evaluated solely at the manager's discretion, as the standard rule mandates a 14-day advance notice. Similarly, if an employee wishes to take an extended continuous break exceeding 15 consecutive working days, standard management approval is insufficient; such extended arrangements require formal authorization from the Head of Human Resources. All exceptions must be documented appropriately within the internal tracking system to maintain compliance with corporate governance standards.

## 6. Related documents
To ensure comprehensive understanding and alignment across the organization, this policy should be read in conjunction with other internal guidelines maintained by the Company. Personnel should consult the employee handbook, attendance guidelines, public holiday schedules, and digital portal user manuals. These reference materials provide supplementary details on operational procedures, system navigation, and broader employment standards upheld by the Company, ensuring all staff remain fully informed of their rights and obligations.
