# Annual Leave FAQ - Part 2

## 1. How far in advance should I submit my annual leave request?
To ensure proper operational planning within the Company, all annual leave requests should be submitted at least 14 calendar days before the first day of leave. Team members are strongly encouraged to plan their time off well ahead of schedule to help maintain seamless business operations and team coverage. However, we understand that unexpected situations can arise in personal lives. Therefore, shorter notice is accepted only at the manager's discretion, depending on immediate team workload and operational readiness. Managers evaluate such late requests on a case-by-case basis, and approval is never guaranteed when submitting with reduced notice.

## 2. Can my manager take as long as they want to review my request?
The Company values timely communication and transparency between team members and leadership. Consequently, managers must approve or reject a leave request within 3 working days of submission. This standard service level agreement ensures that employees receive a prompt response so they can finalize their travel arrangements, personal commitments, or rest periods without unnecessary anxiety. If a manager fails to respond within this timeframe, staff members should politely follow up with their supervisor or contact the HR department for assistance to ensure the workflow stays on track.

## 3. What is the maximum duration for a single continuous leave period?
Taking extended breaks for relaxation and personal well-being is fully supported by the Company. However, to balance rest with ongoing project responsibilities, a single annual leave request may cover at most 15 consecutive working days. This restriction prevents prolonged staff absences that could negatively impact team deliverables. If an employee wishes to take an extended holiday that exceeds this length, an exception can be made. Specifically, this limit applies unless the Head of Human Resources approves a longer period in writing following a formal business justification review.

## 4. Can I cancel my leave after it has already been approved?
Flexibility is a key component of the workplace culture at the Company. If plans change due to unforeseen circumstances or shifting project deadlines, an employee may cancel a pending or approved leave request in the portal any time before the first day of leave. This digital self-service functionality ensures that records remain completely accurate. Once the cancellation is successfully processed through the system, the days return to the balance immediately, making them available for future time-off requests later in the year.

## 5. What happens to my unused annual leave days at the end of the year?
Proper time management includes utilizing your earned rest throughout the year. Nevertheless, up to 5 unused annual leave days may be carried over to the next year and must be used by 31 March. This rollover provision offers helpful flexibility for individuals who prefer to take a vacation during the early spring months. Employees must carefully monitor their balances to ensure they utilize these specific carried-over days before the expiration deadline.

## 6. Are all unused leave days eligible for rollover or payout?
While a limited rollover is permitted under Company policy, structure is necessary to encourage healthy work-life balance and prevent excessive accumulation of time off. Any other unused days beyond the permitted rollover amount are forfeited. This means that if staff members accumulate extra leave throughout the cycle and fail to take it, those surplus days will simply lapse and cannot be cashed out or extended further. Therefore, everyone is advised to schedule their rest periods diligently throughout the active cycle to avoid losing any earned time.
