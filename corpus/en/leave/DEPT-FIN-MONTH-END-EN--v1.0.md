# Finance Month-End Leave Rule

## 1. Purpose
The primary objective of this internal guideline is to maintain seamless operational continuity within the financial sector of the Company during critical closing periods. Managing workforce availability ensures that all monthly financial reports, reconciliations, and accounting duties are completed without disruption. By establishing clear oversight for planned time away, the organization balances individual employee rest with robust departmental deliverables.

## 2. Applies to
This policy is strictly enforced and pertains exclusively to the Finance department of the Company. It governs all team members operating within this specific business unit, regardless of their seniority or tenure. Personnel outside this division are subject to separate operational guidelines regarding their time away from the workplace.

## 3. Department rule
Standard time-off submissions within the Company are typically authorized by an employee's direct manager. However, when a departmental head submits a request, authorization must be granted by the Head of Human Resources. Furthermore, for personnel in the Finance department, any annual leave request that falls within the last 3 working days of a month requires an additional authorization layer from the Head of Finance. This ensures that peak operational windows remain fully staffed to handle closing responsibilities.

## 4. Rationale
The financial cycle of the Company demands intensive oversight and rigorous data processing at the conclusion of every calendar month. When team members take time away during the final operational days, the remaining staff faces increased pressure to finalize ledgers and reports. Requiring specialized authorization from the Head of Finance for time off scheduled during the last 3 working days of a month helps mitigate workload bottlenecks and prevents oversight gaps during high-stress reporting windows.

## 5. Escalation
Should any scheduling conflicts arise or if specific time-off requests require extraordinary review during critical operational windows, personnel must follow the appropriate internal reporting lines. Departmental heads seeking authorization must engage directly with the Head of Human Resources. Meanwhile, staff members navigating complex scheduling disputes regarding the final days of the month should consult their direct manager and the Head of Finance to reach a balanced resolution that protects the workflow of the Company.
