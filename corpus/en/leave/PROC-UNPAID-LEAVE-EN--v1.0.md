# Requesting Unpaid Leave

## 1. Purpose
This internal procedure outlines how employees can take time away from work without pay when they need to manage personal matters that extend beyond their standard time-off balances. Since this type of absence comes with no balance, understanding the proper process ensures that team members can plan their personal affairs while maintaining transparency with the organization. The Company provides this option to support staff during unique life circumstances, provided that operational continuity is carefully maintained and all required authorizations are secured. 

## 2. Who this applies to
This process applies to all active personnel across the organization who require extended absence without financial compensation. Whether an employee needs time for personal development, family obligations, or unforeseen personal situations, they must follow the established channel. The Company requires all staff members to use the designated digital platform for their submissions, ensuring that informal communications such as verbal agreements or direct messages are never accepted as valid authorizations.

## 3. Steps
To secure time away without pay, employees must follow a strict digital workflow. All leave requests are submitted and tracked through the HR self-service portal; email or verbal requests are not valid. 

First, the staff member must log into the designated digital platform and complete the absence form, clearly indicating the requested dates. Second, the direct manager reviews the operational impact and provides an initial decision. Third, the request routes to the Human Resources department for final validation. Unpaid leave of up to 30 working days per year may be granted with approval from the direct manager and Human Resources; it has no balance. 

For example, an employee needing time to relocate can submit their paperwork online weeks ahead. If an unexpected family emergency arises, the employee should still initiate the digital form as soon as possible, noting the urgent nature of the situation. Edge cases, such as requests exceeding the annual maximum limit, require special management consultation and are generally subject to strict denial unless exceptional circumstances are documented.

## 4. Timelines
Proper scheduling helps maintain team productivity and ensures smooth coverage during absences. Unpaid leave should be requested at least 30 calendar days in advance except in emergencies. 

Planning ahead allows supervisors to adjust workloads and reassign critical tasks. In urgent scenarios where advance notice is impossible, staff must submit their digital application immediately upon the emergence of the situation, though approval is never guaranteed and depends heavily on operational urgency and management discretion.

## 5. Contacts
Employees requiring assistance with their submissions or needing clarification on the approval workflow should reach out to the appropriate internal teams. For technical difficulties with the digital portal, contact the IT support desk. For policy guidance regarding unpaid time away limits and documentation requirements, employees should communicate directly with the Human Resources department.
