# Annual Leave FAQ - Part 1

## 1. How many paid annual leave days do I get each calendar year?
Employees at the Company receive a standard yearly allowance of paid time off to rest and recharge. Specifically, personnel are entitled to 21 working days of paid annual leave per calendar year. This provision forms a core part of the total compensation and benefits package managed by the organization. To ensure proper planning, staff members should coordinate their schedules with their immediate managers well in advance. Responsibilities include submitting requests through the official portal and ensuring adequate coverage for daily operations during the absence. For example, if someone takes a full workweek off, exactly 5 days are deducted from the allowance. An edge case involves new joiners or individuals leaving the organization mid-year, where the total time off is typically prorated according to internal guidelines and active service duration.

## 2. Is there an increased leave entitlement for long-serving personnel?
Yes, the Company recognizes and rewards loyalty and extended tenure by offering an enhanced time-off benefit to senior staff members. Specifically, employees with 10 or more full years of service are entitled to 30 working days of annual leave per year. This increased benefit is designed to acknowledge the deep commitment and continuous contribution of long-term team members. Team leaders and human resources personnel must verify employment milestones accurately to ensure this transition happens smoothly. For instance, an individual who reaches this milestone will automatically see their annual quota updated in the system once eligibility is confirmed. An edge case might involve a staff member whose anniversary falls midway through the calendar year, requiring careful tracking of service history.

## 3. When does the long-service leave entitlement actually start applying?
Transitioning to the higher tier of time off follows a specific timeline regulated by internal policy. The long-service entitlement applies from the calendar year after 10 full years of service are completed. This rule ensures clarity and standardization across the entire organization regarding when upgraded benefits become active. Human resources oversees the tracking of service milestones, while staff members are responsible for reviewing their profile details to ensure accuracy. For example, if an employee completes the required tenure in October, the enhanced quota activates starting in January of the following calendar year. An edge case includes verifying continuous service periods, especially if there were any previous leaves of absence or career breaks that might impact the total calculated tenure.

## 4. How are weekends and public holidays treated when I take time off?
When calculating time away from the office, the Company applies a straightforward methodology focused strictly on standard operational days. Leave is counted in working days (Sunday to Thursday); Friday, Saturday and official public holidays are not deducted from any leave balance. This ensures that staff members do not lose their weekend rest days or official holiday celebrations when submitting a request. Employees carry the responsibility of checking the official holiday calendar before applying for time off to maximize their rest period efficiently. For example, taking a Sunday through Thursday break during a week that contains an official public holiday results in only 4 days being deducted instead of 5. An edge case involves public holidays that fall on a Friday or Saturday, which generally do not alter the standard working day calculations.

## 5. How is my remaining leave balance calculated and displayed in the portal?
Transparency is a key priority for the Company regarding time-off tracking and personal allocations. The remaining balance shown in the portal equals the yearly entitlement minus approved days minus days in pending requests. This real-time calculation prevents accidental over-booking and gives staff members a clear picture of their available time at any given moment. The technical systems team is responsible for maintaining accurate calculations, while individual employees must monitor their dashboard regularly. For example, if an individual has their yearly quota, has taken some days, and currently has another submission waiting for manager approval, all of those pending days are instantly subtracted from the visible total. An edge case arises if a pending request is eventually rejected, causing the system to automatically restore those specific days back to the active balance.

## 6. What steps should I take if I notice a discrepancy in my leave balance?
Maintaining accurate records is a shared responsibility between staff members and the administrative teams within the Company. If an employee spots an unusual figure or a potential calculation error on their profile dashboard, they should immediately report the issue. The employee must gather relevant approval notifications and submit a formal query to the human resources department for investigation. Human resources is then responsible for auditing the account history, checking past approvals, and coordinating with technical support if a system glitch caused the error. For example, if approved time off was mistakenly counted twice, the HR team will manually correct the entry. An edge case includes complex historical adjustments stemming from status changes or retroactive policy updates, which may require additional verification steps.
