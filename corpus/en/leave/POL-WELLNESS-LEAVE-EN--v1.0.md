# Wellness Leave (Draft)

## 1. Purpose
This document serves as a draft proposal (version 1.0) and is currently for consultation purposes only. It is not yet in force and is not applicable to any operations or personnel at the present time. Should the leadership formally approve this text, it is scheduled to take effect on 2027-01-01. 

The primary objective of this proposed framework is to prioritize the psychological well-being, mental health, and overall physical rest of personnel across the organization. The Company recognizes that sustaining high performance requires adequate pauses from daily tasks. By introducing this designated time away from duties, the organization aims to reduce burnout, support psychological resilience, and foster a balanced work environment where staff can recharge and maintain their long-term health.

## 2. Scope
Upon potential enactment on 2027-01-01, this draft policy would apply universally to every employee currently active within the Company. Regardless of departmental placement, tenure length, or hierarchical level, all staff members would be included under the umbrella of this initiative. 

Edge cases concerning newly hired personnel, staff undergoing probationary periods, or individuals returning from extended leaves of absence would be managed equitiously to ensure fair access. Part-time or specialized staff would also fall within the designated scope, ensuring that the benefit is distributed evenly across the workforce to support collective mental health and adequate rest.

## 3. Policy
The core of this proposed provision is that a new wellness leave type would give every employee 2 paid days per year for mental health and rest. This allocation is intended specifically for moments when staff require a brief pause to decompress, manage stress, or address personal well-being without financial penalty. 

To illustrate how this would function in practice, a staff member feeling overwhelmed by intensive project cycles could utilize one of these designated days to disconnect entirely from professional communications. Because these days are fully compensated, the individual receives their standard remuneration while taking the necessary time to recover. 

Regarding operational edge cases, these days are designed to be used within the designated annual cycle and do not accumulate or roll over into subsequent periods if unused. Furthermore, unlike traditional vacation days that often require weeks of advance planning, these mental health and rest days are conceived to offer flexibility, though supervisory notification remains standard practice to ensure team coverage and operational continuity.

## 4. Responsibilities
Management, human resources personnel, and individual staff members all share distinct duties regarding the proposed wellness initiative. Employees are responsible for planning their rest periods thoughtfully, communicating their absence transparently to their supervisors, and utilizing the provision strictly for its intended purpose of mental health recovery and personal rest.

Supervisors and departmental managers carry the duty of fostering an encouraging team culture where personnel feel genuinely supported in taking their mental health days without fear of professional repercussion. Managers must review absence notifications objectively, ensuring that operational demands do not unfairly block staff from accessing their allocated rest time. 

Human resources personnel are tasked with overseeing the administrative tracking of these paid days, ensuring accurate payroll integration once the framework becomes active, and providing guidance to staff regarding policy interpretation.

## 5. Exceptions
While the proposed framework is designed to be universal, certain operational emergencies or critical business continuity scenarios may require adjustments to the timing of requested rest days. In rare instances where an urgent, unforeseen client crisis or critical system failure demands immediate staffing, a supervisor may request an employee to postpone their wellness day to an alternative mutually agreeable date. 

Such exceptions are intended to be strictly temporary and exceptional, ensuring that the fundamental right of the staff member to take their designated mental health and rest time is honored shortly thereafter. Under no circumstances would an exception result in the permanent forfeiture of the allocated days during the active cycle.

## 6. Related documents
To ensure alignment across the organization, this draft proposal should be read in conjunction with existing internal HR guidelines, performance management frameworks, and standard operating procedures concerning general time off. Future iterations of this draft will reference updated employee handbooks and welfare guidelines to maintain consistency in how the Company supports staff wellness, mental health, and rest across all operational divisions.
