# How to Request Annual Leave

## 1. Purpose
This internal procedure outlines the standard process for staff members within the Company to apply for their yearly time off. Managing time off effectively ensures that operations continue smoothly while allowing personnel to take their well-deserved rest. The Company provides a structured framework to maintain transparency and fairness across all departments. By following these guidelines, employees can ensure their applications are processed correctly and efficiently by the relevant administrative and managerial teams.

## 2. Who this applies to
This process is applicable to all personnel working across the Company, regardless of their department or location. Every staff member is required to adhere strictly to these operational instructions when planning their absence. It is the responsibility of each individual to understand their entitlements and submit their paperwork correctly. Additionally, managers and supervisors must familiarize themselves with their role in reviewing and deciding upon submissions from their direct reports, ensuring consistent application of internal policies.

## 3. Steps
Staff members must utilize the designated digital platform for all time-off transactions within the Company. All leave requests are submitted and tracked through the HR self-service portal; email or verbal requests are not valid. When preparing a submission, applicants should review their available entitlement. The remaining balance shown in the portal equals the yearly entitlement minus approved days minus days in pending requests. 

Approval authority depends on the organizational level of the applicant. Leave requests are approved by the employee's direct manager; requests from department heads are approved by the Head of Human Resources. If plans change, flexibility is built into the system. An employee may cancel a pending or approved leave request in the portal any time before the first day of leave; the days return to the balance immediately upon cancellation.

## 4. Timelines
Planning ahead is crucial for maintaining team productivity within the Company. Annual leave requests should be submitted at least 14 calendar days before the first day of leave; shorter notice is accepted only at the manager's discretion. Submitting applications early prevents scheduling conflicts within the team. Once an application is submitted, supervisors must act promptly. Managers must approve or reject a leave request within 3 working days of submission. Adhering to these timeframes guarantees that operational planning remains robust and employees receive timely responses regarding their time-off status.

## 5. Contacts
For any questions, technical difficulties with the digital platform, or clarifications regarding the submission process, personnel should reach out to the internal support team at the Company. Human Resources representatives are available to assist staff members with navigating the portal, understanding leave balances, or resolving any exceptional scheduling scenarios. Reaching out to the appropriate department ensures that any issues related to time-off management are resolved swiftly and accurately.
