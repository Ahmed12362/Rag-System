# Cancelling a Leave Request

## 1. Purpose
This internal procedure outlines the official guidelines for personnel who wish to revoke a time-off application within the organization. Occasionally, operational needs shift or personal circumstances change, requiring staff members to reclaim previously submitted absence applications. The Company has established this mechanism to ensure that operational planning remains accurate while giving personnel the flexibility to manage their entitlements efficiently. When an application is successfully revoked, the allocated days are automatically restored to the worker's active pool, ensuring that individual leave records remain transparent, up to date, and readily accessible for future scheduling needs across departments.

## 2. Who this applies to
This process is applicable to all active staff members of the Company who utilize the digital management portal to schedule and track their time off. Whether an individual is a full-time permanent worker or operating under a specific employment contract, anyone who has submitted an absence application holds the privilege to utilize this cancellation function. Supervisors, department heads, and human resources personnel should also understand these operational guidelines to assist team members effectively and to maintain accurate oversight of workforce availability and department scheduling across the entire organization.

## 3. Steps
To initiate the revocation of a time-off submission, the worker must log into the designated digital portal utilized by the Company. Navigate directly to the management section where all personal history and active submissions are displayed. Locate the specific submission that requires withdrawal. Depending on the current status of the submission, select the designated option to retract or withdraw the application from the queue. Upon successful execution of this action within the interface, the system processes the request immediately, ensuring that the previously blocked time is released back to the worker's available quota without requiring manual intervention from administrative personnel.

## 4. Timelines
Timing is a critical factor when managing the retraction of a scheduled absence within the Company. Personnel are permitted to execute a cancellation of either a pending or an approved time-off submission via the portal at any point before the commencement of the first day of leave. It is vital that staff members complete the retraction process well in advance, as once the initial day of the scheduled absence begins, the portal restricts the ability to revoke the submission, and the days will be deducted from the quota as originally planned. Regarding the available quota displayed in the system, the remaining balance shown in the portal equals the yearly entitlement minus approved days minus days in pending requests. This calculation updates in real-time as submissions are processed, approved, or cancelled by the worker.

## 5. Contacts
For assistance with the digital portal, system errors, or inquiries regarding the calculation of the remaining balance shown in the portal equals the yearly entitlement minus approved days minus days in pending requests, staff members should reach out to the internal support team of the Company. Human resources representatives are also available to provide guidance on policy matters, quota discrepancies, and procedural requirements to ensure a seamless experience for all personnel navigating time-off management.
