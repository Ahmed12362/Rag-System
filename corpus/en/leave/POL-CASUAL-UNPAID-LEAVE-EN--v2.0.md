# Casual

## 1. Purpose
The Company has established this policy to ensure that workforce members have structured ways to handle unexpected personal situations, professional development milestones, and extended personal absences. Life is unpredictable, and maintaining a healthy work-life balance requires flexible time-off options that respect operational continuity. When urgent personal matters arise that cannot wait for scheduled holidays, staff need a reliable mechanism to address them without undue stress. Furthermore, professional growth is highly encouraged, and staff members sitting official examinations require dedicated preparation time. This document outlines the framework for managing these specific types of time away from the workplace, ensuring transparency, fairness, and clear administrative procedures for all personnel across the organization.

## 2. Scope
This framework applies universally to all active personnel across the organization who require temporary absence from their regular duties for urgent personal reasons, extended personal needs, or professional examination preparation. Whether an individual is facing an unforeseen household emergency, planning a prolonged personal absence, or preparing to advance their career through a recognized qualification, the guidelines herein govern how their absence is requested, evaluated, and recorded. Team members at all levels, from entry-level staff to senior leadership, are expected to adhere strictly to the protocols described in this document. Human Resources and supervisory personnel must also follow these standardized procedures to ensure equitable treatment across every department.

## 3. Policy
To accommodate unexpected life events, staff members are allocated 6 days of casual leave per calendar year specifically designated for urgent personal matters. To ensure that daily business operations experience minimal disruption, casual leave is strictly regulated and must be taken 1 day per request. Should an urgent situation occur unexpectedly, staff may request this time off on the same day, provided the request is submitted before 10:00. 

When personnel require extended time away beyond standard allocations, unpaid leave of up to 30 working days per year may be granted, keeping in mind that this category has no balance. To ensure proper operational planning, unpaid leave should be requested at least 30 calendar days in advance, except in genuine emergencies where prior notice is impossible. 

For continuous learning and career advancement, staff members sitting an approved professional exam may take up to 5 days of study leave per year. It is important to note that these study days are deducted from annual leave balances. All time-off submissions must follow the established reporting channels to maintain accurate attendance records.

## 4. Responsibilities
Managing time away from work is a shared duty between staff members, direct supervisors, department leadership, and Human Resources. Employees are responsible for submitting their time-off requests accurately and within the stipulated timeframes, ensuring that urgent matters or advance notice requirements are respected. Supervisors play a critical role in reviewing these submissions promptly. Generally, leave requests are approved by the employee's direct manager; however, requests originating from department heads are approved by the Head of Human Resources to avoid any conflict of interest. Human Resources oversees the entire tracking process, ensuring that leave balances, such as the 6 days of casual leave and the 5 days of study leave, are correctly monitored and deducted from annual leave where applicable. Department heads must also manage team coverage effectively when staff members utilize their allotted time off.

## 5. Exceptions
While the rules governing time off are designed to cover most scenarios, certain edge cases and extraordinary situations require specific handling. For instance, the standard requirement to submit unpaid leave at least 30 calendar days in advance is waived in genuine emergencies, allowing staff to seek relief without administrative penalties when unforeseen crises strike. Similarly, same-day casual leave is permitted only if the application is logged before 10:00; requests submitted after this cutoff for urgent personal matters may require alternative arrangements or supervisory discretion based on operational urgency. Any request exceeding the 30 working days limit for unpaid leave cannot be approved under this policy, as no balance exists beyond that threshold. Any deviation from these established parameters requires explicit written authorization from Human Resources.

## 6. Related documents
To ensure comprehensive understanding and alignment with broader organizational standards, this policy should be read in conjunction with other internal guidelines. These include the primary employee handbook, the annual leave management guidelines, the professional development and training framework, and the attendance management directives. All internal documents work together to provide a complete picture of staff rights, benefits, and administrative duties within the organization.
