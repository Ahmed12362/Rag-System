# Taking Same-Day Casual Leave

## 1. Purpose
The Company provides a specific type of time off to help personnel manage urgent personal matters that arise unexpectedly. Maintaining work continuity while supporting individual needs is a key objective for the organization. Through this benefit, the Company ensures that team members can address immediate, unforeseen responsibilities without having to worry about complex administrative approval delays. This document outlines the procedure for securing this specific category of short-term leave on the exact day it is needed, detailing the operational requirements and boundaries established by the Company to maintain daily productivity and team collaboration.

## 2. Who this applies to
This procedure applies to all active personnel within the Company who require urgent time off for personal emergencies. Every eligible staff member is allocated a total of 6 days of casual leave per calendar year. This allotment is designed strictly for urgent personal matters that cannot be scheduled in advance outside of regular working hours. Department heads and supervisors must ensure that team members understand these parameters so that the benefit is used correctly and fairly across all operational units within the Company.

## 3. Steps
Securing this time off on short notice requires a straightforward, step-by-step approach to ensure proper record-keeping and team communication. 
First, the staff member must identify the urgent personal matter requiring immediate attention. 
Second, the employee must prepare the request, ensuring that the duration aligns with the rule that casual leave is taken 1 day per request. Multiple days cannot be combined into a single emergency submission under this specific same-day policy. 
Third, the employee must submit the formal request to their direct supervisor or the human resources department. 
Fourth, the supervisor reviews the submission and updates the internal attendance tracking systems to reflect the absence, ensuring that team coverage is temporarily adjusted to absorb any urgent workload.

## 4. Timelines
Timing is a critical factor when dealing with short-term emergency absences at the Company. Because life is unpredictable, casual leave may be requested on the same day the time off is needed. However, to ensure that operations are not disrupted and that colleagues can cover pressing tasks, a strict temporal boundary applies. Specifically, the request is submitted before 10:00 on the day the leave is requested. Submissions made after this deadline cannot be processed under this same-day provision, and employees must look to alternative leave types or consult human resources for guidance on handling late-arriving emergencies.

## 5. Contacts
For any questions, clarifications, or assistance regarding the submission process, personnel should reach out to the appropriate internal support channels. The human resources department at the Company is fully equipped to guide staff through the policy details, help resolve submission timing dilemmas, and address any edge cases concerning yearly allocations. Employees can contact their designated human resources representative directly via the internal communication directory or speak with their immediate supervisor during regular working hours for immediate operational support.
