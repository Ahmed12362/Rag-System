# Casual and Unpaid Leave FAQ - Part 2

## 1. What is the maximum duration for unpaid leave that can be requested each year?
Team members looking to take extended time away from their duties can apply for unpaid leave of up to 30 working days per year. The Company ensures that this option remains available for significant personal needs, provided that proper authorization is secured. Employees must coordinate closely with their direct manager and Human Resources to obtain the necessary approvals before stepping away from their responsibilities. This provision offers flexibility for various life circumstances, allowing staff to manage prolonged absences while maintaining open communication channels with leadership and administrative teams.

## 2. Does unpaid leave accrue a specific balance within the system?
Unlike other forms of time off that accumulate over time, unpaid leave has no balance. Because there is no pre-allocated pool of days for this category, every single instance is evaluated independently on a case-by-case basis. Management and Human Resources review each submission individually to assess operational impact and ensure business continuity remains uninterrupted. Staff should keep this lack of a standing balance in mind when planning their schedules, recognizing that approvals are discretionary and tied directly to current departmental needs and workload requirements.

## 3. How far in advance should employees submit their requests for unpaid leave?
Planning ahead is crucial for maintaining seamless operations across all departments. Therefore, unpaid leave should be requested at least 30 calendar days in advance. Submitting the paperwork well before the planned departure gives the direct manager and Human Resources adequate time to review the application, evaluate team coverage, and make necessary workflow adjustments. Adhering to this timeline prevents last-minute staffing shortages and demonstrates professionalism, ensuring that the absence causes minimal disruption to ongoing projects and daily business functions.

## 4. Are there any exceptions to the advance notice requirement for unpaid leave?
While advance planning is always expected, the Company recognizes that unforeseen circumstances and urgent situations can arise suddenly. Consequently, the rule requiring advance notice is waived in emergencies. When unexpected crises occur, staff members are still expected to inform their leadership and Human Resources as promptly as possible under the circumstances. The direct manager and administrative teams will then handle these urgent requests with appropriate flexibility, balancing the immediate personal needs of the individual with the operational realities of the workplace.

## 5. Can staff take time off to prepare for professional examinations?
Professional development and continuous learning are strongly supported by the organization. Employees sitting an approved professional exam may take up to 5 days of study leave per year. This benefit is specifically designed to help staff adequately prepare for career-advancing certifications and educational milestones. To utilize this option, the examination must be officially recognized and approved by the organization, ensuring that the time taken directly contributes to both the individual's professional growth and the collective expertise within the workplace.

## 6. How is study leave accounted for within the employee's time-off records?
When staff members utilize the days allocated for professional exam preparation, the time is not treated as an independent category. Instead, study leave is deducted from annual leave. This means that individuals taking time to prepare for their exams must plan their schedules carefully, keeping in mind that these days will reduce their overall yearly vacation balance. Team members should discuss this deduction with their direct manager during the planning phase to ensure that both professional preparation and personal rest periods are properly balanced throughout the year.
