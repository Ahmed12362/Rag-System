# New Joiner FAQ - Part 2

## 1. What happens on my first day regarding medical coverage?
From the very beginning of your employment with the Company, you and your registered dependants are fully covered by medical insurance. This immediate protection ensures peace of mind for you and your family right from day one, allowing you to access healthcare services without any waiting period. Whether it is a routine checkup or an unexpected medical situation, your policy is active immediately. You do not need to wait for onboarding completion or payroll activation to benefit from this healthcare provision.

## 2. Who will help me settle into my new role?
To ensure a smooth transition, every new joiner is assigned a buddy from their team for the probation period. Your buddy is there to guide you through daily routines, introduce you to colleagues, and answer any informal questions you might have about the workplace. This dedicated support system helps you integrate seamlessly into the team culture and understand departmental expectations quickly. Your buddy will check in regularly during your initial months to help you overcome any hurdles and ensure you feel welcome.

## 3. What paperwork do I need to submit after joining?
New joiners must submit their national ID copy, academic certificates and bank details within 10 working days of joining. Timely submission of these documents is crucial for setting up your employee profile, processing your salary correctly, and verifying your qualifications. Human Resources relies on these records to maintain compliance with internal policies and regulatory standards. Please ensure you gather these items promptly to avoid any administrative delays in your onboarding process.

## 4. What is the purpose of the buddy system during probation?
The buddy system is specifically designed to support new employees throughout their probation period by providing a reliable point of contact within the team. Your assigned peer can share practical tips, clarify workflow procedures, and help you navigate internal systems more efficiently. This relationship fosters open communication and accelerates your learning curve in the new environment. It also provides a friendly face who can offer guidance on both professional tasks and cultural norms within the organization.

## 5. When does the medical insurance coverage begin for my family?
Medical insurance coverage for employees and their registered dependants starts on the first day of employment. There are no exclusions or delayed start dates for your family members, provided they are officially registered under your policy. This comprehensive benefit guarantees that your household is protected against medical expenses right from the moment you officially start your journey. If you need to add dependants, make sure their details are provided during the initial setup to activate their coverage simultaneously with yours.

## 6. Are there any penalties if I miss the document submission deadline?
Submitting your national ID copy, academic certificates and bank details within 10 working days of joining is a mandatory requirement for all new personnel. Missing this deadline can cause significant processing delays for your monthly salary payments and personnel file activation. It is essential to prioritize gathering these records immediately upon starting your employment. If you anticipate any difficulty in obtaining specific documents within this timeframe, you should notify Human Resources right away to discuss alternative arrangements.
