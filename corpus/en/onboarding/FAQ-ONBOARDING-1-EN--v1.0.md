# New Joiner FAQ - Part 1

## 1. What happens during my initial period at the Company?
When you start working at the Company, you enter a structured evaluation phase. New employees serve a probation period of 3 months. This timeframe allows both you and your managers to assess role fit, understand team dynamics, and ensure performance expectations align. During this evaluation, supervisors provide continuous feedback, and HR checks in regularly to support your transition. If unexpected absences occur, standard policy applies, though the duration of this initial evaluation remains fixed at 3 months.

## 2. How do I get up to speed during my first week?
Starting a new job involves absorbing a lot of information about culture, policies, and internal processes. To help you integrate smoothly, new joiners complete the orientation programme within their first 5 working days. This introductory schedule includes sessions on company values, compliance standards, and team introductions. Managers schedule these activities carefully so you can balance learning with early tasks, ensuring you finish all required modules within your first 5 working days.

## 3. Will my equipment and workspace be ready when I arrive?
Preparation is a priority for the Company, ensuring you can start working immediately without administrative delays. Laptop, badge and system accounts are ready on the employee's first day. IT support and facilities teams coordinate behind the scenes so that your hardware, building access pass, and digital credentials are fully active. Should any minor login issues arise, IT is on standby to resolve them promptly, guaranteeing that your laptop, badge and system accounts are ready on the employee's first day.

## 4. Who should I contact if I have questions about my onboarding schedule?
Navigating your initial schedule can occasionally raise questions regarding overlapping meetings or mandatory training sessions. Your primary point of contact for any onboarding inquiries is your assigned buddy or direct manager. They can help you prioritize orientation tasks, adjust your calendar, and guide you through any unclear procedures while you settle into your new role.

## 5. Are there any specific documents I need to submit during my initial days?
Submitting necessary paperwork is a vital part of finalizing your employment records with the Company. You will be asked to provide various identification documents, tax forms, and banking details to HR during your first week. Prompt submission prevents payroll delays and ensures compliance with internal administrative policies. HR provides a clear checklist to guide you through each required document submission.

## 6. How does the Company support my continuous learning after orientation?
Beyond the initial introduction, the Company encourages ongoing professional development and skill enhancement. Once you finish your primary onboarding steps, you gain access to internal learning platforms and specialized training catalogues. Managers work with you to identify suitable courses that align with your career goals, helping you grow professionally while contributing effectively to team objectives.
