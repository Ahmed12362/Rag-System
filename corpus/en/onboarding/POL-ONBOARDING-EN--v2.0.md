# Onboarding Policy

## 1. Purpose
The introduction of new personnel into the corporate environment requires a structured and seamless integration framework. This policy establishes the standardized procedures governing how the Company welcomes, equips, and incorporates incoming staff members into daily operations. By setting clear operational expectations from the very beginning, the organization ensures that every newcomer transitions smoothly into their role, understands their administrative duties, and feels fully supported during their initial integration phase. A well-designed reception process sets the foundation for long-term engagement, productivity, and adherence to organizational standards across all departments.

## 2. Scope
This governance framework applies universally to all newly hired personnel across every department and level within the organization. Whether an individual joins in a permanent, temporary, or specialized capacity, the provisions outlined herein dictate the mandatory steps required for a successful transition. Departmental managers, human resources representatives, and incoming staff must all adhere to these established guidelines to maintain consistency and fairness throughout the enterprise.

## 3. Policy
The integration journey is governed by a set of mandatory milestones designed to protect both the worker and the organization while fostering a supportive working environment. 

To ensure complete administrative compliance, all incoming personnel are required to submit their national ID copy, academic certificates, and bank details within 10 working days of joining. This submission allows payroll and compliance teams to verify credentials and set up financial remuneration without delay.

From the very start of their employment, individuals and their registered dependants are covered by medical insurance from the first day of employment, ensuring that healthcare needs are met immediately upon commencement. 

Operational readiness is prioritized so that critical tools are provided without delay. Specifically, a laptop, badge, and system accounts are ready on the employee's first day, allowing uninterrupted access to digital workspaces, physical premises, and communication channels.

To facilitate professional acclimatization, every new joiner is assigned a buddy from their team for the probation period. This peer mentor acts as an informal guide, assisting with day-to-day queries, team culture, and workflow acclimation.

During this initial phase of employment, newcomers serve a probation period of 3 months. This duration allows both management and the individual to evaluate mutual fit, performance standards, and role suitability.

Finally, to build foundational knowledge regarding organizational policies, culture, and operational workflows, new joiners complete the orientation programme within their first 5 working days. This curriculum provides essential briefings on safety, compliance, and departmental objectives.

## 4. Responsibilities
Managing a successful integration requires active collaboration among multiple stakeholders across the organization. Human resources personnel must coordinate administrative workflows, verify submitted credentials, and oversee the timely issuance of medical coverage and technological tools. Departmental leadership is responsible for selecting appropriate peer mentors, monitoring initial performance, and ensuring that operational readiness is maintained. The designated buddy must actively support their assigned colleague, offering guidance and answering operational questions throughout the initial months of employment. Finally, incoming personnel bear the direct responsibility of attending all required orientation sessions, submitting necessary personal and financial documentation within the designated deadlines, and actively engaging with their mentors to accelerate their professional integration.

## 5. Exceptions
While these guidelines are designed to cover all standard situations, exceptional circumstances may occasionally arise. Any deviation from the stipulated timelines, equipment provisions, or administrative submissions requires formal written authorization from senior management. Such waivers are evaluated on a case-by-case basis to ensure business continuity without compromising compliance or security standards.

## 6. Related documents
To ensure comprehensive understanding and adherence, this policy should be read in conjunction with other internal governance frameworks. Relevant supplementary materials include the employee handbook, administrative guidelines, medical insurance benefit schedules, and internal compliance manuals. All staff members are encouraged to review these complementary resources to gain a holistic view of workplace expectations and benefits.
