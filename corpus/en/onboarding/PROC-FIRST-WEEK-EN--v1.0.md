# New Joiner First-Week Checklist

## 1. Purpose
The primary objective of this procedural guide is to ensure that every newcomer smoothly transitions into their professional role within the Company. By establishing a structured onboarding framework, the organisation guarantees that all administrative, technical, and social integration requirements are met efficiently right from the start of employment. This document outlines the essential milestones and responsibilities required to set new employees up for long-term success and productivity in their daily tasks.

## 2. Who this applies to
This onboarding procedure is mandatory for all newly hired personnel joining the Company across all departments and business units. It also directly involves internal stakeholders such as human resources coordinators, IT support personnel, departmental managers, and assigned peer guides. Every individual joining the organisation, regardless of their seniority level, must follow these established steps to ensure complete compliance with internal operational standards.

## 3. Steps
To guarantee a seamless integration into the organisation, new personnel and relevant internal teams must execute the following structured process:
1. Preparation of technical and physical access: The Company ensures that the assigned laptop, building entry badge, and all essential system accounts are fully provisioned and ready on the employee's first day.
2. Peer mentorship assignment: Department leadership pairs every newcomer with a dedicated buddy from their immediate team who will provide support, answer questions, and assist throughout the probation period.
3. Orientation completion: The newcomer must actively participate in and finish the comprehensive orientation programme.
4. Document submission: The employee is required to officially provide their national ID copy, academic certificates, and personal bank details.

## 4. Timelines
Managing deadlines effectively is crucial for a successful transition. The Company mandates that the technical equipment and system access points are available immediately on the initial working day. Furthermore, the newcomer must finish the entire orientation programme within their first 5 working days. Regarding administrative compliance, all required paperwork—specifically the national ID copy, academic certificates, and bank details—must be submitted by the employee within 10 working days of joining. Adhering strictly to these timeframes prevents any administrative delays or payroll processing issues.

## 5. Contacts
For any inquiries, assistance, or clarification regarding the onboarding process, employees should reach out to the designated internal support channels within the Company. Departmental managers can assist with daily duties and team-related questions, while the human resources department handles document collection and administrative queries. Additionally, the assigned peer buddy remains the primary point of contact for day-to-day guidance during the initial phase of employment.
