# Employee Handbook: Office Facilities and Meeting Rooms

## 1. Overview
Welcome to your guide on making the most of our shared workplace! The Company strives to provide a comfortable, productive, and secure environment for everyone. Our office facilities and meeting spaces are designed to support your daily collaboration and focus. Understanding how these spaces and security items work helps ensure a smooth experience for all colleagues. Whether you are setting up a collaborative session or keeping track of your daily access credentials, a few straightforward guidelines keep everything running seamlessly.

## 2. Details
Our workplace features various resources that rely on shared cooperation. For scheduling collaborative spaces, the Company uses a shared room calendar system. This digital calendar helps coordinate schedules across teams. However, keeping schedules accurate requires active participation. Specifically, bookings not used within 10 minutes of the start are released automatically, making the room available for others who might need it. Regarding physical access, your employee badge is your key to the building and internal areas. If a security item goes missing, quick action is essential to maintain workplace safety. A lost badge must be reported to Administration within 24 hours so it can be deactivated promptly.

## 3. What employees should do
To maintain an organized and efficient office, please follow these practical steps. When planning a get-together, reserve your space in advance using the shared room calendar. Be sure to arrive promptly for your scheduled session. If your plans change or attendees run late, remember that bookings not used within 10 minutes of the start are released, so timeliness is crucial to avoid losing your reservation. For your daily security, keep your access badge close at hand. In the unfortunate event that your credential goes missing, take immediate action: a lost badge must be reported to Administration within 24 hours so it can be deactivated before any unauthorized access can occur. Following these habits protects our workplace community and ensures fair access for all staff members.

## 4. Related policies
The guidelines outlined in this chapter connect closely with broader internal standards maintained by the Company. Facilities management guidelines govern the proper use of shared hardware, common areas, and booking systems. Additionally, security and compliance protocols dictate how physical access credentials, visitor logs, and building safety are handled. By aligning your daily office habits with these related policies, you help the Company maintain a secure, welcoming, and well-managed professional environment for every team member.
