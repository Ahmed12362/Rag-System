# Reporting a Lost Badge

## 1. Purpose
The primary objective of this internal directive is to establish a clear and secure protocol for all personnel when an identification pass goes missing within the organization. Ensuring building security and maintaining accurate attendance records are vital responsibilities for every worker. When an identification card is misplaced, compromised, or stolen, immediate action is required to prevent unauthorized access to corporate premises and sensitive internal assets. The Company relies on strict security measures to protect its physical spaces, proprietary information, and technological infrastructure from potential breaches. By outlining a standardized process, this document ensures that all missing credential incidents are handled swiftly, systematically, and uniformly across all departments. Furthermore, establishing these guidelines helps maintain operational continuity while safeguarding the overall work environment. Adherence to this directive minimizes security vulnerabilities and ensures that building management can immediately disable compromised passes to protect everyone on the premises.

## 2. Who this applies to
This procedure is mandatory for all active personnel associated with the Company, regardless of their employment status, department, or hierarchical level. This includes full-time employees, part-time staff, external contractors, temporary workers, and long-term consultants who have been issued an official corporate identification card. Every individual who enters corporate facilities or interacts with internal systems using an assigned pass is bound by these regulations. Department supervisors and team leaders also share the responsibility of guiding their subordinates through this protocol if an incident occurs during working hours. Even remote staff members who visit the main office periodically must follow these exact security measures whenever they misplace their physical credentials. Understanding and complying with these obligations is a fundamental condition of employment and site access, ensuring that accountability is maintained throughout the entire workforce.

## 3. Steps
When an identification card is discovered missing, the individual must immediately initiate the reporting process to secure the premises. First, conduct a thorough search of your immediate workspace, personal belongings, and any recent locations visited within the building to confirm that the item is genuinely lost rather than temporarily misplaced. If the pass cannot be located after a quick search, you must immediately notify the Administration department. Attendance is recorded by the office badge reader or, on approved remote days, by checking in through the HR mobile app. If your physical credential is missing, your daily office entry must be verified manually at the security desk until a replacement is issued. Administration will deactivate the missing pass the moment they receive your notification, rendering it completely useless for entering turnstiles, parking garages, or restricted internal zones. Following deactivation, you must submit a formal replacement request form detailing the circumstances of the loss. Security personnel will then review the request, verify your employment status, and schedule the creation of a new identification pass, ensuring your daily routine experiences minimal disruption while maintaining top-tier security standards across the organization.

## 4. Timelines
Time management is critical when dealing with security vulnerabilities, and specific deadlines govern this entire procedure to protect the organization effectively. A lost badge must be reported to Administration within 24 hours so it can be deactivated. Waiting longer than this designated timeframe significantly increases the risk of unauthorized entry and potential security breaches within corporate facilities. Once the report is officially logged, the deactivation process occurs almost instantaneously to neutralize any risk associated with the missing credential. Replacement passes are typically processed and printed during standard operational hours following the submission of the replacement form. Personnel must adhere strictly to these timeframes to ensure that building security remains uncompromised and that attendance tracking through the badge reader or mobile application continues smoothly without administrative delays.

## 5. Contacts
For assistance with reporting a missing identification pass or to ask questions regarding building security, personnel should reach out to the designated corporate departments. The Administration team serves as the primary point of contact for logging the incident, disabling compromised credentials, and initiating the replacement process. Additionally, the HR department is available to address questions concerning attendance verification, especially regarding check-in procedures via the mobile application on remote workdays. Security desk personnel can also provide immediate on-site support if you arrive at the office without your pass and require manual entry assistance. Keeping these communication channels open ensures that any security concern is resolved promptly and professionally, maintaining a safe and organized environment for everyone at the Company.
