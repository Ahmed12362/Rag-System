# Public Holidays and Working Calendar Policy

## 1. Purpose
The purpose of this document is to establish a clear and standardized framework governing the scheduling of work and official observances across the organization. By defining the standard operational rhythm, the Company ensures consistency in business operations, employee scheduling, and talent management. This policy provides transparency regarding how time away from work is structured and accounted for, ensuring that all personnel understand their entitlements and scheduling obligations. Furthermore, it aligns operational planning with official national observances, allowing the Company to maintain productivity while respecting cultural and statutory celebrations. The guidelines outlined herein serve to eliminate ambiguity surrounding calendar management, supporting a fair and predictable work environment for all teams.

## 2. Scope
This policy applies universally to all employees, contractors, and contingent workers engaged by the Company, regardless of their department, seniority, or location within the organization. Every operational unit, managerial tier, and individual contributor must adhere to the scheduling parameters and observance guidelines set forth in this document. Whether staff members operate remotely, in hybrid setups, or within physical corporate facilities, the principles governing the weekly operational cycle and official time off remain fully applicable. Management personnel at all levels are required to enforce these provisions consistently across their respective teams to maintain organizational alignment and equitable treatment.

## 3. Policy
To maintain a steady and predictable operational flow, the working week within the Company runs from Sunday to Thursday, establishing a standard 5-day professional cycle. Conversely, Friday and Saturday are designated as the official weekend, during which regular business operations are paused and personnel are not expected to perform routine duties. To accommodate cultural, historical, and religious observances, the calendar incorporates specific dates of recognition. Fixed official holidays observed by the Company are 7 January, 25 January, 25 April, 1 May, 30 June, 23 July and 6 October. In addition to these fixed dates, movable observances such as Islamic holidays and Sham el-Nessim follow the official annual announcement issued by the relevant authorities. When managing time off, leave is counted in working days, specifically spanning Sunday to Thursday. To protect employee rest entitlements and ensure fairness, Friday, Saturday and official public holidays are not deducted from any leave balance when individuals take authorized time away from their duties.

## 4. Responsibilities
Execution and compliance regarding this calendar framework are shared among employees, line managers, and human resources personnel. Individual staff members are responsible for planning their schedules, submitting leave requests in alignment with the defined working days, and accounting for official observances when committing to project timelines. Line managers must review and approve leave applications fairly, ensuring that departmental coverage remains adequate during operational periods while respecting the rule that non-working days and official holidays are excluded from leave deductions. The human resources department holds the primary duty of updating internal systems to reflect the annual schedule adjustments—particularly for movable dates such as Islamic holidays and Sham el-Nessim—and providing guidance on complex scheduling scenarios to maintain organizational compliance.

## 5. Exceptions
While the standard calendar and operational schedule apply broadly across the organization, certain operational demands may require deviations. Departments engaged in continuous operations, essential client support, or critical infrastructure management may need personnel to perform duties on days designated as the weekend or during official observances. In such edge cases, management must coordinate alternative arrangements, ensuring that any required coverage aligns with overarching labor standards and internal agreements. Any localized operational exceptions must receive prior authorization from department heads and human resources to ensure proper tracking and compliance with the core principles of leave calculation and rest entitlements.

## 6. Related documents
To ensure comprehensive understanding and operational alignment, this policy should be read in conjunction with other internal governance instruments maintained by the Company. Relevant materials include the comprehensive employee handbook, internal guidelines on attendance and punctuality, standard operating procedures for leave management, and any applicable departmental scheduling protocols. These complementary resources provide further detail on administrative procedures, reporting mechanisms, and behavioral expectations, ensuring that all personnel can easily navigate organizational standards alongside the directives established in this calendar policy.
