# Employee Handbook: Official Holidays

## 1. Overview
Welcome to the dedicated guide on official holidays at the Company. We understand how important it is for all team members to balance their dedicated work efforts with well-deserved rest, celebration, and time spent with family and friends. This handbook chapter is designed to provide you with a comprehensive understanding of how public holidays are structured and observed throughout the calendar year. 

At the Company, we believe that transparency regarding time off helps everyone plan their personal schedules effectively while maintaining seamless business operations. Whether you are working on a collaborative team project or managing independent deliverables, knowing your schedule in advance ensures that our workplace remains productive and supportive. This section serves as your friendly introduction to our holiday calendar, outlining the balance between fixed dates that remain constant every year and variable celebrations that shift according to official national announcements.

## 2. Details
To help you plan your personal time effectively, it is helpful to understand how our calendar is divided between fixed dates and floating celebrations. The fixed official holidays recognized by the Company are 7 January, 25 January, 25 April, 1 May, 30 June, 23 July and 6 October. These specific dates occur annually and provide predictable markers for your planning throughout the year. 

In addition to these fixed dates, our holiday schedule includes important cultural and religious observances. Specifically, Islamic holidays and Sham el-Nessim follow the official annual announcement. Because lunar calendars and traditional observances dictate the exact timing of these events, the exact dates for Islamic holidays and Sham el-Nessim vary each year. Management closely monitors government decrees and official announcements to confirm these dates as soon as they are publicly released, ensuring that all staff members are informed well in advance of any upcoming closures or adjusted working arrangements.

## 3. What employees should do
As a proactive member of the Company, staying informed and prepared is key to managing your holiday schedule successfully. Whenever an upcoming holiday approaches, you are encouraged to review your ongoing tasks and communicate proactively with your team lead or department manager. If your specific role requires coverage during certain public holidays due to operational demands, make sure to discuss scheduling arrangements well ahead of time.

Furthermore, employees should keep an eye on official communications and company-wide emails regarding Islamic holidays and Sham el-Nessim, as these follow the official annual announcement. Once these dates are officially announced by the authorities, update your personal calendars and inform any external clients or stakeholders who might be affected by your absence. Proper planning, timely communication, and coordination with your colleagues ensure that our workflow remains uninterrupted while everyone enjoys their rightful time off.

## 4. Related policies
Understanding our official holiday structure naturally connects to several other guidelines within the Company. To ensure a holistic view of your time away from the desk, please review our separate chapters covering annual leave, general attendance, and flexible working arrangements. These complementary policies work hand in hand with our holiday schedule to support your overall well-being and professional success. 

By familiarizing yourself with how public holidays interact with your regular paid time off, you can maximize your rest periods and maintain compliance with internal guidelines. If you ever have any questions about how a particular holiday impacts your specific contract or department guidelines, do not hesitate to reach out to your human resources representative for clarification.
